# Recorded effects and independently verified outcomes — before repair

FDD-006 identifies a presentation defect: the Work canvas counts every recorded BusinessEffect as an observed business effect. A prepared intent, authorized action, executing attempt or reconciliation-required outcome does not establish completion. This contract precedes the repair on commit 2c4eaf8228f16bed1b8d26f87a1ad1cec0b56d93.

Failure model: total row count is called observed; an arbitrary status substring implies verification; verification alone overrides an executing/unknown owner status; a verified status without a complete verification witness or observation is called complete; partial verification is called verified; prepared intent and possible submission are conflated; a failed outcome vanishes; observation and verifier basis disappear; an empty population invents an effect. The browser must remain a presentation of current canonical owner records, with no new authority, persistence or completion inference.

Independent presentation oracle: seven owner-shaped rows, in this order: prepared/not_started, executing/not_started, reconciliation_required/reconciliation_required, verified/verified with observation+basis+checkedAt, verified/partially_verified, verified/missing verification, failed/unverified. Exactly seven recorded effects and exactly one independently verified outcome. Every row retains its recorded status, verification state or UNKNOWN, and observation evidence or its explicit absence. Prepared intent is explicitly distinguished from execution; executing and reconciliation-required rows retain outcome UNKNOWN and reconciliation guidance. Partial, failed and missing verification never receive a verified icon. A verified witness requires matching owner status and verification state, nonempty basis, a valid checkedAt, and an actual observed result. Empty population produces no per-effect rows and reports zero verified outcomes.

The isolated challenge exercises the real composer and strict CanvasDocument schema before production repair, saves all inputs/results/source pins before asserting, and must fail on the old false observed count. It is a presentation boundary regression, not physical owner completion. The final authenticated mounted installation must additionally show the same owner-derived labels and state distinctions; existing actual owner/reconciliation fixtures supply those records. No fabricated row establishes a real business effect.
