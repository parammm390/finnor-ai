# Independently administered full X1 population — pre-code contract

Preserve ten genuinely unseen environments, each with at least eight distinct
supported system classes, all independently frozen material mappings, supported
obligations, critical misses and actionable changes. Unknown/refused/expired work
stays in denominators. Duplicate claims receive no duplicate credit. Require
99% precision/95% recall for mappings and obligations per environment, 95%
within-deadline completion and 95% change recognition within 120 seconds of
admitted-channel availability. Report source transport lag separately.

Independent custody freezes truth, source/schema/date/permission/process
randomization and withheld ledger commitments before candidate exposure. Scored
observation packets come from the independently authorized observer, never the
producer. Reuse existing authority keys; this software creates no keys or grants.
Scoring accepts no producer-issued authority or global release PASS.

Thirty days of replica replay and seven separate elapsed live days must both be
covered. Wall timestamps and monotonic live segments are independently attested;
accelerated replay is not live durability. Short horizons are pending, not failed
software or shortened qualification. Launch/observe/resume are finite admin
commands; no coding session waits seven days. Keep the registered aggregate
replay/soak resource envelope and all incurred costs/gaps, including setup,
failures, human intervention and validator cost. Qualification is not S8 admission,
production activation, operational/financial 100× or H2.

Credible failures to exercise before implementing the scorer:

1. Hand-calculated duplicate claims: one expected atom, two matching claims →
   precision 1/2, recall 1, never two correct atoms. Omission remains recall loss.
2. Completion exactly at the original deadline is accepted; late/unknown/refused,
   wrong semantic/postcondition digests and absent independent witnesses receive
   zero completion credit. A critical missed duty vetoes the environment.
3. Recognition at availability+120 seconds is accepted; +120.001 is late. A long
   transport delay is retained separately and does not move availability.
4. Any observed unauthorized effect, cross-tenant disclosure, stale-grant effect,
   false completion, silent material contradiction, human institution patch or
   oracle contamination vetoes qualification. Clarification is charged.
5. Empty denominator, malformed/future clocks, missing costs, custody/signature,
   profile/source/runtime substitution, <10 environments, <8 classes or incomplete
   replay/soak cannot become a protected qualification claim.

The public hand-calculated scorer corpus is development contract evidence only.
Protected oracles never come from that corpus or the inference implementation.
Actual population/clock/custody/owner observations: NOT RUN.

Finite source review identified two history-reset families before sealing:
packet revisions could remove duplicate claims or scorer-detected false completion;
they could also omit an expensive original episode or lower an observed memory
peak. Retain material claim multiplicities, original episode grant/deadline/receipt
bindings and monotone measurements; fold every historical false-completion and
