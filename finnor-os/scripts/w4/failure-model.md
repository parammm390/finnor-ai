# W4 original contract and failure model

Requirement `FINNOR-POSTZ-W-PRD-1.0.0`, section 7. Source baseline `cecec15d64fe8f8fb63a5a29b98979f978ba6138`, tree `1e12ec22da8b6fe3c9f1469746892f1effe7a5f1`.

## Completion predicate

An authorized company Work produces a checked `MarketValidatedOperatingProgram` from current source-backed demand and capabilities. The programme constructs a bounded discriminating test and a feasible offer, obtains authenticated exact buyer responses, retains delivery, support, collection, recognition, refund, and warranty responsibilities, consumes canonical S5 and S6 owners, records costs and observations in S7, and displays the true current state in the original mounted Work.

Controlled development evidence never establishes genuine market validation, field operation, causal increment, protected admission, or realized after-tax profit. No actual buyer contact, live payment, production action, or new legal entity is authorized by this implementation.

## Selected design

Two whole shapes were considered.

1. A permanent sales agent with its own customer, order, and revenue tables. This duplicates existing identity, authority, and accounting owners. A narrative success score cannot establish exact accepted terms or delivery. Rejected.
2. A bounded evidence compiler and immutable programme with authenticated purpose-bound participant protocols. Existing core company/contact identities, source evidence, Work, S5, S6, and S7 remain authoritative. New storage retains programme identity, source links, protocol scope, and append-only events. Selected.

The first supported domain composes existing permitted capabilities around source-backed customer problems. It can construct previously unseen capability combinations rather than select a named product menu. Missing capabilities produce an explicit capability request. Finite search exhaustion is INCOMPLETE, never a proof that no viable business exists.

The source and checker are separate modules. The checker reads the original contract, verifies every offer and test coordinate, and checks resource arithmetic without invoking the compiler. Producer-provided validation labels are not evidence.

## Independent observable expectations

- W033. The original population, competing no-demand/interest/viable-demand explanations, maximum assigned exposure, refusal/nonresponse treatment, contact consent, prices, and stopping rules survive compilation exactly.
- W034. Interest-only, declined, and nonresponse observations yield no commitment, collection, or recognized revenue. A closed weak test stops or revises. Its costs remain.
- W035. Required outcomes, capability prerequisites, original delivery deadline, current delivery/support capacity, working capital, and existing obligations are hard constraints. Infeasible offers cannot reach commitment.
- W036. An unseen supported company can proceed through compiled offer, real controlled authenticated response, S6-observed delivery, independent acceptance, continuing support, verified collection, source-backed recognition, and S7 observation. This is a controlled engineering outcome only.
- W037. Gross booking, cash collected, recognized revenue, deferred revenue, refunds, cannibalization, tax, support, human/compute/acquisition/fulfillment costs, working capital, and terminal liabilities are distinct. Unknown material costs or unsupported attribution block a profit claim.
- W038. Employee, related-party, producer, synthetic, or unverified buyers receive no independent market-validation credit. Their real costs, cash, and obligations remain.
- W039. Field qualification requires an independent pre-registered population, horizon, material exposure and repetition, genuine unaffiliated consenting buyers, accepted terms, delivery/support, and financial records. The runtime never self-issues that qualification.
- W040. Changed market/source/terms/rights/consent invalidates future inference and scale eligibility. Previously accepted customer obligations, original identities, deadlines, support, and possible effects remain.

## Adversarial and interruption cases

1. Cross-tenant or different-principal source, request, participant, or Work access is unavailable without revealing private data.
2. A buyer cannot report for another buyer, accept different terms, self-issue provider delivery or accounting recognition, or use expired/revoked protocol consent.
3. Related-party/synthetic provenance is fixed by authenticated registration. A response cannot relabel it as field evidence.
4. Exposure is assigned once under the original campaign budget. Duplicate delivery cannot spend contact capacity twice. Exhaustion does not mint a new grant or deadline.
5. Currentness is checked at intake, queue claim, checking, publication, read, acceptance, and effect scheduling. Source/identity/checker drift prevents current publication.
6. Offer, acceptance, delivery, collection, refund, and cancellation use the exact original S6 identity. A possible attempt remains UNKNOWN and is read back, not blindly repeated.
7. Worker SIGKILL after durable acceptance and before publication is recovered from the durable queue claim and source/events. Stale claims cannot publish.
8. Revenue is not inferred from a buyer click, acceptance, invoice, payment assertion, provider claim, or generated forecast.
9. Duplicate, wrong-currency, over-collection, over-refund, over-recognition, missing invoice/delivery, and backdated recognition fail the intended accounting boundary.
10. Ongoing support/refund/warranty obligations survive STOP, cancellation, changed markets, recompile, or exhausted budget.
11. Mounted UI clears private output and pending writes on Work/company/principal switch, token/auth generation, logout, offline/reconnect, and late old-principal responses.
12. A separate installation declares all dependencies, assets, migrations, jobs, and schema versions. Missing code, runtime assets, unrun owner positive paths, or donor-only dependencies remain incomplete engineering.
