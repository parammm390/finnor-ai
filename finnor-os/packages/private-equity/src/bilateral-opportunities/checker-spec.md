# Independent bilateral opportunity checker, version 1

This specification implements the six conjuncts frozen in
`scope-v/v1-private-market-opportunity-creation/failure-model.md`.
An ACCEPT is a development predicate result, not legal authority, protected
admission, seller propensity, underwriting, funding, or economic attribution.

The checker uses a separate read-only SQL role and an independently issued
original-record credential. It reads the accepted programme, current owner
records, original frozen pipeline, canonical identities, exact action and terms,
and provider-captured messages and participant events. The candidate supplies
identifiers only. It cannot supply a successful result or the checker's key.

1. Establish company identity and the respondent's OWNER or exact authorized
   representation role from the independent signed registry. Bind the canonical
   company/person and independently authenticated channel to those records.
2. Require the exact frozen universe and complete authorized pipeline coverage,
   with no active company/buyer entry in either the independent frozen census or
   canonical frozen census. Never count an unresolved origin as created.
3. Require current accepted activity/actor authority, the existing REQUIRED
   approval floor satisfied by an exact standing authorization, current rights
   and input dependencies, and an independently observed physical message with
   exact sender, recipient, disclosure, terms, Work and effect identity.
4. Require a directly authenticated, fresh positive INTEREST event for this
   buyer, action, terms digest and revision, explicitly considering a transaction
   and agreeing a permitted timed next step. A delivery, click, acknowledgment,
   generic networking statement, forwarded statement, or silence is insufficient.
5. Independently recompute integer consideration, majority/retained/management
   shares and timing under both original bounds. Independently evaluate every
   required preliminary predicate using exact, fresh source fields. Unknown or
   contradicted critical predicates reject qualification.
6. Require NONBINDING terms, explicit exclusions and the unchanged, registered
   full-diligence gap assertions for this company. Management continuity is a
   proposed contractual condition, never guaranteed or already achieved.

Refusal, opt-out and withdrawal are terminal contact suppression across the
connected company/person/endpoint identity component, Works, agents and mailboxes.
No candidate-produced success flag, arithmetic implementation or reconciliation
result is imported. The public schema and signature serialization are shared
wire contracts only. Every receipt binds tenant, principal, Work/input, programme,
candidate, exact terms, source cut, owner references, current dependency digest,
checker identity/specification, timestamps, all six results and measured usage.
An expired, altered, substituted or replayed receipt cannot publish a new effect.
