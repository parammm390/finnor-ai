## Independent acceptance

The checker receives the original accepted mandate, independently enrolled
identity/activity/profile evidence, original authenticated issuer events and
professional determinations, current canonical S5 membership and obligations.
It derives the classification and quantities independently of the controller.
The controller cannot administer provider keys, professional keys, enrollment,
independent answers, S8 admission or observed bank truth.

Signatures establish origin and byte integrity. They do not establish the
signer's entitlement, legal interpretation, capacity, permission or receipt.
Each claim must bind the independently enrolled issuer's exact supported domain.
No professional or provider identity is inferred from a title or mailbox.

## Failure families

1. Wrong tenant, principal, Work input, programme root or legal entity.
2. Invented provider, inactive identity, unauthorized representative, wrong act,
   instrument, jurisdiction or compensation/activity route.
3. Forged, replayed, substituted, incomplete, future or expired issuer evidence;
   signed-looking documents and generic acknowledgments promoted to commitments.
4. Producer-supplied legal answers; unsigned/wrong-scope professional opinions;
   absent committee approval, discretionary condition or MAC interpretation.
5. Missing material fees, security, guarantees, cross-defaults, collateral,
   covenants, ownership changes, dependencies, expiry and withdrawal rules.
6. Combining indicative, conditional, effective, reserved, dispatched and
   received quantities; face value or principal treated as profit.
7. Alias/document/syndication double counting, shared facility over-allocation,
   stale capacity membership, unknown draw exposure dropped or released.
8. Implicit FX, valuation, calendar, rounding, rate, haircut, zero-fee or unlimited
   collateral assumptions; lossy monetary JSON/float translation.
9. Old approvals reused for changed terms, recipients, price, exposure,
   additional guarantees or a broader solicitation/activity.
10. Disallowed raw/version/chunk/search/citation/attachment disclosure,
    cross-principal access, stale private cache after rights loss or Work switch.
11. Response loss and process death after external acceptance; duplicate physical
    approaches, execution, reservation or draw; fresh identity on recovery.
12. Cancellation/expiry/withdrawal erasing accepted effects, costs, liabilities,
    late observations or continuing duties.
13. Incompatible fallback or re-search replenishing one programme's original
    budget, contact cap, exposure, deadline or professional authority.
14. Controller output checked against itself; negatives denied by an unrelated
    prerequisite; generic sent-mail or record-mutation readback called funding.
15. Unregistered jobs, incompatible mixed binaries, missing installed checker,
    migration divergence, generated clients or mounted readers serving stale
    semantics.
16. Blind events counted by retry/message fragment; post-outcome exclusions,
    unregistered readiness/scoring, omitted costs and dependent sample counts.

## Independent development oracle

The decisive externally administered input is a USD 120,000,000 requirement,
USD 80,000,000 authenticated conditional debt and USD 40,000,000 nonbinding
co-investment indication. Expected conditional amount is 80,000,000; indicative
equity is 40,000,000; received funding is zero; readiness is incomplete. The
conditional financing gap and present-eligible gap are separately disclosed.
An effective undrawn equity revision is still not received cash.

Professional test actors specify the operative classification and condition
interpretation before the candidate runs. The checker independently reconstructs
quantities, missing conditions, capacity membership and compatibility from the
original inputs. Python integer/Fraction arithmetic is the separate calculation
oracle. No controller classifier, normalization or expected-value imports.

An expired or withdrawn commitment retains historical commitment and funding
observations. Unknown funding retains its original effect and liability. Late
funding changes the observed-funding history only through the receiving profile's
independent predicate. Readiness cannot be weakened retrospectively.

## Test value bar

Each verification-plan case names its observable contract, credible defect,
strongest actual production boundary and the missing prior coverage. V4 does not
exist on this base, so prior M3 modelling and S5/S6 tests cannot establish these
commitment/legal/disclosure semantics. Existing owner evidence is retained and
reused only for unchanged contracts with an explicit compatibility basis.
No new unit test will be written after implementation. Necessary isolated checker
verification follows this failure model. E2E results retain inputs, commands,
steps, observed API/SQL/queue/worker/provider/mounted evidence and failures.

## Qualification limits

Connected disposable provider/professional systems establish their named test
domain only. They are not live legal authority, bank funds, protected independent
evaluation, S8 admission, B_R, V1/V2/V3 delivery or economic outcomes. Required
internal failures are unfinished engineering, not external blockers.
