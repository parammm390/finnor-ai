## Boundaries and failure modes

* Identity: ref-shaped JSON, altered module/compiler/graph, foreign principal,
  future knowledge cut, revoked rights, stale Work input or plan, and an
  unavailable M3 reader must not resolve as an issued candidate.
* Grounding: exact literal, entity, locator, period and source revision differ;
  old summaries, incomplete selected rooms, amendments, inaccessible appendices,
  ambiguous legal interpretation and proposed agreements cannot prove absence.
* Mechanics: decimal scaling, units, currency, dates, signed extrema, original
  omitted covenants, false bounds, cash timing, concentration, flow/stock/
  occupancy, terminal tail and existing responsibility must survive independent
  reconstruction. Neither checker reads transformed proposer rows.
* Worlds: preserve one S3 joint draw, support, mechanism, correlations, horizon,
  information history and S4 objective. Hidden world IDs, future observations,
  independent marginal recombination, transport and bootstrap counting refuse.
  Adverse outcomes do not refute probability, expectation, causal or ex-ante
  quality claims.
* Effects: independently observe exact account/entity/field/value/unit/currency/
  null/clear semantics. C_01 is not C_010 and 20 is not 200. An isolated fixture
  is not a live provider. Unknown outcomes require read-only reconciliation.
  Replaying a witness must not repeat even a fixture mutation.
* Validation: pressure from a proposer, forged checker identities, a different
  failure, unsupported hypotheses or native/reference disagreement cannot enter
  the independently validated witness set.
* Reduction: retain original proposal and exact candidate, claim, context,
  predicate, lawful joint world, source rights and original constraints.
  Removal producing a missing term, exception, timeout, shifted unit or different
  failure is UNRESOLVED, not a reproduced failure. Only completed deletion checks
  justify 1-minimality for those deletions.
* Result: applicable material witness wins over gaps. Fatal missing checking/
  identity or zero permitted evaluations is BLOCKED. Nonempty permitted checked
  search without a witness is NO_WITNESS_WITHIN_BUDGET, never SAFE or PASS.
* Ledger: every generation, duplicate, rejection, validation, reduction, retry,
  cancellation, timeout and crash has a durable charge or explicit unknown.
  Persist budget debits before execution. Restart never supplies a fresh parent
  budget. No result depends only on a surviving favorable in-memory trace.
* Durability: before freeze, after source read, before check, after check/before
  save, after save/before publish, and after publish/before queue acknowledgement
  are separate crash windows. Expired/foreign/fenced claims cannot publish.
  Idempotency binds exact content; different content conflicts.
* Privacy: ordinary SQL/RLS, principal isolation, immutable content digests,
  bounded JSON, uniform unavailable responses and current owner authorization
  precede every result/trace read. Do not persist credentials or raw claim tokens
  in exported artifacts. Same-context reads are a required positive control.
* Currentness: source/rights, P4 broad code/schema, M1 dependency closure,
  S2 observation availability, S3 kernel, S4 policy/utility, S5 outstanding
  resources and S6 unknown effects are not cached grants. Changes preserve
  historical witnesses but refuse current dependent use.
* Resources: bound decoding, targets, cells, source bytes, traces, trials,
  witnesses, reductions, deadlines and native child output. Kill and reap trusted
  reference children on cancellation/deadline. No arbitrary generated code.
  Scheduling capacity, sampled RSS and a local deadline do not prove protected
  funding or aggregate OS quotas.
* Owners: no dry S5 clear/consume/release, S4 handoff, S6 prepare/dispatch, S7 cost
  rewrite or S8 evaluator change. Disposable owner fixture setup is separate.
* Availability: feasible controls, harmless changes, surviving repairs and
  incumbent-optimal cases prevent reject-everything shortcuts.
* Evidence: source-moved runs, first failures and exclusions remain recorded.
  Browser, genuine M3 integration, sealed GateM4, matched provider comparisons,
  reconciled dollars, physical grants, deployment and H2 economics are independent
  gates, not inferred from development checks.
