single-entity/period exact arithmetic. This ledger is not reconciled billing,

- `finnor-os/scripts/p6/public-cases.json` freezes public development inputs.

  `evidence/native-11-overlay/independent-reference.json` retains that output.

  Core, residual and fresh executions have actual isolated invocation receipts.

  and module and retains paired fresh results and observed cost records. It is
  an exact-C diagnostic, not a qualified completed-baseline/frontier comparison.

  records actual `P6_EXECUTABLE_TRANSFER` for the new75 composition.
- `evidence/mounted-18-overlay/browser/selected-execution-observation.json`

  failed as a transfer test; the correct result alone does not prove reuse.
