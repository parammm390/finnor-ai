- F01 and F02 have identical positive and negative input groups despite
  different pipeline/racing-reader descriptions.

The original 256/256 outcome and 188/188 positive-slot result remain valid
public process observations on those slots. They do not pass the locked
fresh-family or protected-population requirement. Renaming, duplicating,
changing IDs or editing expected outcomes cannot repair that gap.

## Model guarantees are narrower than some labels

The locked PRD section 9.3 explicitly permits a clearly labeled SOUND SUBSET
instead of claiming a weakest/maximally permissive context. That is the
implemented contract. F25's maximality title does not establish a maximality
theorem, and no such theorem is claimed.

Nonblocking is existence of a finite completion or legal-quiescence path,
not inevitability against every infinite environment schedule or Z3's
recurring-progress contract. The original coupled product is explored;
small output context size is not a compact exploration theorem.

Correspondence covers the exact bounded listed kernels plus declared
original-owner bindings, not arbitrary TypeScript, SQL, providers or
institutional behavior. The source adapters are concrete, but their actual
native HTTP/RLS/concurrency/custody/queue paths remain unexecuted.

A separately governed structural campaign would need registered distinct
structures and independently derived expected original semantics BEFORE
exposure, eight fresh cases per family, all critical falsifiers and actual
native/recovery coverage where required. Builder-written public development
supplements cannot self-administer protected qualification. No such campaign
or fresh population is invented here.
