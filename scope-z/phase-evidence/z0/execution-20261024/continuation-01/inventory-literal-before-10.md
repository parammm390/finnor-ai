  of the three original Work query families, or a generic planner economic
  opcode instead of the native-only owner.

  reference array order, metadata inferred from an output instead of its owner.
- A human capability marked model-proposable, an unavailable capability marked
  executable, or an approval floor confused with actual authority.

  two-flag owner predicate and the driver's explicit environment.
