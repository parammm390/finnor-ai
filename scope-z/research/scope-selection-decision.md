## Explicit rejections

1. **Finite decision quotient/bisimulation:** R1 already requires exact information-state reduction and original-history lifting. A bigger quotient, different name or cheaper implementation is not a new outcome.
2. **Decision-focused observation stopping:** R4, X4 and Y2 already require decision-relevant acquisition/stopping.
3. **Ordinary probabilistic circuits:** Y explicitly treats them as methods, not a missing capability boundary.
4. **Guaranteed-output general MPC with n−1 active corruption:** do not replace Y4's explicit abort semantics by an unavailable general fairness guarantee or change the threat model.
5. **Certified revoked-influence removal:** lawful deletion/retraining of retained influence is already an X5/Y4 duty.
6. **Arbitrary code expressibility:** neither “Y5 could write it” nor “it uses a new library” settles a new product obligation. Equip comparators with the same lawful libraries and discovery/construction rights.

These rejections supersede the exploratory coordinator candidate numbering. In particular, the former C2 decision-quotient hypothesis is dropped. Z2 below is a different, explicitly defined incentive concept, not a relabelled quotient.

## Why Z2 is a concrete candidate, not a borrowed equilibrium slogan

Full Y §6.3, particularly source lines 394–398, requires exact sequential rationality for **unilateral** full contingent deviations and one globally consistent tremble-limit assessment. It explicitly disclaims coalition resistance. Full R5 coalition programme search and Y2 policy-conditioned actor response are credited; neither disclaimer is repaired by calling a centralized coalition optimizer an incentive proof.

Freeze the original finite perfect-recall model, protocol family, information rights, chance law, utility semantics, outside options, resources and allowed coalition communication. For a coalition C, derive **all** lawful pure joint contingent plans D_C from that original model. A plan is a tuple of local observation-history policies, not a policy with omniscient pooled information. Allowed shared randomization is part of the declared threat profile; it does not authorize FINNOR to pool private source data.

With outside participants following the prescribed protocol σ, let

$$
g_i(d)=E[u_i(d,\sigma_{-C})]-E[u_i(\sigma)].
$$

Z2's first concept excludes every distribution p over D_C satisfying

$$
p\ge0,\quad {\bf1}^{T}p=1,\quad \sum_d p_d g_i(d)\ge0\ (i\in C),
\quad \sum_{i\in C,d}p_dg_i(d)>0.
$$

This is **no weak-Pareto-profitable correlated joint deviation chosen ex ante, with full lawful contingent continuation**. It is not interim coalition-proof sequential equilibrium, arbitrary future coalition formation, the particularly strong k-resilience definition of Abraham et al., or MASE's average coalition-gain objective. Do not borrow any of those names as a stronger warranty.

The last sum is a feasibility device over per-member normalized utility gains, not a sum of dollars or an owner-value claim. Utility scaling and any real side payments remain fixed input meanings. If transfers are legal, their lawful plans and capacity are in D_C; excluding them silently is unsound.

For finite rational gains, the exact LP maximizes that sum with the first three constraints. A dual certificate can supply rational λ_i≥0 and ν≤0 such that, for every original d,

$$
\nu\ge\sum_{i\in C}(1+\lambda_i)g_i(d).
$$

An independently reconstructed plan family and these inequalities prove the declared absence. A profitable rational p is a counterexample. Check completeness of D_C independently; do not trust a producer's short list.

Pure joint plans alone are insufficient: gains (4,−2) and (−1,1) are not Pareto profitable individually, but mixing with probabilities 1/4 and 3/4 yields (1/4,1/4). A finite protocol randomization grid does not automatically bound the adversary's permitted strategy mixtures. Exact rational certificates avoid tolerances disguising positive regret.

Protocol construction must use these joint counterexamples to select a lawful protocol and preserve the full native Y3 certificate. Merely checking a hand-chosen static payoff table, deleting allowed deviations, altering utilities or attaching an unsupported “collusion-proof” label fails.
