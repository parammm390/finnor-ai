## Non-negotiable comparison

The design actor is FULL intended X + Y, not today's checkout. In particular,
Y5 already discovers uniform algorithms, X3 already writes complete applications,
X5 already compiles experience, and X1/P7 already maintain validity dependencies.
There is no honest claim that a proposed Z algorithm is unimplementable by arbitrary
baseline-generated code. The relevant separation is an independently qualified
runtime computational boundary and material accepted-work consequence against the
strongest equipped baseline. If the baseline constructs and runs the equivalent
capability comparably, the proposed increment is REUSED, not a breakthrough.

Z0 convergence must also be available to the comparison actor. Making old
capabilities reachable is not a post-X/Y invention and cannot inflate Z1–Z5 credit.

## Whole-shape alternatives

1. Further economic construction: larger games, richer counterparty models,
additional economic operators, private distributed solvers, broader theorem
search. Rejected as the programme shape: these are already covered by full Y.
2. Institutional computational substrate: change the mathematical units of
computation, state, time, trust, and composition, while leaving every business
owner, objective, authority and independently observed completion condition fixed.
This is the current candidate shape. Five independent transformations must still
survive source investigation and adversarial research before locking.

## Candidate transformations under challenge

### C1: collective work compilation

One output: `SharedWorkProgram`. The input is a set of distinct material Works
and admitted pure computational kernels, not a single episode. Compile
structural common computation into one shared program with separate assignment
bindings and acceptance witnesses. Runtime updates maintain the program's
semantics, rather than restarting general reasoning or independently evaluating
every assignment.

For specification vector $F=(f_1,\ldots,f_m)$ and evidence state $D$, require
$\operatorname{decode}(\operatorname{run}(C_F,D))=F(D)$, and for admitted updates
$\Delta D$ require exactly
$\operatorname{update}(C_F,D,\Delta D)=F(D+\Delta D)$.
Different Works retain different identity, deadlines, rights, costs and S6 effects.
Share only a pure subcomputation whose semantic/dependency/rights fingerprints
agree. A coalesced effect is prohibited.

DBSP supplies a principled delta transform $D\circ\uparrow F\circ I$; F-IVM
supplies factorized view trees and task-specific rings; self-adjusting computation
supplies change propagation coupled with memoization. These are substrates, not
an assertion of a new algorithm or a FINNOR gain.

Hard challenges: X1 already updates institutional state, P7 already computes
invalidation closure, and X5 already avoids repeated reasoning. A single-query
IVM port, cache, workflow fan-out or database installation must receive zero
new-boundary credit. The protected witness must show jointly compiled,
non-identical material assignments and exact per-Work consequences that do not
come from those existing paths. All cache/build/update/retention costs count.

### C2: decision-preserving state quotient compilation

One output: `DecisionQuotient`. Input is an admitted model and a fixed family
of objectives, observations, resource/obligation predicates and operators.
Find a smaller executable state representation with an independently checked
lifting/refinement relation. No new model, causal fact or preference is invented.

For exact partition $q:S\rightarrow\bar S$, require observation and hard-predicate
equality within each block; fixed action applicability; reward/quantity equality;
and for every permitted action $a$, model $M$ and block $B$,
$\sum_{s'\in B}P_M(s'|s,a)$ is identical for all concrete states in the source
block. The runtime can then lift a quotient policy only through lawful
observations. An interval/simulation abstraction requires conservative bounds,
not a weighted average declared exact.

Primary 2026 work on property-driven causal abstractions is directly relevant,
but explicitly requires expensive whole-model analysis/cause computation and does
not promise a universally efficient abstraction. A discovered predicate is not
a proved quotient. Uncertainty and financial effects must survive abstraction.

Hard challenges: this can be only a solver optimization, or an X5 abstraction,
unless the product constructs and checks counterfactual decision equivalence and
uses the smaller world under fresh objectives within its exact domain. Y5 may
already discover an equivalent general method. Require equipped comparison and
negative cases changing a hidden liability, observation right, or action meaning.

### C3: perpetual institutional viability synthesis

One output: `ViabilityStrategy`. Synthesize a winning policy for indefinitely
renewing obligations under an admitted environment, not a finite favorable rollout.
Safety is universal over supported plays; liveness is conditional on explicit,
independently justified environmental fairness and resource availability.

For finite turn-based safety games:
$W_0=\operatorname{Safe}$,
$W_{k+1}=\operatorname{Safe}\cap\operatorname{CPre}(W_k)$,
$W_\infty=\nu X.(\operatorname{Safe}\cap\operatorname{CPre}(X))$.
For controller moves CPre uses existential action choice and universal admitted
successors; for environment moves it uses universal choice. Generalized Büchi/
GR(1) objectives additionally need nested fixed points and a checkable progress
ranking. A safety-closed wait loop is not a liveness strategy.

Emerson–Lei games provide a wider theory, not a free unrestricted solver.
The read paper explicitly has PSPACE-complete general games and potentially
exponential strategy memory; its whole synthesis proposal remained future work.
The first product profile must have a complete tractable checker, not cite an
unimplemented paper as a receipt.

Hard challenges: S4 already runs standing programmes, R2 searches policies, and
Y3 synthesizes histories. The separation is an independently checked
infinite-play viability/liveness contract, not more dates or a renamed scheduler.
No liveness claim may assume that a real counterparty eventually cooperates
without an actual admitted basis. No strategy grants S6 authority.

### C4: proof-carrying computation compilation

One output: `ExecutionProofCapsule`. Compile an admitted deterministic exact
relation into a prover/verifier binding. An untrusted bounded executor produces
a proof about actual computational steps, consumed without trusting its host or
replaying the full step history. No claim of source truth, legal effect, authority,
or external completion follows from computational correctness.

For step relation $z_{t+1}=F_\theta(z_t,w_t)$, a receipt binds program/semantics
digest $\theta$, initial/final state commitments, exact step count, public outputs,
input revision/rights and checker identity. Verification asserts that the whole
sequence satisfies the relation. In Nova, folding combines two relation instances;
compressed recursive proofs make verification independent of history length under
the selected computational assumptions. Prover/encoding costs can dominate.

Read primary implementation: microsoft/Nova at
`01b1e2d662a847ed7029770ecbaf6687398899dd`, nova-snark 0.76.0,
Rust >=1.88.0. Pedersen/IPA and KZG-based variants have different setup assumptions.
`test-utils` random-tau keys are explicitly insecure; they cannot pass production.
No framework security statement establishes the security of a FINNOR circuit.

Hard challenges: Y4 already establishes confidential computation with exact
correspondence, and Y5 already produces theorems. This is neither: the claim is
succinct per-instance computational execution integrity with runtime relation
compilation. Installing a proof library, hashing a transcript, or checking a
signature cannot qualify. Standard equivalent proof tools remain enabled for
equipped-X/Y comparators. Arithmetic is range-constrained, not silently modulo a
field instead of canonical signed money.

### C5: compositional institutional assurance

One output: `InstitutionCompositionCertificate`. Infer/check explicit local
transition contracts and discharge the entire assumption network to establish a
global property of concurrently composed admitted components without enumerating
the entire product. This verifies fixed components; it does not synthesize C3's
winning policy or C4's proof of one execution.

For local $M_i$, rely $R_i$, guarantee $G_i$, require independently checked
$M_i\models(R_i\Rightarrow G_i)$ and a sound noncircular composition rule
discharging each $R_i$ from the real environment and peer guarantees. Check
initial conditions, interface ownership, interference, deadlocks, and progress.
The formula $(G_1\Rightarrow G_2)\land(G_2\Rightarrow G_1)$ proves neither
guarantee. Liveness needs an accepted well-founded/progress rule, not circular
promises. Counterexamples must reach original component behaviors.

Pacti (2025 journal version), OCRA, rely–guarantee theory, and ECOOP 2026 layered
concurrent-object trace refinement demonstrate relevant machinery and limitations.
Pacti's algebraic contracts are not an all-behavior business truth oracle.
The ECOOP artifact describes Rocq proof certificates for local open transition
systems; it does not certify arbitrary FINNOR processes.

Hard challenges: this can become ordinary CI, more unit tests, or redundant Y5
theorems. Require runtime-derived compositional certificates, real S4/S5/S6
consumption, independently checked assumption discharge, and component replacement
without pretending an untested global product ran. Concurrency and observation
semantics cannot be hidden behind a coarse contract.

2. More economic operators: full Y1.
3. Richer strategic simulation: full Y2.
4. More game dates or static flattening: full Y3 without a new qualitative guarantee.
5. Another MPC wrapper or private database: full Y4.
6. More theorem generation: full Y5.
7. Another experience cache, policy library or transfer trainer: full X5.
8. Probability circuits, confidence sequences and continuous stopping as five
renamed scopes: explicitly already considered as methods by the supplied Y PRD.

## 100× feasibility challenge

There are no measured baseline bottleneck shares yet. No five mechanism ratios
are assigned. Proposed savings must be shown on the same total parent-resource
capacity comparison, with qualified full X/Y, Z0 parity, protected material task
units, complete costs, positive comparator capacity and simultaneous uncertainty.

For each actually limiting resource j, if the unchanged irreducible demand per
accepted assignment is $u_j$ and baseline demand is $b_j$, a necessary condition
for 100× on that resource is $u_j/b_j\le0.01$, before adding any new overhead.
Provider rate limits, unique external observations, exact approvals and final
effects may independently make the target unattainable. These cannot be replaced
by a computational proof or credited as shared completion of separate assignments.

The analysis above is a research hypothesis, not a promised achievement. Retain
UNPASSED/BLOCKED if measured physics, workload economics, comparison or custody
does not support the requested endpoint.
