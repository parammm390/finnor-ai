# Programme qualification design before implementation

Status: proposed analysis contract. No workload result, capability qualification,
counterparty preference, financial value or multiplier has been measured here.

## One endpoint, no manufactured numerator

Freeze independently administered business assignments before candidate exposure.
Each assignment has an immutable business identity, materiality weight, arrival,
deadline, oracle, rights/exposure class and original parent reservation. Subtasks,
proofs, model states, generated code, messages and repeated reports are not work
units. Every offered, unstarted, refused, failed, assisted, late and unfinished
assignment remains on an intent-to-treat ledger.

For actor $a$, load $\lambda$ and independent institution/period block $w$:

$$Q_a(\lambda,w)=\sum_i \omega_i
  1\{\text{independently accepted within original deadline}\}.$$

$$C_a=\max_{\lambda\in\Lambda:\text{original floors hold}}
  \mathbb E[Q_a(\lambda,w)].$$

Mandatory comparison: completed qualified S/P/M/R/V + ALL intended X1–X5 and
Y1–Y5, with full native methods, learned assets, code generation, specialists,
strongest eligible fallback and equivalent Z0 convergence adapters. Actual current
source is not a substitute for that release. Other registered equipped actors
remain mandatory when a competitive claim is intended.

The additional post-X/Y target is exactly:

$$L_{Z}\ge100\max_{b\in\mathcal B}U_b,\qquad
L_{XY}\ge\epsilon_{XY}>0,\qquad
\max_bL_b\ge\epsilon>0.$$

Positive material epsilon values, business weights, task strata, deadlines,
arrival rules and load grid must come from the mandate/independent calibration,
not from candidate outcomes. There is no numeric epsilon supplied by this
repository inspection; its absence blocks scoring, not engineering.

## Simultaneous inference and sustainable loads

Use at least 30 independent institution/period blocks, increasing by independently
registered power analysis. Preserve paired/crossover randomization where lawful.
Do not use individual model steps, replays or correlated assignments as independent
blocks. Separate calibration, public development and protected evaluation.

The pre-exposure analysis must cover actors, strata, floors and tested loads
simultaneously at 95%. A cluster-level max-statistic bootstrap or independently
justified conservative finite-sample familywise procedure is acceptable after
its assumptions and implementation are registered. A bare per-cell 95% interval
does not establish simultaneous 95% coverage. Normal approximations with tiny,
heavy-tailed or few clusters need justification.

For a finite fully tested grid with valid simultaneous intervals, candidate
capacity lower bounds can use qualifying cells whose original floors are
established. Comparator upper capacity bounds must include every tested cell
not ruled nonqualifying and every unresolved potentially qualifying cell.
An untested load with potential higher qualifying capacity is an upper-capacity
gap. Do not replace it with zero, silently extrapolate, or treat a failed
high-load trial as an upper bound on all intermediate loads.

Qualification correctness is measured on CLAIMED completions before filtering
the numerator to accepted completions. Reporting only accepted assignments
would make correctness tautological. Correct refusals count for safety only.
Measurement failures, wrong builds/sessions and missing costs cannot be turned
into candidate passes or baseline failures.

## Matching resources and authority

Match model/provider access, preparation, observations, source information,
tool access, rights, mandatory approvals, capital, risk, contact exposure,
deadlines, horizon, storage, compute, integration, training, proof generation,
checking, cryptographic setup and human supervision/rescue.

Keep inherited episode limits: $100 compute/data/integration; 900 human seconds;
16 GiB aggregate simultaneous peak; 30-second initial response; 3,600-second wall.
Preparation: 80 human hours and $10,000 per actor. Register multiweek/asset
construction/learning/proof aggregate envelopes separately. Children, retries,
checker restarts, fallbacks and observer timeouts never reset grants or deadlines.

Charge failed candidate builds, rejected proofs, ineffective updates, stored
state, maintenance and invalidation. Preregister actual reuse horizon and demand.
Do not amortize over hypothetical unlimited future work. General engineering
costs are separately disclosed in lifecycle analysis; do not invent historical
comparator development costs.

The current machine has about 8 GiB physical memory, not a 16 GiB qualification
receipt. Local development must bound simultaneous use below actual availability.
No cloud/GPU/second Region, production credential or extra paid service is assumed.

## Limiter and impossibility accounting

Measure baseline complete accepted-work demand vector $d_b$ and residual
candidate demand vector $d_Z$ across every potentially limiting resource.
For a serial resource, an unchanged share $f$ gives the familiar upper bound
$1/f$ before any new overhead. Use actual concurrency, pipeline and queueing
models elsewhere. Latency is not throughput.

For bottleneck resource $j$, a necessary steady-state capacity condition is:

$$d_{Z,j}\le d_{b,j}/100.$$

If mandatory per-assignment approval, source observation, provider rate, unique
effect dispatch, human decision or counterparty participation exceeds the
residual budget, no amount of mathematical compression of other computation
can satisfy that resource bound. Publish the bound and retain UNPASSED.

Z0 bug fixes cannot provide novelty credit. Engine-specific cost ratios are
diagnostics, never multiplied together. Public research speedups, token prices
and larger parallel worker counts do not enter an unmeasured business numerator.

## Financial gate is separate

Preserve original principal S7 attribution and the $100 million additional net
owner-value objective; require a frozen material signed decision-payoff improvement
excluding clerical savings. Match capital, downside, rights, resources and horizon.
Track H0 observed/settled state, H1 provisional outcomes and H2 at 36 months.

A new financial 100× additionally requires:

$$L_{\text{Z net owner value}}\ge
100\max_bU_{\text{b net owner value}},\qquad
L_{\text{XY net owner value}}\ge\epsilon_{\text{financial XY}}>0.$$

Intervals include identification, attribution, valuation, sampling, missing
outcomes and full costs. Capital raised, AUM, transaction value, forecasts,
modeled surplus, simulated cash and market appreciation do not qualify as
attributable additional owner wealth.

## Evidence custody

Each protected gate needs an independently governed specification, hidden
population, immutable checker/measurement identity, exposure register and secure
validator path. A new process or another model does not by itself provide
independence. Builders cannot alter or inspect hidden cases and future outcomes.

Public fixtures and locally independent mathematical recomputation are
development evidence. A formal result names its exact theorem, assumptions,
kernel and implementation binding. A cryptographic receipt names its
computational assumptions and soundness parameter; it is not unconditional
mathematical truth. Neither establishes source truth or actual economic effects.
