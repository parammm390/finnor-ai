## 3. A falsifiable `ViabilityStrategy` contract

### Single output and mathematical profile

One finite artifact binds original mandate/model/observation semantics; arena/initial set; safety, recurrence and fairness; complete winning region; finite strategy memory/action/update maps; independent fixed-point/progress evidence; resource/effect interpretation; applicability and invalidation. These are parts of one output, not new ledgers or owners.

Start with a finite, total, turn-based, perfect-information arena

$$
G=(V_C\uplus V_E,E,I,S,\{A_i\}_{i=1}^{a},\{B_j\}_{j=1}^{b}).
$$

$I$ is the original initial set, $S$ safe states, $A_i$ environmental justice sets and $B_j$ recurrence sets. Uncertain outcomes pass through environment-owned choices. Deadlocks need explicit semantics, not vacuous universal quantification. Require

$$
\forall v_0\in I\;\forall\pi\in\operatorname{Plays}(G,\sigma,v_0):
\mathbf G S\ \land\
\left[\left(\bigwedge_i\mathbf G\mathbf F A_i\right)
\Rightarrow\left(\bigwedge_j\mathbf G\mathbf F B_j\right)\right].
$$

**Safety stays outside the fairness implication:** unfairness never licenses unsafe action. This is model-relative.

For $U\subseteq V$,

$$
\operatorname{CPre}(U)=
\{v\in V_C:\exists(v,w)\in E,\ w\in U\}
\cup\{v\in V_E:\forall(v,w)\in E,\ w\in U\}.
$$

Compute $W_S=\nu Z.(S\cap\operatorname{CPre}(Z))$. Within this safety kernel restrict controller choices, retaining every environment successor. The standard GR(1) pattern is

$$
W=\nu Z.\bigcap_j\mu Y.\bigcup_i\nu X.
\left[(B_j\cap\operatorname{CPre}(Z))
\cup\operatorname{CPre}(Y)
\cup(\neg A_i\cap\operatorname{CPre}(X))\right].
$$

Use $A_1=V$ for unconditional generalized Büchi. Require $I\subseteq W$, not one favorable initial state. An arbitrary fixed point is not necessarily the required least/greatest one.

A finite graph encodes infinite plays. Strategy memory may include a recurrence-goal index; generalized Büchi/GR(1) strategies are not all positional. The boundary changes temporal guarantees, not state-space size.

### Independent winning-region/progress checks

The checker derives obligations from original input semantics:

1. Check exact nested iterations or an accepted equivalent certificate, including initial coverage. A winning subset is not the complete winning region.
2. Check totality, action legality, memory initialization/update and every environment outcome. Check all permitted strategy-relation resolutions, or bind its actual deterministic refinement.
3. Check progress rankings/iteration levels toward each goal, allowing only the declared fairness escape. A constant safe rank proves no liveness.
4. Independently check the reachable arena × strategy-memory product. For each $B_j$, remove its vertices from that originally reachable product. A cyclic strongly connected component intersecting every $A_i$ admits a fair infinite suffix violating that guarantee. Inspect **non-bottom** components too: the adversary can refuse an available exit.
5. Rejecting one strategy does not prove unrealizability. INFEASIBLE needs complete-region or checked dual-counterstrategy evidence. Exhaustion is INCOMPLETE/UNKNOWN.

A proposed tractable reference uses at most eight vertices and two assumptions/two guarantees, independently justified finite-memory bounds, exhaustive strategy enumeration and the graph oracle. These tests are **not run**, nor permanent supported-domain limits.

### Information, recurrence and fairness

Begin with genuinely public, completely observed state. A modeled private value or hidden persistent world is not a controller feature. Later observation abstraction needs checked lifting and observation-uniform actions. Passing latent state to a perfect-information solver violates S4/Y2/Y3; general imperfect-information/distributed synthesis is not supplied.

“Unbounded recurring” means arbitrary repetitions with bounded simultaneous obligations, not unlimited queues/data values. Infinitely many completions need not discharge every request. Pending/request-response monitors must preserve each original obligation, legitimate cancellation and S6 identity. Deadlines require bounded-time monitors and explicit time-advance/non-Zeno assumptions; $\mathbf G\mathbf F$ gives no wall-clock bound.

Every fairness assumption needs an independently admitted external basis. “Lenders eventually agree” and “UNKNOWN always resolves” do not follow from forecasts. Check satisfiability/nonvacuity and reject controller-induced assumption failure. Standard GR(1) winning does not establish non-conflicting behavior; synthesizing/checking that stronger condition is an additional engineering obligation.

Global GR(1) fairness can suspend **all** guarantees when one assumption fails. It is not separate resource-to-obligation fairness. P1 explains this distinction. Restrict the supported contract or separately qualify richer objectives; never silently weaken the property.

### Resources, UNKNOWN and native execution

S5 capacity, occupation, contention and replenishment remain original state. Positive renewal cost plus a finite nonrenewing budget makes infinite completion impossible. Renewal and renewed grants need an admitted basis. A temporal theorem grants neither infinite compute/free storage nor renewed parent deadlines or erased costs.

Model authority denial, crash/recovery, pending/partial/UNKNOWN outcomes and independent reconciliation. UNKNOWN can hold capacity forever. Safety must survive; conditional liveness cannot conceal a missing resolution premise. STOP prevents new commitments without erasing old liabilities.

S4 consumes the admitted strategy; S1/S3 retain evidence/model meaning; S5 retains resources; S6 rechecks authority/effects; S8 admits; P7 invalidates; existing Work exposes the result. Refusal of a proved action must be modeled or invalidate the guarantee. A checked replacement/invariant must bridge revisions without erasing exposure.

### First domain and falsifiers

Start with controlled recurring internal reconciliation/readiness, known lawful operations and bounded outstanding obligations, not perpetual financing.

Freeze winning and losing games: safe wait-only recurrence failure; a locally attractive action entering an unserviceable cycle; permanent UNKNOWN locking a reservation; nonrenewing budget exhaustion; identical observations needing opposite actions; mutated environment edges, recurrence labels or policy bytes. Reject identity resets, favorable-prefix evidence and stale proof/source bindings.

These distinguish semantic guarantees, **not comparative superiority**: equipped S4/R2/Y5 may solve the same game. Missing: mandate-to-game synthesis, original-behavior refinement, fairness basis, independent region/progress checking, native recovery/consumption, complete meters and qualified trials.
