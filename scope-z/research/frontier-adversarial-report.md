## 2. What would constitute a strict separation and a 100× result?

### 2.1 Required witness

A qualifying witness must specify

$$
W_n=(I_n,\mathcal A_n,\mathcal O_n,\mathcal M_n,\Phi_n,\epsilon_n,R_n,T_n),
$$

where these are the original inputs, lawful actions, available observations, retained model assumptions, acceptance properties, tolerances, total-resource envelope, and horizon. It must then establish:

1. The Z output is independently accepted on the original problem.
2. Every inherited correctness, information, confidentiality, risk, and authority condition remains intact.
3. A qualified completed-X+Y equipped comparator cannot meet the same declared acceptance condition within the matched envelope, or demonstrably consumes more of a preregistered binding resource.
4. The difference comes from the proposed primitive, not withheld libraries, weaker baseline engineering, extra preprocessing, an easier action set, or a different oracle.

The dossiers distinguish an **exact weak witness** against a restricted representation from a **strict full-baseline witness**. No strict full-baseline witness is established here. This is an evidentiary finding, not a claim of mathematical impossibility for every future Z.

For a new semantic primitive, a witness might show an admissible computation previously unavailable. For a resource primitive, both systems may compute the same function, but a variable-size family and complete accounting must demonstrate the resource difference. Neither a new artifact name nor one favorable microbenchmark is sufficient.

### 2.2 Strong equipped comparators

The comparator gets the known relevant techniques: indexed shared dataflows, exact algebraic simplification, knowledge compilation and variable elimination, decision-focused experiment design, modular proofs, established verifiable-computation libraries, and appropriate specialized algorithms. These are not unfair additions; they prevent baseline starvation.

Any construction, training, theorem discovery, proof generation, preprocessing, integration, and adaptation costs enter both sides' budgets. Common prior assets and privately learned assets need explicit symmetric treatment. A candidate cannot receive a free prebuilt circuit while the comparator pays to rediscover it, or vice versa.

At least three comparison facts remain separate:

- **B_live:** what the pinned checkout currently runs.
- **B_full:** the full intended completed X+Y capability contract.
- **B_eq:** a qualified, strongly equipped realization satisfying that contract for the evaluated domain.

A win over B_live does not establish a win over B_full. Until an adequate B_eq exists and its qualification is independently accepted, the requested comparative gate remains unavailable.

### 2.3 The business-work numerator

For preregistered assignments with fixed materiality weights, let

$$
A_D(T)=\sum_i w_i\,
\mathbf 1\{\text{independently accepted original business assignment }i
\text{ completed by }T\}.
$$

Refusals, proposals, partial work, duplicate outputs, canceled attempts, and unobserved effects do not count as completed work. Their costs remain. A failure cannot disappear from the denominator because a different task was selected later.

Distinct obligations may legitimately share computation, but merely copying one answer to many task labels cannot manufacture material work. Independent acceptance checks the actual obligation and outcome, not the presence of a proof-shaped file.

The target is a **programme-level requirement**, not a product of five hypothetical multipliers. A defensible operational gate would require, under a preregistered simultaneous analysis,

$$
L(A_{Z0+Z1:\!Z5})\ \ge\
100\max_j U(A_{B_{\mathrm{eq},j}}),
$$

with materially positive comparator bounds, identical assignment populations, matched authority, matched total resources, and all inherited quality floors. The confidence level, clustering, power, horizon, and stopping rules must be fixed by the independent evaluator before scored runs. The inspected X architecture uses simultaneous 95% analysis; this report does not substitute an easier analysis or invent a Z sample size.

Standalone capability witnesses, add-one comparisons, leave-one-out comparisons, all-five-disabled comparison, operational 100×, and mature economic efficacy are different gates. None was run here.

### 2.4 Total means total

At minimum, meter money, elapsed time, CPU/GPU/device time, peak concurrent memory, retained storage and occupation, network traffic, provider calls and quotas, observations, human construction/review/approval time, proof production and checking, physical experiments, capital locks, expected and tail liabilities, failed attempts, recovery, fallback, and historical asset construction.

Unknown costs are unknown, not zero. Free disposal of indexed history, trusted setup, previous proofs, learned models, or abandoned experiments would invalidate the comparison. Amortization requires a declared horizon and an actual workload, not an imagined unlimited future.

## 4. Z1: checked semantic change transducers

### Single output and capability boundary

**Output:** one checked change transducer for an existing pure, typed computation. It maps an admitted change and retained computational state to the exact answer change. Its proof and dependency description are parts of that one mathematical artifact, not a new authority record.

The stronger research question is whether independently arising business questions can share and update their computational consequences at a semantic level, including limited changes in programme structure, while preserving original owner obligations. The familiar weaker form, ordinary incremental view maintenance, is not proposed as a new Z scope.

### Mechanism

For a change action on inputs and outputs, the required identity is

$$
f(x\oplus\delta x)
=f(x)\oplus Df(x,\delta x).
$$

For snapshot streams, DBSP expresses incrementalization as

$$
f^\Delta=\mathcal D\circ(\uparrow f)\circ\mathcal I.
$$

The expression alone need not improve cost: it can reconstruct and recompute the entire input. Benefits require algebraic rewrites, maintained state, and operators whose changes are cheaper than their full results. Joins require retained arrangements and their output delta may be large. Shared arrangements expose consistent time-indexed views of physically shared indexed state to different queries [P01–P02].

A plausible stronger artifact would justify reuse across syntactically different but proven equivalent pure subcomputations, with exact invalidation when source semantics change. It cannot identify equivalent arbitrary programmes by magic. Restricting the language and accepting incomplete equivalence discovery is necessary; completeness is not assumed.

An honest cost expression is

$$
C_{\rm Z1}
=C_{\rm construct}+C_{\rm initial}
+C_{\rm retained}
+\sum_t(C_{\rm affected,t}+C_{\rm output,t}+C_{\rm check,t}).
$$

The bound is on the affected computational trace and required output, not simply on input-delta size.

### What completed X/Y already does

X3 can install a maintained incremental application. X5 can retain parameter-dependent assets. R1 already checks continuation equivalence and reuse. P/M owners already manage revisions, dependencies, and durable execution. Y5 can discover and prove incremental algorithms and correspondence. None of this is missing merely because the current task executor happens to recompute.

### Exact witness and strict-separation challenge

**Weak witness:** let $N$ records contribute to $S=\sum_i x_i$, with $K$ separately required outputs $g_j(S)$. After one record changes, a deliberately independent full-scan evaluator uses $\Theta(KN)$ additions, while a shared maintained sum uses constant update work plus the necessary $K$ output computations. The equality is exact.

**Equipped-comparator defeat:** an X3 application can maintain that same sum and share its index. Y5 can prove the update law. Thus the example is **not** a strict separation from completed X/Y.

**Required strict witness, presently absent:** a family of previously unseen, semantically related computations for which the stronger transducer construction, checking, reuse discovery, and invalidation all fit the budget, while the equally equipped completed baseline does not. A benchmark forbidding the baseline's shared arrangements or reuse assets is inadmissible.

### Primary evidence and limitations

- DBSP, 2022-03-30 version read [P01], supplies the stream calculus and incrementalization mechanism, not automatic business-level computational equivalence.
- Shared Arrangements, 2020-06-11 version read [P02], supplies actual cross-query indexed-state sharing. Its reported database measurements are not FINNOR results.
- **Fixing the Fixpoint**, 2026-09-30 [P03], shows why a first zero delta can be an unsound termination test. Its internal-convergence criterion has soundness and useful-class completeness results, with a Lean formalization. Arbitrary expressive circuits do not admit the desired general exact detector.

The last result prevents citing “arbitrary recursive incrementalization” as a completed, generally sound runtime implementation.

### First supported domain and independent oracle

The first defensible research domain is pure exact relational/rational calculations over lawful, shared evidence snapshots, with explicitly declared recursion and update semantics. External reads and effects are outside the reusable computation.

An independently written snapshot evaluator can rebuild the full original answer after every bounded change. Compare values, ties where relevant, dependencies, and resource demands, not producer-generated delta expectations. Rebuild-on-every-change is a tractable oracle for small fixtures even when it is a deliberately slow candidate comparator.

### Failure modes, independence, and potential consequence

Retractions, negation, branch changes, stale semantic hashes, inconsistent timestamp frontiers, hidden-world leakage, invalid recursion termination, nonlocal constraint effects, and retained-state growth can erase or reverse the benefit. One changed scalar can change every output. Evidence reuse must not cross rights or confidentiality boundaries.

Z1 can be evaluated with one fully known world, no experiments, no component-composition problem, and direct execution checking. It therefore does not require Z2–Z5. Its potential consequence is removal of repeated *pure* work on change-heavy workloads. No observation, approval, effect, or whole-business throughput multiplier follows.

**Disposition:** ordinary form absorbed; stronger strict separation OPEN.

## 5. Z2: checked implicit joint-uncertainty query circuits

### Single output and capability boundary

**Output:** one circuit representing the specified query over the original joint model support, with independently checkable representation and query conditions.

The boundary is an intensional answer representation, not a new learned world model, probability law, or economic operator. It aims to avoid constructing every world before answering a particular accepted question.

### Mechanism

Write a query schematically as

$$
Q(e)=\bigoplus_{h}
\bigotimes_k \psi_k(e,h).
$$

The operators and their order must match the question: Boolean support, weighted probability, minimum cost, or another admitted algebra. A probability sum cannot replace an adversarial minimum. Likewise, $\max_a\sum_h$ cannot generally be exchanged with $\sum_h\max_a$; doing so can create an anticipatory policy.

Circuit structure controls tractability. Decomposability separates product-variable scopes; smoothness aligns sum scopes; determinism separates supports. Combining circuits can require compatibility, and alternating aggregation operators can require stronger marginal properties. These are mathematical conditions to check, not generic claims that every sum-product graph makes every query easy [P04–P05].

For a bounded-treewidth factorization with variable alphabet size $d$ and width $w$, variable-elimination-style computation can have work on the order of $n d^{w+1}$ instead of enumerating $d^n$ assignments. This is a representation-conditioned analysis, not an unconditional algorithm for uncertainty.

For Y2 identified sets without qualified probabilities, retain support and common model identity. Do not assign convenient independent weights. A single persistent model or common parameter can couple otherwise small factors.

### What completed X/Y already does

Y2 retains model sets; X4 supplies identified dynamics; R provides robust search and finite reduction; Y5 can discover uniform variable elimination or knowledge-compilation algorithms with proofs. X3 can install the resulting engine. A compact circuit is not beyond Y5 merely because an existing implementation stores an explicit table.

### Exact witness and strict-separation challenge

**Weak witness:** for $n$ independent binary variables, the generating polynomial

$$
\prod_{i=1}^{n}\big((1-p_i)+p_i z\big)
$$

encodes the exact distribution of their sum. Computing coefficients only up to a specified threshold $k$ takes $O(nk)$ arithmetic operations and answers the threshold query without listing $2^n$ assignments.

**Equipped-comparator defeat:** the same polynomial dynamic programme is an ordinary proved family algorithm available to Y5. Independence is a premise, not something Z2 may invent. The witness fails as a strict full-baseline separation.

**Required strict witness, absent:** an actual admitted business-query family whose original dependent support, query order, construction cost, proof cost, and arithmetic bit complexity fit the Z2 envelope, but not a comparator equipped with the same established compilation techniques. Saving only explicit-world storage is insufficient.

### Primary evidence and limitations

- The 2021 circuit-operation atlas [P05] gives sufficient conditions and hardness results. Incompatible products and unsupported transformations do not inherit easy inference.
- **A Compositional Atlas for Algebraic Circuits**, 2025-02-24 [P04], treats aggregation, product, and elementwise mappings across semirings. It explicitly supplies sufficient tractability conditions, not universally necessary conditions or a general small-circuit constructor.
- PyJuice's first-party implementation [P06], with a release shown on 2026-10-22, demonstrates practical PC compilation into GPU-friendly representations. Floating-point likelihood computation and its reported training benchmarks do not provide exact rational, robust-control, legal, or financial certification.

### First supported domain and independent oracle

Start with exact bounded-width declarative dependency models used for an internal reconciliation or feasibility question, where the full factorization and meaning of every quantity are independently admitted. A supplied compact model is not independently identified merely because it is tractable.

For small variable counts, a separate original-assignment enumerator can check support, normalization where lawful, exact query values, and action/information ordering. Structural checks must additionally inspect the actual circuit and its connection to original factors. Numerical agreement on samples cannot establish equivalence over unvisited worlds.

### Failure modes, independence, and potential consequence

Compilation can be exponential; circuit size can explode after a new constraint; arithmetic bit lengths can dominate operation counts. Determinism can be costly to establish. Marginals of a convenient model can be exact while the model is wrong. A factorization that removes cross-party dependence or repeated-game correlations changes the original problem.

Z2 works on one immutable instance with no reuse, no new observations, one component, and direct checking. Z1 cannot replace it when all subcomputations are new; Z3 cannot remove uncertainty that affects the required answer.

Potential consequence: replace explicit exponential world enumeration with an admitted compact calculation on a restricted structural domain. It does **not** eliminate exponential uncertainty in general or establish any 100× business result.

**Disposition:** ordinary form absorbed; certified-query strict separation OPEN.

## 6. Z3: decision-sufficiency certificates instead of unnecessary identification

### Single output and capability boundary

**Output:** one certificate that the remaining uncertainty cannot alter the original required decision beyond its prescribed tolerance, including feasibility and liabilities.

The boundary is between “identify the system” and “acquire only the information necessary for this original decision.” It is not permission to skip mandatory evidence, narrow the action space, or accept unverified business facts.

### Mechanism

Let $\Theta_t$ be a model set justified by the actual permitted observations. A proposed policy $\pi$ can stop acquiring information only if, for example,

$$
\sup_{\theta\in\Theta_t}
\left[V^*(\theta)-V^\pi(\theta)\right]\le\epsilon,
$$

and every original hard constraint holds for every relevant retained model. This is a proposed regret certificate, not a theorem that every business can cheaply produce one.

If $\Theta_t$ is statistical, its simultaneous coverage must survive adaptive observation and stopping:

$$
\Pr_\theta\{\theta\in\Theta_t\text{ for every }t\}\ge 1-\delta.
$$

That guarantee requires the stated sampling and model assumptions. A supplied causal identified set without a probability law is different from a confidence sequence. Neither type should be relabeled as the other.

Statistical decision theory also imposes a floor. In the independent one-parameter bandit setting of [P07],

$$
\mathbb E_\mu[\tau_\delta]\ge
T^*(\mu)\operatorname{kl}(\delta,1-\delta),
$$

where

$$
\frac1{T^*(\mu)}
=\sup_{w\in\Delta_K}\inf_{\lambda\in\operatorname{Alt}(\mu)}
\sum_a w_a\,\operatorname{KL}(P_{\mu_a},P_{\lambda_a}).
$$

This is not a universal business formula; it is a precise example of unavoidable evidence needed to distinguish models requiring different answers.

### What completed X/Y already does

X4 already chooses experiments and tests withheld interventions. R4 already searches for sufficient identification plans with costs and timing. Y2 already keeps model ambiguity rather than demanding one falsely precise model. S4 robust control can act when the ambiguity is harmless. Y5 can prove decision-sufficiency results.

Thus “do decision-focused active learning” is not a new Z capability.

### Exact witness and strict-separation challenge

**Weak witness:** let the model be $\theta=(s,\eta_1,\ldots,\eta_n)$ with independent unknown bits. The only decision has payoff one exactly when $a=s$. A lawful noiseless query reveals $s$. One query determines the decision; learning every nuisance bit requires further observations.

**Equipped-comparator defeat:** completed X4/R4 can request the query of $s$ directly, and robust S4 can ignore the nuisance bits. Full identification was never a mandatory completed-baseline restriction. The apparent separation is therefore invalid.

**Required strict witness, absent:** a lawful experimental family where the certificate and its actual observations are cheaper than the strongest equipped baseline's decision-focused plan, while preserving the same full action family, contingent deviations, risk, and evidence requirements.

If two observationally indistinguishable retained models require incompatible accepted decisions, no certificate can resolve the difference without a new permitted observation or a legitimate robust alternative. Compression cannot create information.

### Primary evidence and limitations

- Garivier and Kaufmann, 2016-06-01 version [P07], gives a tight sample-complexity characterization and asymptotically optimal Track-and-Stop in its specified independent bandit model. It does not cover arbitrary confounding or strategic observation.
- **Decision-Focused Sequential Experimental Design**, 2026-02-05 [P08], exploits the geometry of downstream linear optimization. Positive scaling of a predicted cost vector leaves its optimizer unchanged, motivating directional uncertainty. Earlier-stopping results have conditions, including gap assumptions for the stronger comparisons.

The latter's scale invariance is about the optimizer, not invariance of financial value, risk, liability, or evidence duties. Its experiments and guarantees do not establish valid stopping for FINNOR's full policy-conditioned strategic system.

### First supported domain and independent oracle

Start with a finite, controlled, nonstrategic decision experiment whose observation channels and risks are independently known, with a fixed gap and original acceptance tolerance. The policy may be evaluated against an explicit model set; no live experiment is authorized by this report.

A separate small-domain oracle can enumerate every retained model and permitted policy, check maximum regret and feasibility, and verify the stopping condition. For a statistical certificate, additionally use an independently specified finite sampling model or exact finite probability calculation where tractable. A synthetic generator shared with the producer is not an independent field oracle.

### Failure modes, independence, and potential consequence

Near ties make identification expensive. Model misspecification, adaptive sampling bias, confounding, delayed feedback, nonstationarity, strategic response to observation, and correlated losses invalidate easy confidence claims. A certificate for one mandate may become irrelevant under a revision. Ignoring a nuisance variable that affects a covenant is unsafe.

Z3 is independently testable on a tiny explicit model with negligible compute and no compression. Z2 can make a query cheaper but does not establish that an observation is unnecessary. Z1, Z4, and Z5 need not exist.

Potential consequence: avoid genuinely unnecessary lawful observations. It cannot avoid necessary provider readback, legal approval, required evidence, or the information-theoretic distinction between incompatible decisions.

**Disposition:** ordinary form absorbed; observable-cost strict separation OPEN.

## 7. Z4: contextual refinement interfaces for interaction checking

### Single output and capability boundary

**Output:** one checked contextual refinement interface for a component. It states the exact environment assumptions and observable guarantees under which replacement or composition preserves the original property.

This is a mathematical interface, not an additional workflow, authority ledger, or business-contract product. The stronger question is whether a compact interface can avoid whole-system interaction rechecking while admitting all relevant lawful environments.

### Mechanism

A schematic assume-guarantee rule has the form

$$
C_1\models A,\qquad
C_2\models (A\Rightarrow G)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
C_1\parallel C_2\models G,
$$

with side conditions appropriate to the underlying semantics. The implication is not a license for circular assumptions to prove themselves.

For concurrent refinement, a useful correctness relation is trace inclusion,

$$
\operatorname{Traces}(C_{\rm implementation})
\subseteq\operatorname{Traces}(C_{\rm specification}),
$$

preserved under admitted horizontal and vertical composition. Encapsulating state **and control/threads** can make noninterference and trace projection explicit [P10].

For probabilistic parameterized systems, the same parameter valuation and consistent projected strategy must underlie the component arguments. The 2025 pPA work develops precisely such projection-based rules [P09]. Independently selecting a convenient parameter or scheduler for each component would not establish the global claim.

### What completed X/Y already does

Y1 already learns composition laws and footprints. X2 supplies operation semantics; X3 maintains whole applications. Y5 already discovers uniform composition theorems and proves implementation correspondence. Verified compilation is inherited from R. A modular proof by itself is not a strict extension.

Nor may a Z4 interface weaken Y3 into checking only locally optimal strategies or finite named deviations. Full contingent unilateral deviations and global belief consistency remain required wherever relevant.

### Exact witness and strict-separation challenge

**Weak witness:** $n$ independent two-state components have $2^n$ global states. If each preserves a local invariant and their resources and observations are genuinely disjoint, a composition theorem can establish the conjunction from $n$ local proofs without enumerating the global product.

**Equipped-comparator defeat:** completed Y5 can discover or use that theorem and check the same local proofs. A monolithic model checker was not the full baseline. The exponential-versus-linear comparison is therefore not a strict Z witness.

**Required strict witness, absent:** an admissible variable-size interaction family where interfaces are constructed and checked within a matched envelope and remain sound for the full original environment class, but the strongest equipped full baseline cannot obtain the same accepted result within that envelope.

If the interface needs to encode every coupled strategy, contingent history, or global resource allocation, its size can grow like the product it was meant to avoid. A theorem allowing composition does not prove small interfaces exist.

### Primary evidence and limitations

- Mertens, Quatmann, and Katoen, 2025-06-10 [P09], provides pPA assume-guarantee rules, parameter-region consistency, strategy projections, multi-objective and reward reasoning. The conclusion explicitly leaves implementation and case studies to future work; extension to parametric stochastic games is also future work.
- Ni and Wang, published 2026-06-25 [P10], gives operational open LTS with encapsulated threads, trace-refinement composition, and a Rocq formalization. Its explicit limits are **sequential consistency, safety properties, and abstract transition systems/pseudocode**. Weak memory, liveness, and connection to actual code require further work.

Consequently neither paper establishes end-to-end correctness of deployed enterprise interactions, economic equilibria, or effectful distributed business software.

### First supported domain and independent oracle

Begin with a pure bounded concurrent component family under an explicitly pinned memory and failure model, verifying safety only. Existing owners still determine the actual authority and effect boundary.

For small products, an independently implemented reachable-state/interleaving enumerator can check the global property and test proposed interfaces against all bounded environment behaviors. A proof assistant can separately check a general theorem. Agreement between two producers using the same missing environment assumption is not independent validation.

### Failure modes, independence, and potential consequence

Hidden shared state, aliases, asynchronous callbacks, failure recovery, timing channels, weak-memory behavior, liveness obligations, and common parameter uncertainty break naive decomposition. Mutual guarantees can be vacuous. Shared capital or approval capacity cannot be framed away as “local.”

Z4 is independently testable with known deterministic component semantics and no experiments, no repeated updates, and direct execution checking. Unlike Z5, it proves a system-level semantic property, not the correctness of one run of an already specified computation.

Potential consequence: make some interaction proofs proportional to component and interface size rather than the global product. It cannot remove genuine global coupling or justify changing owners' resource invariants.

**Disposition:** ordinary form absorbed; compact contextual-interface separation OPEN.

## 8. Z5: succinct proofs of evolving computation

### Single output and capability boundary

**Output:** one succinct proof that a pinned computation executed correctly from its bound initial input to its claimed output for a specified number of steps.

It is not a proof that the inputs are true, the computation expresses the mandate, a provider performed an effect, or the result deserves independent business acceptance. Those remain different claims.

### Mechanism

For a transition function $F$,

$$
z_{i+1}=F(z_i),\qquad
\pi_i\ \text{attests to the first }i\text{ steps}.
$$

Incrementally verifiable computation updates the accumulated proof without verifying the entire earlier execution again.

Nova uses relaxed rank-one constraint systems and folding. A schematic relaxed relation is

$$
(Az)\odot(Bz)=u(Cz)+E.
$$

Folding uses a challenge to combine instances and their cross-term residuals. It accumulates a checkable relation rather than storing a literal full execution trace in the final recipient's proof.

The pinned implementation's `src/nova/nifs.rs` actually commits to a cross term, absorbs parameter and instance bindings into its random-oracle transcript, derives a challenge, and folds instances and witnesses [P11]. Its optimized absorption assumes the previous instance hash is carried in the next instance's public input. That caller invariant matters; treating the folding routine as a context-free proof compressor would be wrong.

Succinctness in step count does not automatically imply independence from step-circuit size, input/output size, preprocessing, or soundness assumptions. The first-party README distinguishes uncompressed IVC, proof compression, and a preprocessing variant for step-circuit-independent verification. Some backends require universal setup material. This report did not audit or execute those cryptographic systems.

### What completed X/Y already does

Y5 already generates machine-checked general proofs and implementation correspondence. R already has verified compilation and independent checking. Y4 already compiles exact computations into malicious-secure MPC with abort.

Y4's confidentiality is not the same property as a succinct public proof of execution. That is a meaningful distinction between mechanisms, but not proof that an equipped X+Y system cannot implement verifiable computation. Existing proof libraries are available to B_eq too.

### Exact witness and strict-separation challenge

**Weak witness:** suppose a computation has $L$ steps of ordinary cost $c$, and $m$ mutually distrustful recipients otherwise each replay it. Replaying costs approximately $mLc$. A proof route costs

$$
C_{\rm setup}+C_{\rm compile}+C_{\rm prover}(L,c)
+C_{\rm compress}+mC_{\rm verify}
+C_{\rm communication}+C_{\rm custody}.
$$

The proof route can be cheaper only if that **total** is below the strongest valid alternative. Short verification alone proves nothing about this inequality.

**Equipped-comparator defeat:** the baseline may use the same proof library, a much cheaper problem-specific certificate, an already admitted exact checker, or a verified direct algorithm. For a convex allocation, for example, replaying the optimizer is not necessarily the right comparator if an independently checkable optimality certificate exists. A signature is an alternative only when the original trust/authority contract permits it.

**Required strict witness, absent:** a real accepted business-verification family for which no stronger equipped baseline route within the matched budget closes the advantage, and where the entire prover, setup, correspondence, custody, and recipient costs still leave a material work gain.

### Primary evidence and limitations

Nova's README and folding code were read at commit `01b1e2d662a847ed7029770ecbaf6687398899dd`, whose repository date was 2026-09-11 [P11]. They provide an actual maintained implementation, not a speculative recursive-proof label.

The original Nova and MicroNova PDF links were not processable by the available page reader. Their first-party bibliographic/abstract material is not treated here as a full security-proof review. The mechanism above is grounded in the pinned code and README. No claim is made that reading those files verifies the cycle-of-curves instantiation, compiler, setup ceremony, implementation security, or business integration.

### First supported domain and independent oracle

Start with public or appropriately authorized pure bounded-integer computations already tied to original source semantics. Do not begin by assuming a prover may pool Y4 participants' private witnesses. Distributed/private proof production would be another substantial research obligation.

A separate exact interpreter can replay small original executions and compare the actual public statement, outputs, step counts, range semantics, and proof acceptance. Malformed, altered, stale, and wrong-program statements must be rejected. Small tests cannot establish cryptographic soundness; that requires the accepted theorem assumptions and a reviewed implementation.

### Failure modes, independence, and potential consequence

A proof can correctly certify the wrong programme. Field arithmetic can wrap while business integers must not. A stale computation can remain cryptographically valid after a source revision. Setup compromise, transcript mistakes, verifier bugs, omitted public bindings, leakage, and prover overhead are material failure modes.

A cryptographic argument also changes the mathematical error model relative to an unconditional exact checker. If the existing acceptance floor forbids that change, this candidate is inadmissible under matched authority and risk.

Z5 is testable on one known deterministic sequential computation, with no model uncertainty, experiments, sharing, or component composition. It compresses *checking of an executed computation*; Z4 checks semantic composition. They must not both claim credit for the same avoided replay.

Potential consequence: lower repeated computational-integrity verification costs when the valid alternative really is costly verification by many recipients. It does not establish truthful observations, legitimate approval, private-input pooling permission, or more completed business effects.

**Disposition:** established primitive is comparator equipment; total-resource strict separation OPEN.

## 9. Five other distinct candidates, challenged and rejected

### C6. Intervention-preserving macrovariable dynamics

**Single output and boundary.** One reduced controlled model whose macrovariables preserve every declared observable and relevant admitted intervention. This reduces dynamical state, unlike Z2's representation of a joint query or Z3's observation-stopping criterion.

**Mechanism.** For a linear projection $L$ and controlled dynamics $\dot x=f(x,u)$, exact closure would require

$$
Lf(x,u)=g(Lx,u)
$$

for every admitted $x,u$, with preserved required observations. An autonomous exact-lumping identity is not automatically valid under new controls. Approximate closure additionally needs an error bound valid over the original horizon and constraint margins.

**Completed baseline.** R1 already preserves finite information-state control semantics through independently checked reduction. X4 already learns intervention models. Y5 already covers uniform reductions and their implementation correspondence.

**Exact witness and equipped path.** A biochemical system with $\dot x_1=-kx_1$, $\dot x_2=-kx_2$ admits $y=x_1+x_2$ and $\dot y=-ky$ for that output. But states $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ cease to be equivalent for an intervention whose effect depends on $x_1$ alone. Exact polynomial identity checking establishes the restricted reduction; it does not establish unrestricted intervention preservation. The equipped baseline can use the valid reduction or refuse the invalid extension. **No strict full-baseline witness.**

**Primary sources.** The 2026-08-07 causal-MDP abstraction paper [P12] partitions states by property-related feature causes and compares average, interval-MDP, and stochastic-game abstractions. It explicitly requires expensive analysis of the whole MDP to obtain its effect sets and causes. The 2024-11-21 approximate constrained-lumping paper [P13] defines the residual

$$
\|Lf(\bar L Lx)-Lf(x)\|_2
$$

and bounds finite-horizon reduction error under its assumptions. It does not prove arbitrary controlled, strategic, or uncertain-parameter preservation.

**First supported domain and oracle.** A declared polynomial/rational ODE family with specified observables and controls. Independent symbolic coefficient/identity checking is tractable for small bounded degrees; original-versus-reduced finite-horizon integration with validated bounds is needed for approximate claims.

**Hardness and failures.** Finding useful reductions can cost as much as solving the original problem. Approximation error can cross a covenant boundary, unobserved variables can mediate interventions, and parameter changes can destroy lumpability. There is no universal small quotient.

**Independence and potential consequence.** Works on one dynamical model without updates, experiments, cryptography, or component proofs. It can reduce simulation dimension on favorable models. That is not evidence of 100× material business work.

**Selection:** reject as a new Z boundary; useful baseline scientific technique.

### C7. Realizability-complete continuous/function-space control

**Single output and boundary.** One realizable controller with a checked original-problem gap bound, obtained without an arbitrary finite menu excluding better controls.

**Mechanism.** Occupation measures represent a trajectory/control pair through its time spent in state-action regions. For suitable test functions, the weak dynamics constraint has the form

$$
\int\phi(T,x)\,d\mu_T-\phi(0,x_0)
=\int(\partial_t\phi+\nabla\phi\cdot f)\,d\mu.
$$

An optimization over measures can be convex even when the original control problem is not. Moment/SOS approximations supply bounds under appropriate premises. The decisive additional obligation is recovering an actually realizable control and bounding the gap to the original, not merely reporting a relaxed optimum.

**Completed baseline.** Y1 can admit parameterized economic meanings, X4 can identify controlled dynamics, Y3 already admits full contingent deviations, and Y5 covers uniform algorithms. The completed baseline is not confined to a current producer's finite grid.

**Exact witness and equipped path.** On $u\in[0,1]$, minimizing $(u-\tfrac13)^2$ has optimum $u=\tfrac13$. A menu $\{0,\tfrac12,1\}$ misses it. This is an exact witness against that menu, **not** against completed X/Y, which can represent the parameterized control. Comparing against the local Y3 synthesis grid would violate the requested baseline.

**Primary source.** Augier, Korda, and Rios-Zertuche, 2025-03-17 [P14], establishes sufficient no-gap conditions using state-constrained relaxation theory. Its open-domain result requires boundedness/local-Lipschitz conditions and appropriate open state/target sets; closed-domain extensions require additional premises. The paper explicitly discusses gaps and open questions.

**First supported domain and oracle.** A small nonstrategic controlled process with independently known polynomial dynamics, actuator limits, and a nonzero safety margin. An analytic scalar solution or independently checked primal/dual bounds plus validated trajectory evaluation can be a tractable oracle.

**Hardness and failures.** No-gap limits need not give a finite executable controller with the required dwell time. Chattering may be physically impossible; transaction costs, indivisible actions, state constraints, delay, and irreversible commitments invalidate relaxed mixtures. Truncated moment hierarchies are not automatically exact.

**Independence and potential consequence.** This concerns the admissible action function space, not reuse, observation acquisition, or proof compression. It could eliminate a genuine artificial action restriction. No such restriction has been demonstrated in FULL intended X/Y.

**Selection:** reject as a present Z choice; do not invent a restrictive baseline to make it radical.

### C8. Equality-saturation quotients of programme search

**Single output and boundary.** One chosen programme representative with an independently checked equivalence derivation, obtained from a compact representation of many equivalent programmes.

**Mechanism.** E-graphs represent congruence classes of terms. Rewrites add equalities without immediately choosing one direction; congruence closure and rebuilding propagate them; a cost model extracts a representative. Egglog combines this with Datalog-style rules, incremental execution, partial-function tables, and merge behavior [P15].

**Completed baseline.** X2 already supplies learned operation semantics; R already provides verified compilation and finite CEGIS; X5 retains executable assets; Y5 already discovers reductions and uniform algorithms. An e-graph is an implementation technique available to those systems.

**Exact witness and equipped path.** Associativity yields Catalan-many parenthesizations of an $n$-term exact sum; adding commutativity yields many more syntactic forms. A congruence representation or canonical normalization avoids separate exploration of each. The equipped baseline can apply the same proved rules or a simpler canonical form. **No strict full-baseline witness.**

**Primary source.** Zhang et al., 2023-04-11 version [P15], provides full egglog semantics, rebuilding, semi-naive evaluation, and implemented case studies. Its floating-point case study specifically addresses previously unsound rewrites. The first-party repository was also inspected as an implementation lead, not run or audited.

**First supported domain and oracle.** A pure exact arithmetic DSL with explicit division guards, ranges, and units. An independent polynomial/rational identity checker on a bounded expression language can check the derivation; legal effects and unknown software operations are not assumed algebraically interchangeable.

**Hardness and failures.** Saturation can grow without bound, extraction can be difficult, and the cost model can optimize the wrong resource. Algebraic reassociation can be invalid for floating point, overflow, exceptions, information timing, or side effects. A proof of mathematical equality is not proof of operational equivalence under those semantics.

**Independence and potential consequence.** Compresses programme syntax/search rather than uncertain worlds or dynamical state. It can reduce synthesis or optimization work. It supplies no new mandate, operator meaning, authority, or measured business throughput.

**Selection:** reject as a new Z boundary; equip the comparator with it where appropriate.

### C9. Coherent quantum expectation estimation

**Single output and boundary.** One expectation estimate with a justified error/confidence envelope, computed using coherent reversible access to a specified distribution and payoff.

**Mechanism.** Ideal amplitude estimation starts from an operation such as

$$
\mathcal A|0\rangle
=\sqrt{1-a}\,|\psi_0\rangle|0\rangle
+\sqrt a\,|\psi_1\rangle|1\rangle
$$

and estimates $a$ through coherent repeated applications and inverses. In the ideal bounded-oracle setting, the accuracy dependence can improve from classical Monte Carlo's $O(\epsilon^{-2})$ sample count to order $O(\epsilon^{-1})$ coherent queries, apart from confidence factors.

**Completed baseline.** Y5 can discover or prove quantum algorithms; the hardware and coherent input interface are the genuinely different physical resource. If matched resources permit that interface to the candidate, the equipped comparator receives the same allowed resource. If they do not, the candidate has changed the comparison.

**Exact witness and equipped path.** Ideal quantum-versus-classical black-box expectation estimation has an oracle-model distinction. It is not a witness for live business observations: a provider API, customer action, or physical experiment cannot simply be queried coherently and reversed. With a known simulated distribution, classical variance reduction, quasi-Monte Carlo, analytical reductions, and surrogate methods remain comparator equipment. **No strict total-resource business witness.**

**Primary sources and important current correction.** The 2021-05-25 threshold analysis [P17] accounts for loading, arithmetic, errors, and example derivative resources; its approximately 8,000 logical qubits and 54-million T-depth are that paper's estimates, not available resources or measurements in this project.

The **2026-05-03** version of Chen, Li, and Neufeld [P18] is especially instructive. Its rigorous multidimensional Black–Scholes analysis does **not** give an unconditional full quadratic advantage after construction errors. Its main general bound has $\epsilon^{-3}$ dependence; the stated bounded-payoff circuit-application improvement is $O(\epsilon^{-3/2})$, with distribution-loading and other assumptions and substantial dimension dependence. Its numerical implementation is low-dimensional. Citing only ideal amplitude estimation would conceal the actual mechanism costs.

**First supported domain and oracle.** A small bounded-payoff simulated pricing instance with a fully specified distribution, not market truth. Independent exact enumeration or validated numerical integration can check small cases.

**Hardness and failures.** Loading, reversible arithmetic, inverse access, error correction, state preparation, readout, calibration, device occupation, energy, and classical preprocessing must all be charged. Simulation of a quantum circuit is not a quantum throughput result. Model error remains even if the numerical estimate is exact.

**Independence and potential consequence.** This changes physical query access, independently of the five retained hypotheses. It could lower some numerical estimation costs in a future resource regime. No current matched-total-resource 100× route follows.

**Selection:** reject as a present Z choice. No hardware provisioning or execution is authorized.

### C10. Invariant-confluent merge computation

**Single output and boundary.** One merge algebra with a proof that independently valid reachable branches preserve the original invariant when merged.

**Mechanism.** Invariant confluence requires, schematically, for valid branches from a common reachable ancestor,

$$
I(S_1(D))\land I(S_2(D))
\Longrightarrow I(S_1(D)\sqcup S_2(D)).
$$

Under the model of [P16], invariant confluence characterizes when invariant-preserving, available, convergent execution can avoid coordination. This is stronger than mere commutativity of an operator and weaker than arbitrary business consistency.

**Completed baseline.** X3 can install distributed applications, Y1 can learn footprints/composition laws, Y5 can prove merge algorithms, and existing resource/authority owners already decide where coordination is compulsory.

**Exact witness and equipped path.** Union of independently added valid facts can preserve a row-local invariant without a central barrier. But two replicas each reserving one unit of a single remaining capacity unit are locally valid and jointly invalid. The latter is an exact counterexample to coordination removal under the original scarce-resource invariant. Escrow/preallocation can change the execution pattern, but its original reservations, coordination, occupation, and cost must remain charged. The equipped baseline can use the same safe merge or escrow construction. **No strict full-baseline witness.**

**Primary source.** Bailis et al., 2014-02-11 version [P16], supplies the invariant-confluence definition and theorem under explicit transaction, merge, availability, and convergence semantics. It also discusses limited analysis languages, destructive updates, and recency guarantees. Its database performance results are not FINNOR business measurements.

**First supported domain and oracle.** Internal monotone derived facts under already granted read rights, with no irreversible effects or scarce-resource allocation. A small independent reachable-state enumerator can inspect every valid fork/merge pair.

**Hardness and failures.** General invariant analysis is not automatic; common ancestry, merge semantics, deletes, uniqueness, revocation, global quotas, freshness, and external side effects matter. “Eventually consistent” cannot substitute for an original atomic approval or reservation.

**Independence and potential consequence.** This removes some synchronization rather than reducing solver work or observation demand. It can lower internal coordination cost on confluent workloads. Removing mandatory approvals or S5/S6 coordination is not a permissible consequence.

**Selection:** reject as a new Z boundary; safe forms belong in baseline system design.

## 10. Adversarial objections to the retained five

### 10.1 “These are all things a completed Y5 could implement”

This objection largely succeeds against their familiar forms. The research does not establish that a new runtime artifact makes them nonredundant. A useful architectural reorganization is not necessarily a new computational boundary.

The remaining possible claim is a proved or measured *resource separation* under a precisely admitted representation/access model and a strong equipped comparator. None is supplied. The coordinator should not transform OPEN into “designed breakthrough.”

### 10.2 “The five are one generic proof-carrying optimizer”

They would collapse into that forbidden bundle if their outputs were left as generic “certified solutions.” The proposed units are deliberately different:

- Z1: an exact **change law** for a computation.
- Z2: an exact **query representation** over joint support.
- Z3: an **information-stopping condition** for a decision.
- Z4: an **environment/refinement interface**.
- Z5: an **execution-integrity proof**.

Each has an independent fixture with the other four absent. Shared use of a small checker is infrastructure, not an additional breakthrough. If one implementation secretly needs the other four to function, the advertised independence has failed.

### 10.3 “Exponential speedups prove a business multiplier”

They do not. A representation-conditioned exponential difference against enumeration may be real while absent against a specialized comparator. Construction can absorb the saving. The saved work may be a negligible share of the accepted assignment. Producing more candidate policies can even increase review and observation costs.

### 10.4 “A theorem can replace an observation or approval”

Only when the original accepted claim is genuinely computational. A theorem about a model does not establish a provider's state, empirical identification, external effect, consent, or legal interpretation. Neither a succinct proof nor a sufficient statistic grants authority.

### 10.5 “Small exact oracles establish the intended worldwide domain”

They establish only bounded mathematical development claims. A separate implementation can share the same false modeling assumption. Field validation, adversarial held-out populations, and independent evaluator custody remain necessary.

### 10.6 “The new interface can ignore hard-to-model interactions”

Then it changes the problem. Coupled actors, one persistent model, common uncertainty, nonanticipativity, confidential inputs, full contingent deviations, shared capacity, liabilities, timing, and revision dependencies must remain. If preserving them makes the interface or circuit enormous, the correct result is loss of benefit, not selective omission.

## 12. Mandatory bottlenecks and necessary total-resource bounds

### 12.1 Unchanged work imposes a ceiling

Under an additive per-assignment cost model, if fraction $u$ of the comparator's binding total cost is unchanged and the remaining fraction is accelerated by $s$, then

$$
S\le \frac1{u+(1-u)/s}.
$$

Thus an unchanged fraction above 1% rules out 100× in that model even if all other work becomes free. At exactly 1%, reaching 100× would require eliminating the rest entirely. This is a necessary ceiling under stated assumptions, not a measured FINNOR cost split. Parallel pipelines need their own bottleneck analysis rather than mechanically applying a serial latency formula.

### 12.2 Approvals are not computational waste by default

If $H$ mandatory approval-minutes are available and every completed assignment needs at least $h_{\min}>0$ such minutes under the unchanged authority contract, then the number of accepted assignments satisfies

$$
K\le H/h_{\min}.
$$

A proof may make a review easier only if the original approver and acceptance contract legitimately allow it. It cannot auto-approve, broaden a delegation, merge distinct consents, or suppress required human deliberation.

### 12.3 Provider and observation floors remain

Analogously, a provider quota $Q$ and an unavoidable minimum $q_{\min}>0$ calls per accepted assignment give $K\le Q/q_{\min}$. The same applies to independently required observations. Batching is available equally to the equipped comparator only when provider semantics, authority, freshness, and independent acceptance permit it.

Real-world latency, delayed outcome visibility, rate limits, and irrevocable effects do not vanish when internal calculation is instantaneous. Cached data cannot stand in for a required current observation.

### 12.4 Risk and physical resources are not denominators to hide

If original acceptance requires at least $\rho_{\min}>0$ of a specified risk allocation per completed assignment, a fixed compatible risk budget $\bar\rho$ gives the conditional bound $K\le\bar\rho/\rho_{\min}$. This is not a claim that all work has the same risk or that risk is always additive. Correlations, tail loss, common exposures, netting rights, and unknown liabilities require the existing owner's original treatment.

The same principle applies to capital occupation, physical production, staff attention, and counterparty capacity. A relaxed model cannot divide an indivisible approval or duplicate a reserved resource.

More generally, for a resource with a justified positive per-assignment lower bound,

$$
K\le \min_{r:r_{\min}>0}\frac{R_r}{r_{\min}}.
$$

This is only a necessary bound. Dependencies, congestion, correlated failures, and finite observation horizons can make achievable throughput lower.

### 12.5 Computation has irreducible costs too

- Emitting $b$ required output bits cannot generally cost less than writing them.
- A small input change can force a large output change.
- An arbitrary joint query may remain hard after compilation; the circuit can be exponential.
- Incompatible decisions under indistinguishable permitted observations cannot be resolved by representation.
- A globally shared invariant may require global information or coordination.
- A succinct verifier does not make proving free.
- A quantum query bound does not include free coherent loading or extra hardware.

These are reasons to demand a narrow admitted structural domain, not reasons to lower the 100× target.

## 13. Finite falsification and qualification plan, not executed

The following is a **proposed development protocol**, not inherited gate text, a product implementation plan, or a record of passing tests. Failure modes above are specified before any implementation. The purpose is to kill false boundary claims cheaply before a coordinator authorizes engineering.

### 13.1 Bounded independent mathematical checks

| Hypothesis | Finite development inputs | Independent oracle | Mandatory counterexample |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Z1 | Exact pure programmes over 16, 64, and 256 rows; a frozen sequence including insert, retract, replacement, and branch-changing updates. | Full original snapshot recomputation after every change. | A small input change that alters every output; unsupported recursive zero-delta termination. |
| Z2 | Binary models with 4, 8, 12, and 16 variables; fixed low-width and deliberately coupled variants. | Complete original-assignment enumeration with exact arithmetic. | A constraint destroying the proposed factorization, and a max/sum order swap. |
| Z3 | At most eight retained models, four actions, and two finite observation channels; predeclared tolerances. | Enumerate original values, feasible decisions, and finite observation histories. | Indistinguishable models requiring different accepted decisions; a nuisance variable affecting a hard constraint. |
| Z4 | Two, three, and four bounded components with all original bounded interleavings enumerated. | Independent global transition product plus a separately checked composition theorem. | Hidden shared capacity, unjustified circular assumptions, and a failure/recovery interleaving. |
| Z5 | A fixed exact bounded-integer transition at 1, 2, 4, and 8 steps, then a separately budgeted scaling instance. | Original interpreter plus independent verification of actual statement bindings. | Wrong initial input, wrong step count, wrong programme, altered output, stale owner basis, and field/integer mismatch. |

The sizes above are finite research fixtures, not supported business-domain limits or performance claims. The oracle must parse original inputs and define expected outcomes independently. Producer code, normalized producer assertions, or a shared expected-answer generator cannot be the sole oracle.

Keep every failed attempt. This report itself records no such executed capability run.

### 13.2 Separate gates

1. **Source and claim integrity:** identify the original capability and source premises; distinguish existing technique from stronger hypothesis.
2. **Strict novelty/separation:** survive the equipped-comparator defeat in the relevant dossier. If not, classify as baseline improvement and stop calling it a Z boundary.
3. **Mathematical development:** pass the frozen small positive and adversarial cases with an independent tractable oracle; check a general theorem only where actually supplied.
4. **Engineering E2E:** if separately authorized later, exercise real original-owner intake, producer, separate checker, native consumer, invalidation, recovery, and original fallback, with a reconstructible evidence artifact. No unit-test substitute for that path is proposed.

6. **Operational comparative gain:** qualify full equipped comparators, freeze assignments/resources/authority/analysis, and evaluate the 100× requirement. Include standalone, add-one, leave-one-out, and all-five-disabled results without multiplying ratios.
