### 6.3 Exact first incentive concept

Coalitions commit their deviation **ex ante**, before the specified private-state draw; their plans can condition on every subsequently lawful local observation/message. This does not establish interim or recursively coalition-proof sequential equilibrium, arbitrary later coalition formation, or the stronger Abraham et al. k-resilience.

For every admitted original model m, coalition C and all original lawful pure joint contingent plans $d\in D_C(m)$, derive:

$$
g_{i,m}(d)=E_m[u_i(d,\sigma_{-C})]-E_m[u_i(\sigma)].
$$

Exclude every lawful correlated mixture p with all member gains nonnegative and at least one strictly positive:

$$
p\ge0,\quad {\bf1}^Tp=1,\quad
G_mp\ge0,\quad {\bf1}^TG_mp>0.
$$

The last expression is a feasibility device over normalized utilities, never aggregated cash or owner value. Preserve original scales/units, outside options, legal transfers and budgets.

The exact rational LP maximizes the last expression subject to the first three constraints. A no-profit dual witness supplies λ_i≥0 and ν≤0 satisfying:

$$
\forall d\in D_C(m):
\quad \nu\ge\sum_{i\in C}(1+\lambda_i)g_{i,m}(d).
$$

The independent checker reconstructs **complete lawful plans** and verifies every inequality. A profitable mixture is an exact counterexample. Approximate LP tolerances, sampled plans and heuristic best response do not certify absence.
