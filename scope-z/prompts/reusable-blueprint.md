## Body grammar

Each active body starts with one `/goal` and has exactly these five headings:

1. OUTCOME AND COMPLETION PREDICATE
2. VERIFIED STARTING POINT AND INTEGRATION BOUNDARIES
3. IMPLEMENTATION FREEDOM AND REQUIRED ENGINEERING METHODS
4. ACCEPTANCE EVIDENCE
5. EXECUTION CONTROL, FINISH DISCIPLINE AND HANDOFF

Section 1 fixes one principal output, original quantifiers, a finite support
profile and falsifiers. Section 2 identifies the source cut, actual owner
consumer, all owned leaves, shared patches and missing sources without
pretending proposed adapters exist. Section 3 adopts the full pinned methods
under current model and authority constraints. Section 4 contains explicit
A–J obligations for the particular phase, not ten generic assurances.
Section 5 gives finite execution, separate result states, reconstruction,
costs, next-context delivery and the seven exact final labels.

No active phase fields remain to fill in. Runtime observations are deliberately
NOT_RUN or UNKNOWN rather than invented. Concrete future observations must be
new immutable evidence, not edits that rewrite an earlier seed.

containing request and fragments, all 20 sealed source members, the full

Quoted `/goal`, old model names, foreign subagent/commit/PR instructions,

## Execution, sealing and reconstruction

The current authoring chat does not launch, provision, commit, push, post,
merge, deploy or edit product/controller files. A future phase executes only
after a real dedicated Computer, exact-model receipt, immutable execution-pack
seal and phase-specific launch snapshot are supplied under actual authority.
The controller is owned elsewhere; this pack provides inputs and interfaces,
not another controller.

Use `assemble.py capture`, `self-check`, `build`, and `check` as documented.
Only `capture` needs original read captures. Rebuilding the complete vendored
pack is offline and deterministic. An authoring payload digest is not a
coordinator execution-pack lock. The index and receipt avoid self-reference;
the coordinator seals their final digests externally with its controller and
launch instructions.

Reconstruction records source/base/head/tree, changed-file/interface manifest,
toolchain/dependency/native asset versions, isolated install/build steps,
finite tests and retained failures, currentness/integration requests, all
meter gaps and remaining obligations. Never infer dedicated delivery,
ACKNOWLEDGED_READ, qualification or a 100× result from successful assembly.
