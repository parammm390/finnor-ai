## Preregistered implementation domain and failure model

S7 qualifies the incremental net owner wealth of complete contingent programmes
against registered business-as-usual. The statistical implementation domain is a
fixed finite weighted eligible population of independently assigned interference
clusters, with prospective S2-owned categorical assignment probabilities, bounded
potential owner wealth and no interference between registered clusters. Within
cluster interactions, noncompliance, human overrides, unsuccessful delivery and
stopping belong to the complete regime and stay in the assignment denominator.
This is no transport or per-action credit theorem. Unsupported observational,
unknown assignment, unidentified interference, future-outcome-informed revisions
and unsupported adaptive assignment remain unidentified or explicitly bounded.
Missing money, costs, liabilities, residual marks and follow-up remain missing.

The inference target is simultaneous 95% coverage of every registered contrast
and finite registered look, conditional on its design and bounded support. Use
exact decimal/rational owner bookkeeping and conservative interval arithmetic;
statistical numerical conversion carries outward numerical uncertainty. Freeze
population, cluster mapping, support, contrast family, stopping/looks, assignment
law, horizons, cost coverage, discount/carry, valuation protocol and benchmark
requirements before assignment or access to scored outcomes. Independent design
support and preregistered power evidence are distinct from estimator precision.
Source requirements and authority verification are recorded in the component
audits before source implementation.

Owner accounting follows the frozen equation: dated discounted net realized
owner cash minus additional capital, plus discounted independently bounded
residual equity minus extra uncovered owner liabilities, minus complete costs
deducted once, minus matched opening wealth. Every item has one canonical identity
and destination across programmes and owner boundaries. Ownership/waterfalls,
currency, dates, valuation debt adjustments, internal transfers, retained cash,
funded distributions, embedded costs and overlapping programme totals must reconcile.
Independent valuers are preregistered, authorized issuers; a FINNOR model cannot
self-certify H2. H0/H1 are retained with their actual supported claims. The primary
system endpoint remains 36 months and 90 days closed-loop operation.

Credible failures: post-outcome question editing; forged/cross-tenant references;
substituted probabilities or cohorts; dropping execution failures; confounded
outcomes; treating dependent decisions as IID; informative missingness; silently
zero costs; retained/distributed or debt-funded cash counted twice; residual
self-certification; stale independent evidence; unsigned owner corrections;
changed idempotent retries; concurrent duplicate ingestion; crash before receipt;
late source corrections erasing history; false H2 labels; weakest-baseline choice;
negligible denominators; incompatible benchmark versions and repeated looks.

Runtime budgets initially fixed to at most 4,096 eligible clusters, 32 registered
arms, 32 registered looks, 65,536 source/accounting items per assessment and 8 MiB
canonical records, bounded API ingestion and bounded source read cuts. Actual
implementation schemas may use stricter operational limits, documented before
their first scored use. These local budgets do not certify the system reach gate.
Development human/compute/data costs are unmetered unless measured evidence
supports them and cannot be assumed free or amortized over hypothetical missions.

No coverage deletion. New checks use real semantic owners, ordinary-role SQL,
authenticated handlers and separate-process S6 enforcement where available.
Generated observations never supply the production attribution/reconciliation or
maturity result. Each run records source hashes, versions, input cuts, seeds,
oracle truth, observed results, costs/gaps and rerun commands. Test selections,
retention and independent review follow the full OpenClaw test-audit skill.
