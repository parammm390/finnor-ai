1.2 One consequential firm-level case
A PE firm maintains transaction readiness, financing capacity and institutional liquidity under a standing mandate. Lenders, co-investors, administrators and LP-related counterparties have independently controlled data, rights and changing alternatives.

The owner asks FINNOR to find and advance materially better permissible arrangements while preserving the firm's risk, liquidity, staffing, compensation and employee-benefit conditions.

FINNOR can already source opportunities, obtain primary evidence, prepare/close authorized transactions, secure verified capital commitments and operate its software through V/X. Y must add something beyond those routes.

The new chain is:
1. Construct a candidate economic operator family whose meanings are not already supplied by the admitted programme grammar.
2. Establish which strategic responses to its use are supported, which are ambiguous, and how one party's choices affect others.
3. Synthesize the permitted disclosure, negotiation and commitment history.
4. Evaluate joint constraints and select a qualified programme without unauthorized raw-data pooling.
5. Discover and prove a reusable reduction or algorithm for a recurring problem family.
S4 selects the programme. S5 checks feasibility and reservations. Each real effect remains under S6. Actual counterparty acceptance, signatures, conditions and settlement remain independently observed V/S6 outcomes. S7 measures net owner value.

A conditional loan, staged consideration, shared collateral, rollover, cross-default or ordinary co-investment is not automatically a new Y1 action. V already includes those classes. The action-theory separation must be established against the actual baseline.
1.3 Architectural selection
Two whole-shape designs were examined.

The selected design changes economic construction: action meaning, strategic response, interaction history, private computational access and general algorithm knowledge.

The alternative concentrated on probability circuits, distribution ambiguity, time-uniform evidence, stochastic path certificates and continuous stopping. Those are useful candidate techniques where their assumptions fit. They chiefly enlarge reasoning within an existing economic action space.

This PRD selects economic construction because the requested outcome concerns newly attainable business results as well as computational efficiency. The alternative techniques may be implementation choices inside an engine; they do not become five additional scopes.

No empirical superiority between these designs is claimed. During implementation, measured baseline limitations must determine whether the selected mechanisms actually create the expected increment.

3.2 Five meanings that cannot be conflated
1. Observed: admissible retained observations support the statement.
2. Model-relative: a statement follows from an explicitly supplied/inferred model under listed assumptions.
3. Empirically qualified: protected independent observations support the claimed performance in a stated population.
4. Formally checked: the declared theorem holds under its exact specification and accepted assumptions.
5. Externally effective: the required authorized real-world acceptance, conditions and effects are independently established.
A machine-checked theorem does not establish a counterparty valuation. A signature authenticates an assertion; it does not prove its financial content. A simulation is not settlement. Legal efficacy is not derived from executable contract syntax.

4.5 Decisive fixtures
Public mechanics fixture: A permitted reservation-right substitution preserves a fixed capacity C. The current right remains active until exact consent and replacement conditions are established. An admitted replacement retires the old right and activates the new one without a state permitting both to consume C.

Rejection, expiry, revoked consent and ambiguous external observation have independently prescribed states. A previously settled external effect cannot be undone by changing an internal right record.

Mutation fixtures introduce a double-active state, erase a pending obligation, move expiry, change currency or treat an unsigned interpretation as acceptance. The compiler/checker path must reject them.

This is a public safety fixture, not evidence of novelty. Existing S5/S6/V capabilities may already express it.

Protected separation fixture: An independently administered problem family requires an economic semantic theory meeting withheld interpretation/trace checks. Before either actor runs, the oracle fixes the property contract, admitted comparison closure, equivalence relation and eligible construction resources. It does not supply the target semantic body to either actor.

An expressiveness certificate must quantify over every eligible programme in the frozen bounded admitted closure: no such programme has an observationally equivalent denotation within the registered parameter/trace profile. Use an independently checked certificate or complete search. A separating trace against one attempted baseline programme is insufficient. This is a claim about that admitted closure, never about arbitrary-code impossibility.

Represent actor-local observations, economic objectives/preferences as hypotheses, permitted actions, messages and current commitments. Retain which state is actually observed and which is latent.

A supported computation may take the form:
a_i,t ~ response_i(local_information_i,t,
                   public_rule_t, commitments_t, private_state_i,t)

s_t+1 ~ transition(s_t, a_1,t, ..., a_n,t, common_regime_t)
The implementation may use agent-based dynamics, structural econometrics, probabilistic programmes, differentiable games or other qualified techniques. A roleplay transcript is not a calibrated economic transition model.

Distinguish observed predictive fit from causal policy-response identification. Use S2/S3 to establish permitted interventions and identification assumptions. If different models fit the same evidence but predict different policy responses, retain their disagreement.

Compute the joint response under the declared behavioral/equilibrium basis. A solver converging to one fixed point does not establish uniqueness, stability or relevance to actual parties.

Evaluate withheld policies, strategic spillovers and regime changes. Test which assumptions are needed for transport. Calibrate uncertainty and apply the original decision-loss rules.

Publish model-relative predictions separately from qualified field predictions. Observed counterparty interest or funding remains a direct V/S6 fact, not a predicted state.
5.4 Qualification profile

Keep synthetic, controlled human-participant and consented field evidence separate. A synthetic actor that follows its programmed utility is not proof that a real lender has that utility.
5.5 Decisive fixture
Two model families match all observations under the incumbent financing rule. Under a withheld rule, one predicts complementary counterparty participation and the other predicts crowd-out.

FINNOR initially must retain both. A permitted intervention can distinguish them only if the registered identification conditions hold. A forbidden or uninformative intervention cannot resolve the ambiguity.

After acquiring admissible evidence, the model predicts a fresh interaction involving changed outside options and another actor's response. Independent observations check the joint prediction and recommended programme.

A later regime removes the transport basis. FINNOR invalidates that claim and retains actual commitments already created. It cannot keep the optimistic model because the recommended transaction would otherwise lose feasibility.

6.3 Required computation
Build the supported extensive-form interaction from admitted operators, model sets, observation rights and the mandate.

Represent each party's information set. A participant cannot condition an action on another party's private value or its own future observation. The planner cannot use oracle state to choose a current branch.

Search communication, disclosure and commitment policies. Use suitable game/optimization techniques, contingent-policy search or certified relaxation within the chosen profile.

Evaluate full permitted deviation strategies, not only one report changed while all later behavior is held truthful. Check participation, payments, timing, current resources and every relevant continuation.

The first profile uses model-relative sequential equilibrium in finite extensive-form games with perfect recall. Bind the prescribed participant strategies, independently admitted common prior, state/signal laws, information partitions, utilities and outside options. The protocol fixes disclosure and commitment rules; it cannot choose convenient participant beliefs independently of those rules.

For every admitted model, the independent checker verifies exact sequential rationality: at each supported participant information set, the prescribed continuation has zero profitable gain from any permitted unilateral contingent continuation deviation. It also verifies belief consistency with the prior, chance laws and protocol. At positive-probability information sets, beliefs follow Bayes' rule. At off-path information sets possible under the fixed chance support, supply an independently checked consistency certificate establishing the assessment as a limit of completely mixed participant-strategy assessments. Histories impossible under the admitted chance law are explicitly outside that model's domain. Missing off-path support or an unchecked consistency claim yields UNKNOWN.

One common sequence of completely mixed joint strategy profiles must induce the entire belief assessment. Separate convenient sequences for different participants or information sets do not qualify.

Full permitted continuation strategies, including coordinated early misreports and later choices, remain in the deviation family. This guarantee is relative to the declared models and unilateral deviations; it does not establish coalition resistance or predict actual counterparties will comply. A different incentive concept needs its own separately labeled qualification.

If randomness is used, bind its probability grid, provenance and financial semantics. A randomized policy is outside R3's stated deterministic profile; it still needs its own qualification and the same hard constraints.

Retain valid objective bounds and witnesses. A heuristic best response cannot certify absence of a profitable deviation. A sampled history cannot certify the entire supported strategy space.

Compile the accepted interaction into existing participant-local Work/policy/effect interfaces. Obtain exact current consent to the same terms revision. Actual behavior that leaves the model domain invalidates the incentive claim.

First development bounds are two parties, two private-state values, three decision dates and at most sixteen information sets per party.

8.5 Public exact fixture
Use an explicitly synthetic time-homogeneous Markov chain with states 0 through N. States 0 and N are absorbing. Conditional on an interior state and the prior history, the next transition moves upward with probability 1/3 and downward with probability 2/3.

The public reference asks for the probability of reaching N before 0 from state 1, over arbitrarily many events. For N >= 1:
p_N = 1 / (2^N - 1)

h_N(i) = (2^i - 1) / (2^N - 1)
h_N(0) = 0
h_N(N) = 1
h_N(i) = (1/3) h_N(i+1) + (2/3) h_N(i-1)
At N = 8, the exact answer is 1/255.

The full formal claim requires the absorbing-process and bounded-martingale reasoning, not just checking seven equalities at N = 8. A twelve-period simulation does not establish the arbitrary-horizon probability.

This is a public mechanics fixture with a supplied answer. It does not count as a protected discovery or real financial risk model. Actual LP remittance, lender behavior or money exposure does not inherit this random-walk assumption.
