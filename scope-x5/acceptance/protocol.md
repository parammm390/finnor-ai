# Registered public development protocol

This is a finite engineering protocol, not protected X5 qualification. Freeze
candidate, procedure, original/new checkpoints, runtime/source/schema,
partition and oracle digests before fresh evaluation. Candidate failures remain
visible. Protected cases are not available and are not queried.

## Populations and feedback

Training uses at least eight independently approved native responsibility
records, including delayed/known/current observations, with explicit training
consent. Preserve the complete bounded record histories and refusals. Do not
copy old record/tenant IDs, private text, exact amounts or grants into the policy
or features. Use source refs only for provenance, under current authorization.

Transfer uses fresh records and a differently structured/renamed application.
Retention uses held-out fresh records in the original application. Changed
consistency and invalid-domain strata are reported separately, not removed from
the denominator. At least 16 paired fresh supported assignments and eight
retained assignments are the finite development target; this does not establish
population power, 95% transfer qualification or protected magnitude.

Pair `COMPILED_ORIGINAL` and `COMPILED_TRAINED` on equivalent pre-action native
states with identical procedure, tools, data, context, authority and remaining
allocation. Preserve original checkpoint for direct reruns. Alternate order.
Also execute `NATIVE_EXACT` (strongest available local lawful interpreter) and
`COMPILED_NO_NEURAL`. Do not present untrained weights as the strongest incumbent.
Procedure evidence and parameter evidence receive separate dispositions.

Task success requires actual required reconciliation followed by current,
different-principal approval, before original deadline, with exact business
quantities and no authority/effect violations. The independent oracle inspects
SQL/events, executed checkpoint identity, guard/fallback and resulting record.
Loss improvement and controller class accuracy are supplemental only.
An old checkpoint that waits rather than executes has not completed the task.

Public development evaluation can report aggregate/literal outcomes. It cannot
be relabeled as one-use hidden protected transfer or independent key custody.
No post-result endpoint switch, pruning or silent profile shrink is allowed.

## Mandatory proofs

1. Signed login, original Work/input, native experience, consent and complete
   acceptance resolution. Forged labels/references, poisoned missing-reconcile
   success, future cut, rights revocation and corrected histories refuse.
2. Authenticated submit enters canonical durable queue; ordinary physical
   worker claims it; scrubbed separate child compiles/trains; immutable safe
   JSON original/new checkpoints and resource receipts persist.
3. Fresh guarded native uses bind input/record/application/role/source/runtime/
   policy/checkpoint and original S5 deadline/reservation. Independent approval
   observes actual reconciliation. Invalid observations really wait/fallback.
4. Original/new fixed-surroundings comparison; exact native and neural-disabled
   comparisons; transfer and retained-domain strata; harmful/critical-regression
   evaluation withheld rather than promoted.
5. Typed S8 propose/register/evaluate/admit/current/use/revoke/restart operates
   through existing signed journal. Wrong artifact, missing proof, shared
   proposer/evaluator, stale sources/rights, substituted checkpoint and
   expired evaluator refuse. Disposable authority is labeled.
6. Two competing Works, duplicates, cancellation, interrupted child/evaluation/
   publication, expired/stale delivery, deadline/attempt/storage/use exhaustion,
   restart and earlier-revision compatibility. Read actual queue/delivery/SQL/
   process state before retry after a failure. No allocation replenishment.
7. Mounted maintained Work shows evidence, checkpoint, domain/guard, training/
   evaluation, ablations, transfer/retention, admission/revocation, uses/fallback,
   costs and unresolved gaps. Drive real accessible controls, reload, selection/
   root/principal/token changes, logout/sign-in and delayed-response fences.
8. Final emitted API/worker/training/checker/consumer assets, migration upgrade/
   replay/in-flight, generated, backend/frontend types, scoped lint, security
   audit and standalone source/lock/checkpoint reconstruction parity.

## Resource and meter basis

Training is serial, max 30 seconds, 256 MiB V8 heap, 64 KiB logs and 4 MiB IPC.
Original Work bounds remain 3,600 seconds, eight attempts, 256 uses and 16 MiB
retained JSON. Verification commands may run at most 900 seconds individually
unless their registered command explicitly states a different bounded limit.
Do not overlap builds/training/live stacks except the two-Work test.

Retain wall time, process CPU and peak RSS when observable, payload/storage
bytes, physical job/delivery identity and all failed attempts. Sampled host
memory is not exact aggregate simultaneous peak. Unknown host/provider money,
human engineering/supervision/rescue, storage rent and hidden reasoning tokens
stay UNKNOWN with reasons. Zero observed provider calls is not a 10x reasoning
claim. Freeze horizon at 32 actual eligible development assignments before
amortization; no speculative infinite demand. Complete economics stays
inconclusive when any required category is unknown.

## Unchanged external gates

The original three sequences, ten cycles each, 1,000 paired fresh assignments
per sequence, independent calibration/custody, simultaneous 95% endpoints,
95% completion, -0.02 retention floor, repeated material promotions, 10x
reasoning and complete lifecycle economics remain unchanged. Independent S8
admission, production activation, all-engine operational 100x, B_V/B_ref and
mature financial qualification are separate. Preparation packets are NOT_RUN.
