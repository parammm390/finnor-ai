## Frozen development domain

`PE_FIRM_DELAYED_RECONCILIATION_V1` covers firm-operation responsibilities in
the installed X3 typed application family. It does not add portfolio-company
operation. It supports one entity workflow with an initial state, a mandatory
exact dimensional reconciliation transition and a subsequent independent
approval transition. Identifiers, screens, canonical role names and field names
are fresh bindings. Training may not transfer business values or old grants.

The compiler resolves actual installed specification, Work/input, complete
record command history, successful reconciliation and separate-principal
approval. It checks the mandatory method against the original specification.
Approval is operational acceptance of that bounded contract, never a causal
action reward, protected population result or mature financial outcome.
Refused commands, failures, later edits/corrections and invalidation remain
visible. A caller cannot submit a positive success label or fabricated receipt.

Training requires an explicit current owner consent record with train/reuse
purpose, institution/application scope, knowledge cut, permitted transfer,
expiry and revocation policy. Ordinary application read permission is not
training permission. Revocation blocks use; removing a row is not unlearning.

Transfer state contains boolean structural properties only: known quantities,
dimensional agreement, current observations, pending effects and workflow stage.
The data-only procedure has observe/classify/guard/dispatch-or-wait/check
instructions, explicit operation signatures, postcondition/approval floors,
failure monitor, original resource bounds and dependencies. It cannot execute
guest code, open files, use credentials, spawn processes or dispatch external
effects. Unsupported semantics use a current native interpreter fallback or a
specific WAIT/BLOCKED/STOP disposition, never a flag pretending to be fallback.

At most 256 accepted training records and 4,096 structural samples, 64 selected
history events per record, 4 classes and 8 features. Architecture is a small
multinomial linear neural controller trained by bounded full-batch gradient
descent. No external weights, network, GPU or training library is required.
The original deterministic untrained checkpoint is preserved. It is a parameter
ablation, not an equipped incumbent and never grounds a protected ratio claim.
Training configuration is fixed before scored development evaluation.

Per original compilation Work: at most 8 training attempts, 256 uses, 3,600
seconds elapsed, 16 MiB cumulative retained payload and a 30-second physical
training process with 256 MiB V8 heap and bounded IPC/output. One training child
at a time through the canonical compute lease. API bodies are at most 128 KiB;
responses at most 4 MiB. All retries/fallback/evaluation share original counters
and deadlines. No reset on restart, replacement or cancellation.

Development, calibration, training, transfer and retained-domain identities are
distinct; partition manifests bind institution, application semantics/version,
workflow structure, chronology and dependence groups. No protected objects
are supplied to the training child. Training/inference receive only safe
data and fixed trusted modules. Deployment uses the same Node 22 runtime.

These are declared bounds, not capacity or qualification measurements.

## Independent verification and falsifiers

Read `acceptance/failure-model.md` and `acceptance/protocol.md` before critical
checker code. Actual signed-user HTTP, durable jobs, separate physical
training/worker processes, ordinary SQL roles and independent literal outcomes
are the primary proof. No post-code unit tests are added. Tests do not supply
the receipt or success row that the product must produce.

The decisive fixture delays an export, completes canonical reconciliation and
obtains a different principal's acceptance. FINNOR selects that native history,
compiles and trains. Fresh applications bind different roles, fields, states
and records, including an altered consistency condition. That condition is
preserved by the existing interpreter or causes fallback/refusal. An adversarial
approval trace omitting reconciliation is excluded before positive training.

Falsifiers include memorized record IDs, missing actual parameter-dependent
task behavior, dropped reconciliation, stale grants, hidden truth access,
unmetered descendant retries, substituted loaded checkpoint, self-admission,
new-parameter resumption of an old call, or expensive repair erasing economics.

The development diagnostic runs the strongest available native interpreter,
compiled policy with original/new parameters and neural-disabled surroundings.
Hold tools, source access, authority, provider, context, inputs and deadlines
fixed for parameter comparisons. Report no-gain and critical regressions.
No provider reasoning usage is inferred from absent calls or unavailable meters.
