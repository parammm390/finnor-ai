# Mounted review predicates, before repairs

1. Begin an actual submit/lifecycle request, then change the read/focus epoch.
   Its pending-operation cleanup must still release controls for the same
   identity. An old identity must not release a newer identity's request.
2. After actual accepted values are visible, dispatch the browser's real offline
   transition without a synthetic focus event. Clear evidence immediately,
   advance the read epoch and refuse any pre-disconnect response. The actual
   online transition must reconstruct through authenticated current owners.
3. If capsule B publishes between P2-plan and native-currentness projections,
   do not combine plan A and native reader B as current evidence. Require equal
   immutable capsule digests, search and original Work revision, including the
   absence/currentness cases. A mismatched pair must clear and retry.
4. A killed or timed-out browser driver cannot execute its own finally blocks.
   The owning wrapper must terminate its own driver process group and restore
   assigned-pane offline emulation itself, without closing/reloading Desktop.
5. Enforce the registered prewarm180-second bound on successes too. Each HTTP
   timeout must fit the remaining allowance, and success after expiry fails.

These are additional owning browser/runner requirements. They do not replace
the unchanged twelve journey predicates or independent native continuation
oracle. Retain every failed attempt and all costs; do not extend business time.
