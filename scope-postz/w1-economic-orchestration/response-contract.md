# Pre-code measured-response contract

The failure model predates this estimator. These predicates and the response proof driver precede its implementation.

## Meaning and ownership

Fit immutable task-conditioned **joint treatment** response from native measured observations. A treatment reference binds actual methods, topology, context partition and independent checking. There is no additive per-agent benefit, monotone-effort assumption, model-vote independence or invented ROI. The original outcome's acceptance remains outside this estimator.

A native-resolved dataset binds tenant/principal/purpose, population, original criterion, feature/structure references, source/model/provider/method/price/checker/runtime revisions, S2 assignment protocol and knowledge cut. A sample binds original Work, independently declared unit/lineage cluster, trace, assignment, lawful evidence, completion/censoring/rescue, actual latency/CPU/peak memory/human effort and known monetary accounting or explicit UNKNOWN. Observations are not authority or protected admission.

Only TRAIN and CALIBRATION samples enter the fitting boundary. HELD_OUT and protected/future observations are refused, not accepted and later ignored. An independent unit cannot cross splits. Original sample identities are immutable and unique; retrying an observation cannot increase sample size. The update API appends to an original dataset, retaining failed/censored work and the previous learned-state identity.

## Estimation and limitations

Fit each exact feature/structure plus full joint treatment separately. Repeated Works sharing one premise/independent unit do not become independent confidence: aggregate within the declared unit, then average units. Censored or human-rescued observations remain in the record and do not count as independently accepted successes.

Training estimates use only training observations. Calibration readback is separate and cannot improve the fitted mean. Unknown dollars propagate to UNKNOWN complete cost. Known costs can be averaged only for one original currency/unit. No money/seconds conversion is invented. Latency includes all measured treatment work; controller/checker/native overhead must also be metered by the owning runtime.

For a preregistered randomized independent-unit design only, return conservative simultaneous Hoeffding acceptance bounds over the actual fitted treatment count, explicitly conditional on the supplied design/independence assumptions. Logged/selection-confounded observations retain `[0,1]` bounds and ASSOCIATIONAL_ONLY status. None is a protected empirical qualification or causal transport guarantee. Missing combinations are UNKNOWN, not interpolated benefits.

Applicability requires current lawful context, feature/structure, population and exact source/model/provider/method/price/checker/runtime/assignment revisions before a consumer uses a fitted estimate. A revision change invalidates only the dependent estimate. No prior cost, accepted result or original grant is erased.

## Independent development observations

The preregistered driver supplies literal observed outcomes and exact expectations: two units with three shared-premise samples yield two independent units, not three; TRAIN mean acceptance is exactly 1/2; a held-out entry is rejected at the boundary; calibration cannot change that mean; one unknown monetary observation makes complete mean cost UNKNOWN; append changes the fitted identity without changing old samples; duplicate/conflicting identities fail; price drift refuses applicability; an unseen joint topology stays UNKNOWN; logged observations stay associational. Development data is labeled synthetic, never a native or protected evidence claim.
