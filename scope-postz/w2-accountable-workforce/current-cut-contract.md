## Local recovery design, before the receipt adapter

The recovered source is the 03:00 UTC snapshot on 31 October 2026. Later cloud
changes are unavailable. The existing compensation campaign cannot run locally
at the observed 1.1 GiB free disk without violating its original disk floor.
Its pre-fix receipt assertion is therefore NOT_RUN on this local recovery cut.
Source inspection confirms the unconditional `UNAVAILABLE` projection. That is
not a substitute for a demonstrated failing owning campaign.

The data shape is one append-only S5 accounting link from an accepted W2 promise
to the existing closing payment consumption, original effect, signed receiving
fact and independent check. A current check determines whether the promise is
settled now. Historical paid cash remains spent after a later correction.

Two designs were considered in a sequential self-review. A new W2 consumption
would duplicate the original payment and split financial ownership. The chosen
design keeps the original closing consumption and records only an accounting
link in the existing S5 history. The complete snapshot subtracts that linked
coordinate from the overlapping W2 hold while retaining the original financial
reservation. The W2 carve reports that same coordinate as spent and cannot
reuse it. No new payment request, rail, privilege, grant or credential is added.

The existing closing check client records the link only after its independent
receipt is durable and current, using the existing S5 portfolio transaction.
Readback verifies the link against its immutable preimages. Receipt currentness
uses the closing owner's full material cut and publication-cut rule. A receipt
correction can reopen compensation but cannot erase spend or cause a retry.
