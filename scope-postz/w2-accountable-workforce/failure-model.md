# Failure-first W2 contract

This specification predates critical W2 compiler/checker implementation. It is development evidence, not protected custody or independent domain admission. Expected observations come from original canonical records, never from producer summaries.

## Observable predicates

1. Identity. Every binding resolves to a current canonical tenant-scoped actor; names, personas and claims do not create identities.
2. Competence. Every required skill resolves to evidence with scope, expiry and uncertainty; unsupported qualifications cannot become suitable.
3. Availability. Capacity depends on real availability/agreement and canonical shared reservations; missing evidence remains UNKNOWN.
4. Original meaning. Every assignment retains the exact original root, task, independent criterion, unit/calendar and original deadline.
5. Exact terms. Acceptance resolves to the actual authorized recipient disposition and immutable terms revision, not delivery acknowledgment or silence.
6. Authentication. Wrong actor, forged callback, cross-tenant submission and changed-principal delivery cannot mutate accepted state.
7. Internal assignment. Legitimate institutional assignment is distinguishable from voluntary external consent and requires its actual owner authority.
8. Attenuation. Every edge is bounded by parent and recipient grants, purpose, information, terms and time at actual use.
9. Disclosure. A child receives only authorized task context, not parent credentials, another actor's private data or hidden checker state.
10. Conservation. For every real resource coordinate, spent plus live reserved plus unresolved exposure does not exceed the original root grant. A carved reservation is counted once on the active frontier.
11. Ancestry. Resource ancestry is acyclic and rooted even when reciprocal operational requests form cycles.
12. Whole graph. Every active node, edge, leaf and possible effect is checked. A parent cannot close while descendants, labor or exposure remain open.
13. Progress. Offer, acceptance, arrival, work, delivery, verification, dispute and external completion remain different observations.
14. Refusal and return. Decline, silence/expiry, block and lawful return preserve the root and incur bounded, metered follow-up.
15. Reassignment. Before replacement, original S6/provider state is reconciled. MAY_HAVE_LEFT or UNKNOWN cannot authorize a new physical attempt.
16. Cancellation. Requested cancellation is not confirmed release; accepted labor, compensation and residual exposure survive local cancellation.
17. Currentness. Qualification, availability, rights, authority, terms, consent, task, resource and method changes fence the smallest sound dependent closure at each trust/time boundary.
18. Fault recovery. Duplicate delivery, stale claim, SIGKILL and restart cannot reset a root, grant, deadline, effect identity or accepted terms.
19. Useful completion. Suitable positive work reaches a real executor and independent original acceptance; reject-everything and canned-actor behavior fail.
20. Bounds. The entire declared 128-actor/512-live-assignment/depth-eight representation is exercised without silent truncation. Over-profile input is explicitly unsupported.
21. Persistence and product. Ordinary authenticated Work reaches durable queue/claim/fence, compiler, independent checking, native owner consumption, persistence and mounted controls. A JSON export or endpoint 200 is insufficient.
22. Privacy lifecycle. Reload, Work/company/principal switch, logout, refresh, revocation, offline/reconnect and delayed old-principal replies cannot resurrect private workforce data.
23. Metering. Matching, offers, failed/cancelled work, checking, supervision, human time, storage, reconciliation and fallback retain measured values or named UNKNOWN gaps. Unknown is not zero.
24. Human truth. Controlled-service, synthetic, genuine-human and field populations remain separate. No builder/model may supply a person's consent or feedback.

## Decisive adverse boundaries

Mutations must reach the relevant graph, recipient-authentication, current-grant, canonical-capacity or dispatch consumer. Earlier syntax rejection does not prove those predicates. Each run retains original inputs, independent expected observation, reached boundary, source/runtime identity, actual result, duration, failures, cleanup and rerun command.

At least sixteen genuinely distinct structural families with eight fresh cases per family must be preregistered for protected qualification. Builder-authored development cases do not satisfy protected custody. Per-family critical floors and the original useful-completion estimator remain unchanged.

## Physical and human proof

Physical fault injection kills only task-owned controlled processes after durable acceptance and at a meaningful publication/possible-egress boundary. Read back actual queue/claims/fences, S5 frontier, S6 original identity and provider state before any retry.

Genuine-human proof requires an already authorized consenting participant and authenticated actual disposition/feedback through the implemented channel. If unavailable, finish the protocol, UI and drivers, then retain that exact unsatisfied gate without claiming full E2E completion.

## Additional native boundaries, frozen before extension

- A reduced rational must cross domain/S5 codecs exactly. Reject malformed encodings; never round, infer zero, or expose an S5-only encoding to the domain checker.
- Closing a declined or returned attempt cannot satisfy its original deliverable. Work completion needs an independently verified, closed attempt for every original task.

Preserve that released attempt, the original root reservation, and the original deadline. Any physical intent, consumption, live descendant, labor obligation or unresolved effect prevents this shortcut.
- A replacement must actually receive an offer, authenticate exact acceptance, run through the registered executor, and satisfy the original independent criterion. A replacement flag alone is not fallback evidence.
- A terms revision remains inside the original compensation, disclosure, subdelegation and deadline limits, increments the revision, withdraws old acceptance, and requires a fresh exact disposition.

- Outcome experience must be reconstructed by its S8 owner from committed native Work, command, delivery, independent verification and S5/S7 records. Retain failures, unknowns and source class. Store it under S1 without altering qualification, granting training rights, labeling causal rewards or admitting a new worker capability.
- Installed W2 bytes require a distinct W2 manifest and profile. Reuse the checked local packaging machinery, not an X1 qualification label. Refuse ambiguous sibling manifests, changed runtime/source/dependency bytes, external dependency links, a different migration cut or production targets. A cold reconstructed source tree must build without the original checkout.
- Mounted polling is real workload. A read slower than one poll interval must not create overlapping reads, invalidate itself indefinitely or preserve another principal's input. Delay an actual authorized response, change scope or revoke its principal, and verify that releasing the old response cannot restore private content.
- Next may generate its route/root-parameter references from the original `.next/types` or `.next/dev/types` form. Accept only an exact preimage-bound substitution into the selected owned build directory. Preserve the first observed packaging refusal; never permit unrelated source changes to make a built artifact pass.
- Once a backup has a current skill, a later compile may legitimately select it. Authenticate acceptance and canonical revocation against the actual returned assignment recipient, not a fixture's original preferred identity. Retain the selected offer, unchanged exact terms and native failure rows. The physical interruption predicate must still reach actual acceptance, durable intent, SIGKILL, lease quarantine and original read-only recovery; a wrong-principal 404 is an earlier refusal, not that proof.
- The bound campaign's unaffected-branch observation must retain every HTTP 500, even when a worker subsequently closes the leaf. Before a repair, collect bounded, literal-redacted PostgreSQL error statements and a single-flight 250 ms session/blocker trace across that original branch. Distinguish row/mutex wait, slow SQL, client-side checking and host pressure from the resulting TypeError. Assert the actual read status before dereferencing its graph. These are diagnostic additions only: the 10-second database statement timeout, 45-second branch observation, 600-second compile observation, 1,200-second campaign and 128/512/8 population do not change.

- A protected-child refusal includes the actual observation time in its content digest. Retrying the same refused request checks a new cut, without changing its parent programme or creating a child. Retain both exact immutable refusal bodies, not an identity-conflict error hidden by the refusal catch. ## Canonical outbound compensation boundary, before implementation

- An accepted payable retains its exact original tenant, Work, programme,
  assignment, terms digest, recipient/principal, acceptance command and
  disposition, original S5 reservation and one same-currency CASH coordinate.
  Releasing labor, canceling work, an invoice, S7 cost or an inbound customer
  payment cannot settle it or free its unresolved original hold.
- A lawful settlement request must resolve those facts from their actual owners,
  not accept replacement amount/payee/terms/root fields from the caller. Prove
  current authority, recipient isolation, exact retries and changed-request
  refusals at the concrete native consumer, not merely a parser.
- The software path must assemble and reach the actual protected outbound
  financial adapter boundary. An unadmitted boundary preserves reconstructible
  original request/refusal history, OPEN compensation, the original root and
  reservation, and creates neither a physical transfer nor new spending rights.
  A constant UNKNOWN projection without that path is not software completion.
- Only the canonical financial owner's independently resolvable settlement
  observation can close a payable, after exact amount/currency/payee/terms and
  original-obligation joins. An acknowledgment or caller-supplied receipt is not
  settlement. Disposable fixtures must distinguish lawful boundary assembly
  from malformed, stale, foreign, forged and duplicate evidence.
- Controlled fixture observations never authorize real money movement,
  protected admission or genuine-human/field claims. Real settlement remains
  NOT_RUN without the actual authorized financial owner and protected rail.

## Participant validity, before expiry repair

A naturally expired PROPOSED or CONFIRMED existing-contact binding must not
occupy the partial "current binding" uniqueness key forever. An authorized
different proposer must be able to obtain a fresh exact recipient confirmation
after that expiry, without inheriting old consent, exposing the old private
binding or erasing its events. Record expiry as expiry, not a fabricated human
revocation. An unexpired competing binding is a bounded opaque conflict, never
an unhandled PostgreSQL uniqueness error/HTTP 500. Freeze both native expiry
fixtures before changing the consumer or forward-only migration.
