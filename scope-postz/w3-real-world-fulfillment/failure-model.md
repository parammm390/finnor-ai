# W3 failure model and independent observable predicates

This preserves the original pre-code failure model and the current-main
continuation's preregistered contracts. Historical verification is not Linux
evidence. A contract below is not a claim that its proof has passed.

## Failure families

1. A read, casual question or provider message activates procurement without a
   separately rooted original mandate.
2. Tenant, principal, company, Work or site is substituted. Delayed output
   restores a previous principal's private context.
3. Decomposition loses dates, time zones, calendar exclusions, exact quantities,
   units, currency, acceptance or original deadlines.
4. Construction omits a required instance or dependency, truncates the supported
   population, or calls an exhausted search complete or physically infeasible.
5. Availability, qualifications, consent, current grants or actual competence
   are invented, stale or mistaken for independent verification.
6. An invitation, silence, quote or acceptance of different terms becomes a
   current authenticated commitment.
7. Provider assertion, inspection, sensory preference, qualified safety and
   original acceptance collapse into one success flag.
8. Controlled participants become genuine people. Conflicts become consensus,
   or operational disclosure includes unnecessary private data.
9. Readiness, access, materials, logistics, equipment, evaluator capacity,
   handback or billing readiness are assumed from an invitation.
10. Cancellation, a no-show or substitution erases an original failure,
    commitment, cost or unresolved effect.
11. A backup ignores a live primary or UNKNOWN identity, duplicates exposure,
    or receives a fresh business assignment, allocation or deadline.
12. Spent, live reservation and unresolved exposure exceed an original
    same-unit frontier, or incompatible units are added.
13. Death after durable acceptance or possible egress permits a stale
    publication or blind retry with a new consequential identity.
14. Method, checker, runtime, rights, consent, requirements or original-source
    drift crosses submission, claim, publication, use or dispatch.
15. Invalidation discards historical accepted work, unaffected service,
    actual costs, disputes, payment liabilities or exact older terms.
16. An invoice charges unobserved quantity. A lost payment response becomes
    either settlement or permission to pay again.
17. Operational completion closes financial responsibility, or access is
    called retired without authorized original-identity native readback.
18. S7 omits or duplicates provider, material, trial, inspection, logistics,
    cancellation, coordination, recovery, compute, storage or maintenance cost.
19. A row, HTTP200 or mock owner is called integrated native consumption,
    protected admission or physical delivery.
20. Installed assets, interpreters, jobs or migrations differ from checked
    source, or cold reconstruction borrows donor dependencies.
21. A logical control clock becomes real elapsed field service.
22. An unavailable completed-Z baseline is called defeated, or subconditions
    inflate the count of original assignments.

## Independent checker and observation predicates

Derive obligations from the immutable original outcome and canonical owners,
not producer completion flags. Match every required site, local date, window,
time zone, exact quantity, unit, hard criterion and terms digest. Check the
entire finite dependency graph for coverage, uniqueness, existing endpoints
and acyclicity. Do not sample leaves or drop contradictions.

For commitments require authenticated authorship, the same original S6
identity and terms, appropriate current authority and consent, and supported
capacity. A source signature proves authorship, not physical truth. For each
observation independently check subject, purpose, role, qualification, scope,
terms, time, original source, evidence class and consequence.

Hard constraints are conjunctive. Actual safety requires the qualified
process and actual evidence. Genuine sensory evidence requires a real
consenting evaluator. CONTROLLED and REPLAYED reports cannot establish
GENUINE_HUMAN or FIELD predicates.

Retain original and replacement commitments, original failures, residual
costs and unresolved effects. UNKNOWN survives timeout, cancellation and
restart until original-identity readback. Operational acceptance, financial
reconciliation and verified access retirement remain separate predicates.
Completion never grants resource release or protected authority.
