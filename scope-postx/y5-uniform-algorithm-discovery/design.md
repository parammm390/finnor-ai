## Whole-shape comparison and decision

1. Free-form generated Lean plus a general model/GPU prover, separately
   translated to JavaScript. This offers a larger research domain, but requires
   unavailable model authority, a general untrusted-code sandbox, a new
   translation proof and substantial additional native assets.
2. A bounded, typed search language, native Lean proof construction and a
   separately invoked Lean kernel. Execute the very same Lean algorithm
   definition, not a JavaScript translation. Resolve the original S5 problem
   through its current owner, recognize a sufficient structural domain, and
   recheck the returned decision through the existing exact S5 checker.

Choose 2. This is a bounded research implementation, not a claim that a finite
grammar can discover every algorithm. Grammar exhaustion is INCOMPLETE.
Public development evidence cannot qualify the protected twelve families.

## Mathematical and economic profile

with its original units, exact rational objective, information, actions,
liabilities, resources, covenants, commitments, risk and human constraints.
Its existing qualification remains MODEL_RELATIVE / UNADMITTED.

The implemented adapter recognizes a fixed full-Boolean-box decomposition and
a dominating additive scenario. It reconstructs constraints from ORIGINAL
S4/S5 meanings, never the numerical producer matrix. Exact row bounds establish
redundancy; this adapter-to-Lean translation is NOT_FORMALLY_VERIFIED.
An incompatible interaction, forced coordinate, coupled constraint, missing
bound or scenario rejects applicability, not feasibility. This fixed recognizer
does not yet learn a new decomposition from recurring accepted episodes.

The mathematical family is arbitrary finite lists of exact integer weighted
binary decisions, with every Boolean choice permitted. Positive common
scaling preserves the original exact rational order. The candidate program
chooses each coordinate using a synthesized Boolean expression. Search must
construct a local exchange/dominance lemma and lift it across arbitrary list
length. No upper bound on mathematical list length occurs in the theorem.
Termination is structural recursion. A counted interpreter supplies a declared
coordinate/Boolean-operation bound; this is NOT a bit, CPU, memory or wall bound.
Exact ties omit a zero-weight decision. Forced choices are outside this
implemented theorem domain, rather than silently treated as free.

Definitions and generic tactics are reused assets. Retain every candidate,
counterexample, attempted proof and reused asset. Do not label a familiar
method, a generic induction strategy, or adapter code as protected discovery.
The development specification supplies definitions and a generic counted
`run`; the search supplies a decision expression and tactic-plan instantiation.
The implementation searches only six atomic expressions and three generic
local and three uniform tactic plans. It does not receive a successful proof
object, but this limited predicate/proof search is not autonomous discovery of
new structural objects. Qualification requires independently held
specifications and a substantially broader structure-learning implementation.
