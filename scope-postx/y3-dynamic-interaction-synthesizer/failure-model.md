# Failure model, frozen before mathematical implementation

## Mathematical and economic failures

Wrong rational normalization; floating-point regret tolerance; negative or
unnormalized chance/strategy probabilities; integer/denominator growth; a
convenient invented prior; distinct actor models averaged together; edited
operator/payoff/outside-option/expiry/payment meaning; future or private-state
policy keys; information partitions finer than lawful observations; forgotten
own actions; cycles, repeated information sets or absent-mindedness; omitted
mandatory disclosures; authenticated assertions treated as facts; unsupported
chance-zero and strategy-zero histories conflated; menu removal of a permitted
withdrawal; invented participation; hard limits checked only in expectation.

An early lie combined with a later option/withdrawal/action must remain in the
full continuation family. A report-only or one-action check is not the oracle.
A profitable permitted deviation is a counterstrategy, not a new exclusion.

Beliefs may be stale under changed priors/signals/disclosure/partitions.
Locally plausible posteriors may require incompatible tremble ratios. Separate
witnesses for different information sets or parties are unsound. A finite list
of epsilon examples is not a limit certificate. A common polynomial sequence
must be positive, normalized, converge to the complete prescribed profile and
induce the entire belief assessment under fixed chance support.

The independent checker reconstructs every obligation from original specification
bytes. It does not trust producer completeness flags, self-issued signatures,
precomputed trees, copied expected results or an equilibrium backend label.
Exact rational best responses cover every contingent continuation, including
mixed deviations. Perfect-recall premises are checked independently.

## Owner, security and lifecycle failures

Unauthenticated or cross-tenant/principal/Work reads; opaque unqualified model
references; request/terms substitution; body size/time exhaustion; SQL partial
updates; stale source/evidence/Work/mandate/runtime/checker revision; rights or
consent revocation; consent reused for revised terms; S5 capacity contention;
queue replay; incompatible old/new job versions; expired original deadline;
stale physical claim/compute lease; cancellation/restart creating a new episode;
worker/checker death around candidate acceptance or publication; missing cost
tails; a checkpoint authorizing effects; no genuine compatible fallback.

Execution must not select a hidden model node from an oracle. Participant-local
continuation uses only authenticated available observations and observed prior
actions. Every consequential intent binds exact action, terms, local principal,
resources, current grants and original responsibility. UNKNOWN cannot be cleared
by internal rollback, cancellation, revision or blind duplicate submission.

S8 development admission cannot become protected/production admission in storage
or presentation. A checked protocol cannot masquerade as an admitted S5 method.
Y1 meanings remain immutable. Y2/Y4 output is not presumed qualified.

Private results must disappear on Work/root/principal/token changes, genuine
logout, revocation, offline/reconnect and delayed responses from an old actor.
UI badges cannot turn provisional evidence into verified claims.

Installed builds may omit Python/checker/profile/solver assets, load donor
workspace links, hash newer source instead of emitted bytes, or replay only
source without installation. Reconstruction must detect each.

## Acceptance

CHECKED requires exact binding of problem/profile/policy/strategy/certificate,
globally consistent beliefs and zero full-continuation profitable gain at every
chance-supported information set for every admitted model, plus information,
participation and all-history hard-constraint checks.

INFEASIBLE requires an independently checked whole-family goal bound or complete
elimination proof under the same equilibrium concept. Search/timeout/process
failure is UNKNOWN or INCOMPLETE. Protected custody and protected comparison are
not established by public development cases.

No unit tests will be added after implementation. E2E cases follow
verification-plan.json. Necessary isolated mathematical campaigns use an
independent exhaustive small-game reference specified before implementation.
No production test-only authority, receipt or success-injection seam is allowed.
