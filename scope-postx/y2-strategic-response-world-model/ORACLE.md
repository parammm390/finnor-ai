# Frozen public development oracle

This specification precedes learner, checker and scenario-generator code.
It is public synthetic development evidence, not protected or field evidence.

For the decisive two-actor fixture, the independent synthetic environment uses:

    score_i = 1 + incentive + sign * 2 * interaction * previous_peer_count
              - outside_i + common_signal
    response_i = 1 when score_i >= 0, otherwise 0

`sign=+1` is complementary participation; `sign=-1` is crowd-out. These are
oracle mechanisms, never caller-supplied actor models. The learner receives only
observations and the general frozen finite coefficient/tie hypothesis class.
Lagged actions, outside options and common signals are observed only under
declared lawful actor-local information. Initial actions are `[1,1]`.

* Incumbent financing rule: interaction=0. Both families match every possible
  incumbent observation, including variation in incentive/outside/common/lag.
* Uninformative intervention: interaction=0 under another label. Both remain.
* Informative permitted intervention: interaction=1, including registered
  overlap/assignment and feature coverage conditions. Observe independent joint
  responses. A forbidden/private or nonoverlapping intervention cannot resolve
  the causal ambiguity.
* Withheld fresh interaction: incentive=1, interaction=1,
  outside=`[3,2]`, common=0. Complementary response is `[1,1]`; crowd-out response
  is `[0,0]`. The incumbent (incentive=1, interaction=0) predicts `[0,1]`.
* Canonical predicted decision payoff is USD 20 only when both participate,
  otherwise USD 0, with USD 2 coordination cost. No other payoff is implied.
  Under the complementary model the coordination programme has net USD 18.
  Under crowd-out it has net USD -2. The incumbent has USD 0.
  The original robust mandate therefore retains the incumbent while both
  remain and can choose coordination after supported disambiguation.
* Predictions are not consent or money. A disposable internal prior commitment,
  if created through unchanged Y1/S5/S6, must survive later transport withdrawal.
* A later unsupported regime withdraws affected predictions. The optimistic
  model is not kept merely to preserve transaction attractiveness.

Development extensions use two, four and eight actors, complete actor-local
lagged graphs, heterogeneous outside options, delays and shared common signals.
Cases distinguish joint participation from identical individual marginal
summaries. Parameters/tie alternatives are model hypotheses, not observations.
All numerical profiles and bounds are frozen before the scored run.

## Comparison and probability limits

Y2-on/off keeps Y1, meanings, objective, rights, evidence access, owner adapters
and total original resources fixed. Off retains baseline S3/X methods and
compatible incumbent. Policy-conditioning/coupling ablations diagnose mechanism
only. No qualified equipped B_X_release was supplied, so that separate
comparison is NOT_RUN/BLOCKED, never a defeated baseline.

No weights are assigned to alternative coefficient/behavior/regime models.
Support coverage and width are reported on independent withheld observations.
Frequency/probability calibration, if unavailable for this deterministic
set-valued profile, remains UNKNOWN/NOT_RUN, not a passed inherited probability
gate. Original 95% correctness, regret <=0.05, coverage error <=0.02 at
50/80/90/95%, width <=0.5 and material-decision confidence gates remain unchanged.
