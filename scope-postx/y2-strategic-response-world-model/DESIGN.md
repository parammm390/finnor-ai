## Limits and provenance

The frozen operative contract supports 2–3 actors, binary responses, at most
four periods and 512 complete joint worlds. Unsupported combinations refuse
without truncation. `ORACLE.md` also mentioned four/eight-actor development
extensions. Those are outside the frozen profile and are **not implemented or
passed**. The original frozen record is retained rather than edited after runs.

The public oracle retains six incumbent parameter/tie models across three
interference families. An executed synthetic probe narrows the family but keeps
two equivalent bias/tie alternatives. Conditional S4 values 0, 18 and 38 do not
establish field identification, calibration, business returns or equipped-X gain.

Existing joint S3 dynamics and actual S4 candidate search are reused, not new
Y2 capabilities. The incremental change is actor-local learned response sets,
explicit admitted public-rule meaning and their policy-dependent joint coupling.
The generic exact-source packet codec is reused unchanged from Y1.
