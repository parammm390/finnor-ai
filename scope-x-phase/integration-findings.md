# Current cross-phase integration findings

Read-only tracing of the actual integrated source established the following.
This is an engineering inventory, not a qualified business result.

## Existing genuine composition

- X3 compiles an explicit business specification, independently checks it,
  installs an immutable release and executes authenticated commands against
  persisted records under native authority/currentness.
- X5's `learning-experience.ts` resolves accepted native X3 event history, current
  consent, exact reconciliation and separate-principal approval. Supplied
  success labels cannot stand in for that history.
- X5 physically trains and retains parameters, then its consumer invokes
  `commandApplicationWithBoundary()` against fresh X3 records. It does not
  grant independent approval or manufacture acceptance.
- X4 has an actual experiment/effect/readback/check/replication/model/decision
  path. Its decision explicitly grants no business completion.

## Engineering still missing from the requested whole chain

1. **Actual X1/X2 source and contracts.** The institutional-program references
   in X3/X4/X5 are explicitly unresolved. They are not implemented by putting
   otherwise separate assets under a generic Work ID.
2. **A learned-operation experiment binding.** X4's current disposable loopback
   experiment/readback/control protocol is not a generic X2 operation adapter.
   X3's application language does not implement that scientific protocol.
   Do not fabricate a human-built application-specific wrapper. The PRD's
   unknown export can belong to the unfamiliar portal rather than the X3 app.
3. **A guarded decision-to-application continuation.** X4's checked decision
   currently ends at canonical decision/evidence persistence. X3 does not
   resolve its model validity, replication, consent and exact intervention as
   an operation precondition. An ordinary EVIDENCE reference is not equivalent.
4. **An integrated accepted-experience/evaluation domain.** The existing X5
   non-effectful domain rejects all tenant external operations and dispatched
   effects. A real X4-active tenant cannot be silently inserted into that
   unchanged evaluator. Any broader domain needs an explicit native contract,
   independent acceptance, retained original domain and complete shared-cost
   accounting. This is not permission to relax the original oracle.
5. **One maintained Work chain.** Current Canvas mounts X4 for deal Work and
   X3/X5 together for company-rooted Work. Correct panels and identity fences
   are useful, but do not establish institutional continuation or the complete
   five-output chain.

These are genuine engineering gaps, not merely missing protected sample counts.
Peer-dependent bindings require the real X1/X2 contracts before implementation.
The independently achievable current-cut regressions and native worker-factory
proof continue while those inputs are unavailable.

## Existing proof limitations

The inherited X3 `worker-once.mts` explicitly uses a one-second lease and only
registers X3. The X5/X4 loops register selected handlers directly. Preserve those
registered fault proofs and report their actual limits. They are not evidence
of an unconfigured 300-second production worker factory or full service boot.

The added opt-in native proof is specified in `acceptance/native-worker.md`.
It can establish actual factory registration plus business execution, not
managed-secret loading, scheduler/heartbeat leadership, container/arm64 parity,
native Mission execution or production activation.
