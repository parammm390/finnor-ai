## Failure model written before isolated verification

1. A producer omits a covenant, changes its sign, ignores an obligation or silently
   nets unlike currencies, entities, windows or rights. Its own matrix-based
   checker agrees with the mistake.
2. Standalone rankings/summed values miss complementarity, substitution or the
   common shock that makes a selected combination wrong or infeasible.
3. Terminal value or uncertain proceeds become present cash; delayed liabilities,
   prefix spend, maintenance demand or terminal commitments disappear.
4. All mutually exclusive alternatives are summed, or coincident/correlated
   branches each reuse the same funds. Private/future observation determines recourse.
5. Different processes solve the same snapshot and each reserve scarce capacity;
   aggregate predicates race despite individual-row updates/transactions.
6. One resource is acquired before another fails, leaving invisible partial
   reservations, or a database abort/restart duplicates a commitment.
7. Same idempotency key accepts a changed semantic payload; changed demand under
   a retained policy identifier or a foreign/stale certificate is accepted.
8. Rights/resource/policy/model/mandate revision races pass a stale preflight.
   A syntactically correct digest is confused with the issuing owner's current record.
9. Attempt/acknowledgement is treated as verified consumption/settlement. Expiry,
   policy cancellation, a worker crash or a timeout releases unresolved exposure.
   Replayed corrections double-consume or double-release.
10. FEASIBLE/UNKNOWN/timeouts become OPTIMAL; a float tolerance or wrong objective
    direction admits an unsafe margin; a fabricated upper bound proves a false gap.
11. An empty allocation is called safe while existing obligations already violate
    hard limits. Exhausted search or unavailable backend loosens constraints.
12. A fake prepared ExperienceLedger event or ordinary receipt is promoted to
    protected S6 authority; H1 objective is booked as H2 value.
13. Scaled scenario/horizon/resource coupling exhausts memory/latency while the
    reported budget counts only the optimizer and omits resolution, contention,
    retries, restart or human work.

These failures select the following small set of coherent scenarios. Each row
is the required four-answer OpenClaw authoring gate. An implementation may split
execution for reporting, but duplicates do not create additional evidence.

## Independently derived small reference problems

These suggested numerical cells are hypothetical H1 fixtures, not claimed
enterprise evidence. Fix exact inputs before scored runs; do not copy a producer's
compiled matrix, reservation, receipt or choice into expected results.

### Joint complementarity with a material covenant

Four fixed policies A/B/C/D, binary selection, simultaneous period 0. Capital/cash
limit 100, shared capacity 2, covenant leverage load at most 10. Two common
unweighted joint paths NORMAL/DOWNTURN; robust objective is the lower path value.
No probabilities are attached. All values use the same registered mandate unit.

| Policy | Capital | Capacity | Covenant load | NORMAL value | DOWNTURN value |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A supplier programme | 70 | 1 | 6 | 25 | 20 |
| B financing programme | 40 | 1 | 4 | 14 | 8 |
| C information/transition programme | 30 | 1 | 5 | 6 | 4 |
| D standalone programme | 60 | 2 | 0 | 18 | 14 |

The supplied qualified interaction gives B+C an additional 20 in both paths.
No interaction is inferred from individual fits. Feasible portfolios are empty,
A, B, C, D, and B+C. B+C consumes capital 70/capacity 2/covenant 9 and has path
values 40/32, so the exact optimum is 32. Ranking by standalone worst-path value
admits A then cannot admit the rest, producing 20. A+C consumes capital 100 and
capacity 2, but covenant load 11 rejects it. Challenge a producer/certificate
deliberately claiming A+C feasibility after omitting/altering the covenant; an
original-canonical-constraint checker must reject it even if hashes and compiled
matrix are mutually consistent. Claiming U=31 is false since checked B+C=32 is a
lower bound on the optimum. Derive all 16 combinations by a separate Python
Fraction/enumeration implementation; compare a mature MILP solver under identical
formulation, information and budgets. Greedy is only a coupling diagnostic.

### Cash timing cannot borrow from terminal value

Cash availability starts at 50. Existing liabilities require 20 in period 0
and 10 in period 1; a separately evidenced guaranteed inflow of 40 occurs in
period 1. A fixed policy requiring 60 in period 0 leaves cash -30 and is refused,
even if supplied terminal value is 100. A separately owner-produced linked S4
policy delaying that same commitment to period 1 leaves cash 30 at period 0 and
0 at period 1, hence fits. S5 selects between exact existing policy references;
it cannot rewrite the period of one policy. Replace guaranteed inflow with
uncertain proceeds and require unresolved/robust conservative behavior. Add a
terminal liability and verify STOP/expiry cannot erase it. This cell independently
checks temporal balance accounting, rather than a positive terminal objective.

### Common-path optionality without double use

Cash 100. One shared period-0 inquiry spends 10. Its available period-1 public
signal is UP/DOWN. P spends 70 if UP, 50 if DOWN; Q spends 20 if UP, 40 if DOWN.
On each common path their combined total including inquiry is 100. Keeping both
contingent fixed policies is feasible and useful. Adding all alternative demands
would reserve 190; adding standalone maxima would reserve 120. Neither is the
actual coincident envelope. Another policy spending 30 when UP cannot coexist:
its UP path would require 130. A forged independent-path justification cannot
remove that common UP overlap. Requesting UP at period 0 or using private state
as the signal must be rejected by the actual authorized observation/branch path.
Ordinary body-supplied exclusivity is insufficient; bind verified S4 node/tree
and supplied compatible joint-observation model revisions.

### Real persisted contention and crash boundaries

At least two, preferably eight separate ordinary-role processes simultaneously
request demand 60 from persisted capacity 100. Different keys permit at most one
winner. Duplicate same semantic key must resolve the same durable reservation,
while changed payload under that key must refuse. Independently query sum of live
reserved/consumed/unresolved commitments and compare to original capacity after
all children finish, again after cold restart, and after retry. Include competing
two-resource requests, reversed request ordering, one acquisition failure and a
resource revision after solve but before reserve. Full abort must leave neither
partial reservation nor missing liability. A process-only Promise.all call is
not this proof.

Kill a real consumer process at externally observed durable boundaries: reservation
committed before handoff; attempt commitment before acknowledgement; acknowledged
but not independently reconciled. On restart, absent qualified settlement retains
exposure. Replay identical supported consumption/reconciliation/release evidence,
change semantic payload under evidence identity, expire policy/certificate while
the external outcome remains unknown, and try a second allocation of those funds.
The second allocation remains blocked until real supported release. If only
generated/preexisting unknown rows or an owner assertion can be supplied, label
the demonstrated claim conservative refusal/ordinary state recovery, not actual
protected S6 execution/reconciled settlement.
