## Failure model fixed before implementation

1. Preregistration is mutable, occurs after assignment/outcome access, or binds a
   different tenant, owner, mandate, population, treatment, comparator, endpoint,
   horizon, method or source version from the analyzed programme.
2. Failed/declined/overridden programmes leave the economic ITT cohort; actual
   exposure or missing observation becomes intended exposure or economic zero.
3. Assignment probabilities, confounder law, independence, shared-counterparty
   exposure or behavior-policy support are inferred from later decisions.
4. Interference is treated as IID actions; cluster/member composition changes;
   interacting effects are allocated as individually identified causal rewards.
5. Positive market movement/confounding produces confident positive credit;
   selection, bankruptcy, weak support or opportunistic stopping narrows bounds.
6. Dollars, dates, ownership/waterfalls, source versions or discount/carry rules
   mismatch; floating errors flip a financial or benchmark threshold.
7. Cash and residual equity double count one asset; borrowed cash is profit;
   additional capital is omitted; embedded costs are deducted twice; unmetered
   costs, outstanding liabilities, failed research and displaced resources vanish.
8. FINNOR forecasts self-certify residual H2; unauthorized/stale/conflicted valuers
   or wrong as-of ownership/debt produce admissible residual value.
9. Same economic item is booked twice across programmes/companies/nested owners;
   intra-owner transfers create wealth; aggregate joint/unallocated value is lost.
10. Duplicate/concurrent ingest, restart between writes, conflict, backdate or late
    correction rewrites history, duplicates value or loses original dependencies.
11. Submitted JSON forges owner references/receipts; cross-tenant or sealed evaluator
    access succeeds; source/current-authority revocation leaves cached admission.
12. H0/H1 become H2 by label/age; immature endpoints, unknown residuals/liabilities
    or incomplete costs become a finished economic comparison.
13. Weakest comparator substitutes for the maximum, denominator <= epsilon becomes
    a ratio win, wide intervals disappear, or incompatible protocols are pooled.
14. Numerical/resource exhaustion returns a fabricated estimate; invocation, route,
    cost, source, dependency or library provenance is absent from the assessment.

## Statistical calibration preregistration

The supported estimator's actual assignment/dependence/bound assumptions must be
fixed before its first scored run. The following evaluation population is fixed
now: independent cluster assignment with externally known probability 0.5; complete
programme outcomes include failures and all cost items. No clinical, S3 bootstrap,
cross-fitting or weak observational assumptions are silently substituted.

Use tractable exact assignment support first: eight independently assigned clusters
have 256 equiprobable assignment vectors. Enumerate all vectors for each registered
zero/positive/negative/interaction potential-outcome table and evaluate claimed
coverage over the complete design distribution, including no-arm/support refusals.
If a method is defined conditional on minimum arm support, report its support
probability and conditional coverage separately; refused assignments do not count
as numerical intervals. The statistic-specific appendix will register the exact
tables, interval/reference method, multiplicity family and comparison tolerances.

Then use fixed-seed independent repeated trials at n=24 and n=64 clusters (not
decision counts), 2000 trials per registered table/domain, seeds 2026100401 and
2026100402. Report point coverage, exact binomial Monte Carlo uncertainty, average
width, support/refusal rate, bias, positive false-credit rate and detection power.
Do not use one truth-inside-interval result as calibration. For an advertised 95%
coverage method, the preregistered failure criterion is that the one-sided 95%
Clopper–Pearson lower coverage bound is <0.93; report the 0.95 target separately.
This tolerance is a software statistical falsification threshold and does not
replace any frozen benchmark coverage requirement. For exact enumeration, claimed
coverage below its declared simultaneous target is failure without Monte Carlo
tolerance. No power requirement will be invented after looking at results; a wide
conservative interval may pass coverage while remaining economically inconclusive.

An observational/nonidentified subclass is scored on refusal or defensible bounds,
not estimation accuracy. Repeated-look inputs must obey one registered fixed
analysis or an actually supported time-uniform method; a confidence interval
recomputed repeatedly is not certified anytime-valid. Statistical agreement with
another estimator is corroboration, never an identification proof.

from deterministic bookkeeping, runtime crash/recovery, prospective field outcomes
