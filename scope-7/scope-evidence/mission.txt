- Well-defined causal programmes and longitudinal inference: Hernán and Robins, Causal Inference: What If. Study target-trial specification, aligned eligibility/assignment/time zero, consistency, exchangeability, positivity, time-varying treatment and selection. Translate the mathematics to the actual economic programme; do not copy clinical assumptions into PE.
- Identification before estimation: DoWhy's causal-effect workflow. Candidate machinery for explicit identification, compatible estimation and refutation. Passing refuters does not prove the causal graph or remove unmeasured confounding.
- Nuisance estimation and honest inference: DoubleML's user guide, cross-fitting, and sensitivity analysis. Evaluate supported orthogonal scores, dependence handling and assumptions. Cross-fitting is not randomization, and robustness to nuisance error is not robustness to arbitrary confounding.
- Heterogeneity and supported treatment regimes: EconML. Evaluate relevant estimators and their actual inference domains. Heterogeneous estimates, feature importance and policy interpreters cannot automatically certify realized causal credit or individual-action contributions.
- Interference across firms and shared counterparties: Aronow and Samii, Estimating Average Causal Effects Under General Interference. Connect assignment design, exposure mapping and estimands. Its known interference assumptions matter; shared vendors, capital and strategic responses do not become independent observations.
- Staggered implementation: Callaway and Sant'Anna, Difference-in-Differences with Multiple Time Periods. Relevant when timing varies and the required parallel-trends conditions are defensible. Do not treat a convenient regression or nonsignificant pretrend as proof of identification.
- Selection and partial identification: Lee, Training, Wages, and Sample Selection: Estimating Sharp Bounds on Treatment Effects. Relevant bounding ideas for informative selection. Inspect the necessary assumptions, including those required for trimming; do not apply them automatically to every missing economic outcome.
- Sequential policy evaluation: Jiang and Li, Doubly Robust Off-policy Value Evaluation. Relevant to logged sequential regimes with justified behavior probabilities and support. Offline replay cannot certify unsupported actions, hidden decision inputs or a programme's mature owner value by itself.
- Repeated looks and stopping: Howard et al., Time-uniform, Nonparametric, Nonasymptotic Confidence Sequences. Evaluate sequential inference only where its conditional dependence and distribution assumptions hold. Anytime validity does not repair causal identification or justify treating thousands of dependent decisions as independent samples.

Relevant foundations include ownership/capital-structure adjustments, as-of evidence, calibration and documented valuation judgments. Frozen S7 specification
Name: Economic attribution.
Fundamental responsibility: Determine the incremental economic consequences attributable to intervention programmes.
Canonical primitive: OutcomeEstimand.
Owns: Preregistered contrasts, measurement and horizons; assignment versus actual exposure; interference/noncompliance/censoring treatment; identifiable value or bounds; reconciled programme/company/portfolio accounting.
Explicitly does NOT own: Physical completion, operational model training, enterprise objectives or self-selected reward definitions. Allocating aggregate credit does not identify each action's causal contribution.
Why distinct: Mechanical settlement and subsequent profit do not establish causal economic value.

Build one coherent estimand/attribution system with these observable properties:
1. An immutable, preregistered economic question. Bind OutcomeEstimand to the authorized mandate, legal owner/ownership interests, eligible opportunity population, assignment unit, programme/regime versions, comparator, time zero, outcome definitions, horizon, accounting convention, causal contrast and analysis plan. Bind collection/assignment to S2 protocols and interventions to S3 vocabulary. Freeze required choices before assignment and access to scored outcomes. 2. Complete assignment and realization. Programme assignment is the primary intention-to-treat contrast. Execution failure or noncompliance does not remove an assigned programme from its economic denominator.
3. Identification specific to the actual contrast. Support design-based attribution where assignment warrants it and compatible observational identification or bounds where justified. Make exchangeability, positivity, consistency, selection, interference, time alignment and transport assumptions explicit. Frozen economic measurement
Implement the locked accounting and benchmark semantics without replacing them with a more convenient metric.
For controller/programme a at registered horizon T:
W_a(T)
  = Σ[t ≤ T] D_t × (Cash_owner,a(t) − K_add,a(t))
    + D_T × (E_res,a(T) − L_extra,a(T))
    − C_once,a(T)
    − W_opening

V_a^H2 = E_P[W_a(T) − W_0(T)]
B      = max[b ∈ qualified baselines] V_b^H2
M_economic = V_FINNOR^H2 / B, only when B ≥ ε > 0
Here:
- Cash_owner is realized owner cash/distributions, net of settled owner liabilities and costs assigned to that cash leg.
- K_add is additional owner capital contributed.
- E_res is independently and conservatively bounded residual equity, net of entity debt/liabilities already incorporated in that valuation.
- L_extra contains only outstanding owner-borne liabilities not reflected elsewhere.
- C_once contains the complete discounted attributable intervention/system/human/compute/data/integration/maintenance/recovery costs not already deducted. Include attributable learning, failed research, admission, remediation and realistic development/integration allocation. No amortization across hypothetical future missions.
- W_opening is the matched opening owner wealth. 0 is preregistered business-as-usual. P is the fixed eligible portfolio/opportunity distribution. Discount/capital-carry conventions are registered and do not charge the same capital cost twice.
Report realized cash and independently bounded residual equity separately. Speculative modeled upside is never booked as realized cash. An unmetered cost or unresolved liability is not free.
Compare pinned current FINNOR, independently equipped frontier-plus-specialist controllers, Muse, dots and relevant expert PE/operations baselines using matched starting assets, eligible opportunities, information rights, preparation, capital/resource/risk envelopes and horizon. Include all comparator human support and costs.
Preserve simultaneous 95% intervals [L_a,U_a], incorporating sampling, identification, valuation, costs and censoring. The locked economic claim requires:
L_FINNOR ≥ 100 × max_b U_b
max_b L_b ≥ ε

Named comparisons additionally require:
L_FINNOR ≥ 10 × U_Muse, with L_Muse ≥ ε
L_FINNOR ≥ 10 × U_dots, with L_dots ≥ ε
All registered reach, material absolute-value, decision-payoff, capital, downside, resource and safety requirements must also pass. The strongest-baseline maximum and simultaneous inference cannot be replaced by choosing the weakest comparator.
Preserve the primary T = 36 months endpoint from programme assignment, at least 90 days of closed-loop operation, the registered downside floor ES_0.99(−W_a) ≤ R_0, and all frozen power, stopping, multiplicity and denominator requirements. Shorter intermediate results do not satisfy that system endpoint. Longer exit claims require their registered longer endpoint.
