# S7 method, reuse and claim boundaries

Version: `s7-bounded-owner-value-v1`, method `BOUNDED_HT_HOEFFDING_V1`.

PROBABILISTICALLY_SUPPORTED: the method's declared domain is a fixed finite eligible population of independently randomized interference clusters, fixed positive categorical assignment probabilities, registered complete contingent programmes, fixed weights summing exactly to one, and independently supported bounded potential owner wealth. Consistency, aligned eligibility/assignment/time zero, no between-cluster interference, and fixed population/support remain substantive assumptions. S2 owns the native randomization law and actual cryptographic draws. Failures, noncompliance, overrides and missing follow-up stay in the denominator. Missing outcomes use the full registered support; an unresolved monetary notice refuses bounded causal credit when that support cannot be reconfirmed.

For programme a versus business-as-usual 0 the weighted Horvitz–Thompson score is the sum of w_i[1(A_i=a)Y_i/p_a − 1(A_i=0)Y_i/p_0]. Each independent score summand has its preregistered bounded range, including zero for other arms. Hoeffding's two-sided radius is sqrt(sum(width_i²) log(40 m L)/2), where m is the complete registered contrast family and L is the finite registered look count. A Bonferroni union bound provides simultaneous conditional 95% coverage over that family and those looks. There is no claim of unrestricted anytime validity. The point score's expectation is the fixed finite population regime contrast when the assumptions hold. Measurement/valuation intervals propagate through the score; bounds intersect the registered contrast support. Unsupported observational designs stay unidentified.

DETERMINISTICALLY_VERIFIED within the tested bookkeeping examples: amounts use exact BigInt rational arithmetic, with 12-place outward decimal endpoints. The logarithm uses rational atanh-series bounds with an explicit remainder; integer square-root ceiling bounds the radius. This was built for the constrained bounded method rather than using floating-money arithmetic. The canonical semantic encoder remains in S7; the native protected wrapper is `s7-economic-record-v2`. S6 authenticates the small commitment envelope and references without owning the arithmetic.

Reuse/adapt/build decisions: reuse installed Zod 3.25.76 contracts, pg 8.23.0/Drizzle 0.45.2 tenant transactions, Node v22.18.0 cryptography/process metering, existing S6 durable delivery/protected process and immutable owner readers. Build S7's exact owner-value reconciliation and credit admissibility. Adapt the bounded independent-summand inequality, not an unsupported observational effect estimator. No new dependency was installed. TypeScript 5.9.3, Vitest 4.1.11, Next 15.5.25 and Embedded Postgres 18.4.0-beta.17 are recorded in the final manifest. The original start snapshot used Node v22.22.3; scored final runners used v22.18.0. Source/runtime pins are per artifact.

Primary foundations: Hoeffding (1963), Theorem 2, https://www.cs.rpi.edu/academics/courses/spring06/random/hoefding.pdf; Aronow and Samii's general-interference identification framework, https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.6156. These do not establish the empirical identification assumptions for a FINNOR programme. IPEV's 2025 valuation guidance informed authority/as-of/ownership discipline, not a claim of accounting-standard compliance or realized cash. The research references are accessible scholarly/official sources; none were copied as software dependencies. The user-selected OpenClaw test-audit instructions and MIT license are retained at their pinned source digest.

PARTIAL: observations/valuation/cost intervals are only as sound as independently authorized sources. Authentic signatures bind issuer and bytes, not financial or causal truth. The isolated inference fixtures use generated trusted-resolver descriptors and cannot authenticate field inputs. Native tests use real signed S6 receipts under visibly disposable authorities, not production admission. Independent monetary resolutions require a different registered valuation principal with explicit resolution authority, exact original question/notice/owner/currency/look bindings and actual used corrected sources when included. A generated resolution cannot mature an assessment to H2.

UNKNOWN: actual attributable provider, development and human costs were not supplied. Process CPU/RSS/elapsed measurements describe local runs; RSS is a process interval observation, not isolated peak allocation. Registered structural limits are 4,096 clusters, 32 arms/looks, 4,096 items per cluster, 8 MiB records/requests and 30-second compute/source-read budgets, with a stricter 4,096-reference native source cut. Valid schemas do not guarantee that every maximum-size combination fits the native record budget. Exhaustion refuses; a single successful 256-cluster generated pure-engine case does not pass the system reach/resource gate. No cgroup/shared-S5 production reservation or billing admission is certified by these local limits.

PARTIAL precision: 12,000 generated Monte Carlo trials covered truth in every run, with conservative intervals and very weak power. In effect +30 challenges the positive-credit rate was 0.001 at n=24 and 0.006 at n=64; typical widths were 130–193. This supports the method's bounded conditional behavior in the tested subclass, not useful PE precision, transfer, arbitrary interference, attainable 100× headroom or mature economic success. DoubleML, EconML, DoWhy, staggered DiD, Lee trimming and sequential off-policy estimators were not integrated because the current verified assignment/source domain does not justify their additional identifying assumptions. This is a bounded initial method, not a universal causal estimator.
