# Additional owned-path failure model, recorded before implementation

S2 prospective protocol/assignment: late or substituted protocols, nonpositive or nonunit probabilities, caller-selected draws, cross-tenant/role/principal access, rerandomization on concurrent/restarted retry, partial group commit, unprotected S7 preregistration, assignment after outcome access, and protocol or population substitution. The protocol may be prepared locally, but assignment requires authentic protected preregistration and an independently resolved business-owner mandate. Crypto categorical draws use registered integer units out of one million; supported finite fixed cluster laws only. Assignment records do not commission or authorize intervention execution.

Reconciliation: overlapping item identities, substituted ownership or currency, nested unregistered boundaries, internal transfers counted as value, naive sum of causal contrasts, and ambiguous allocation of jointly caused value. Aggregate readbacks expose component intervals and joint/unallocated value, without manufacturing a portfolio causal contrast. A complete separately registered portfolio estimand can supply the aggregate contrast. Unknown component wealth propagates to unknown total.

Consumer: caller-invented scores, stale assessments after invalidation, privileges expanded from S8 read into S7 append/execution, disappearing failures/corrections and per-agent causal rewards. S8 reads immutable permitted assessments and append-only history; it receives no action rewards or estimator-admission authority.

Benchmark: weak baseline selection, unrun required arms, missing signed preregistration, post-outcome benchmark selection, mismatched pinned versions, circular gate self-reference, stale frontier, incomplete costs, negligible or negative denominator, negative numerator interval arithmetic and multiplier/capability mixing. No economic PASS can follow generated/disposable evidence. Full causal/support intervals and all joint effects remain visible.

Historical read: track_commit_timestamp disabled or aged/frozen xmin must return incomplete, never a retrospectively complete cut. New assessments retain the exact native history cut and propagate invalidations. Full dependency manifests replace truncation when event dependency bounds are reached.

Validation uses observable owner/API contracts, real PostgreSQL transactions and a separate signed ledger process; synthetic authorities remain visibly disposable. Statistical challenge changes retain original preregistered coverage threshold and all failures. Independent reference calculations are distinct from human release approval. No new unit tests are authorized or added.

Late economic notice audit (before repair): new COST, CORRECTION or VALUATION knowledge must invalidate a stale assessment and cannot be silently ignored by reassessment. Unknown adverse amounts may invalidate the frozen bounded support; an unresolved notice must produce unidentified credit and unknown costs, retaining every assigned unit. Closure requires an authenticated independently registered authority that explicitly has economic-notice resolution permission, bound to the original question, notice, owner, currency and look; included corrections must bind the actual used financial sources. Historical scores remain immutable.

Protected benchmark dependency audit (before repair): the computed assessment digest is a semantic child of the durable S7 assessment wrapper, not a separately committed S6 reference. Benchmark event dependencies must use the authentic native wrapper instead of requesting an absent standalone score reference. The full score preimage remains in the wrapper and benchmark input; no source dependency is dropped. The signed outbox drain regression exposed two UNCOMMITTED_OR_UNAUTHORIZED_REFERENCE failures.

Repeated protected assessment history (before final repair): authenticated event readbacks include each old event's entire detail, whose assessments themselves embed earlier event readbacks. This duplicates committed history recursively and can exhaust the unchanged 8 MiB S7 budget on a later correction. Retain every event/reference identity, signed receipt, exact content digest and metadata, with immutable protected preimages addressable by their original identities, rather than nesting full historical bodies. Do not enlarge Ring-0 or S7 budgets, remove the late-notice case, or label the refusal as a passing reconciliation. The integrated-final artifact preserves the actual 413 failures.
