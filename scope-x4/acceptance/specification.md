# Independent development acceptance, revision 1

This specification precedes the generator, instrument and critical checker.
It is not a protected-population registration or a mechanism answer supplied to
the candidate. Changes require a new oracle revision and retained old failures.

## Acceptance predicate

The original operational question, target, affected decision and requested claim
class must match the owner contract. Unknown/excluded assumptions must remain
explicit. No ordinary code gap is an external qualification asset.

For the incomplete-export development case the owner needs a current complete
export before treating reconciliation as complete. The tested alternative causes
are early snapshot, page truncation and role filtering, including conjunctions.
At least two executable alternatives must fit the ordinary initial observations.
The candidate does not receive mechanism names, programmes or private state.

The runtime must generate programmes by grammar closure over admitted variables,
freeze their outcome predictions before experiments, select an authorized
discriminating input and actually cause a physical experiment. The external
system's independent operation journal establishes actual input/exposure and
record identity. HTTP ACK alone cannot pass.

A finding is supported only if every retained analyzed record agrees with every
surviving programme under tolerance 0; every refuted programme retains its
counterexample; missing/failed/deviant experiments retain their original records;
and the registered search/representation scope is disclosed.

Fresh prediction and a separately administered fresh-instance replication are
mandatory before checked publication. They must use fresh record identities and
at least one intervention condition not analyzed during discovery.
Predictions are immutable before outcomes. Replication is initiated by another
currently authorized principal, not accepted from a worker summary or caller
grade. Failed replication immediately makes current use false.

Claim classes are PREDICTIVE, INTERVENTION_IDENTIFIED and MECHANISM_SPECIFIC.
This development profile can support a conditional intervention contrast within
the exact controlled finite domain, but never a unique hidden mechanism or
production transport from successful prediction. Remaining equivalent
programmes are retained. Exhausted search is explicitly distinct from a proof of
non-identifiability.

The decision owner resolves the actual checked finding and its current
assumptions, chooses a permitted action/observation/wait/stop, and persists that
choice with its model and canonical finding lineage. Unsupported conditions must
withhold completion. The mounted Work must show that actual choice.

## Instrument oracle

Known controlled cases are independently specified before unknown experiments.
The measurement path reads only the same legitimate role-bound paginated
surfaces used for unknown results. Test controls separately cover:

* exact record/event identities and expected finite set;
* more records than one displayed page, including a misleading displayed count;
* request/start/completion ordering and nonambiguous clock/slot interpretation;
* authorized role visibility without cross-role credential reuse;
* exact integer values, unit and transformation identity;
* missing records, duplicate identities, repeated/skipped pagination cursors;
* stale reads, pending availability, terminal empty result and bounded timeout.

Instrument output must match independent controlled expected records, not a
hypothesis or the instrument's own normalizer. The checker uses a separate raw
page walk and programme evaluator. Deliberate missing/duplicate/wrong-unit/stale
defects must be detected or disposition UNKNOWN/INSTRUMENT_INVALID.
Delayed pending observations may later complete within the original allocation.

| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Fixed explanation menu, hypotheses fit passively | Different generated ASTs, pre-observation differing predictions, physical discriminating interventions |

| E | Equivalence/search exhaustion/unsupported noise becomes mechanism truth | Alternatives and exact boundary retained; no mechanism-specific or transported claim |
| F | Artifact attached but never used | Canonical checked finding is resolved by a real decision; changed validity yields wait/stop |

| G | Cancellation deletes liability or renews budget | Original counters/deadline/effect/cost lineage remain; recovery read only |
| H | Old user/Work/token response resurrects private result | Real UI switch/logout/reconnect/delayed response yields no foreign/obsolete scientific data |
| I | Builder-only runtime, migration or hidden donor dependency | Fresh ordinary lock install, emitted API/worker/assets, migration/restart/in-flight and standalone replay |
| J | Observation-only arm presented as strongest incumbent | Registered observation-only, actual strongest existing supported path and absent B_ref separately reported |

Every new or changed check has this observable contract, a credible defect, an
owning boundary and an independently computed expectation. No fixture may insert
terminal X4 models, admission receipts, verified effects or claimed completion.
Normal disposable input/identity/authority setup is allowed and labeled.

## Adaptive analysis and bounds

Exact deterministic eliminative analysis preserves the full assignment/stopping
history. No p-values, posterior, IID claims, multiplicity correction or calibrated
confidence are invented. Missingness, interference, confounding, noise or
unsupported timing invalidate the deterministic analysis rather than disappearing.
The selection criterion is finite worst-case partition separation, not a
probabilistic information score. Solver/search timeout is UNKNOWN.

The frozen domain/bounds in `architecture.md` cannot be narrowed after a failure.
Overflow, unrepresentable behavior and exhausted budget are retained outcomes.
Counterfactual predictions are conditional programme outputs until checked
against external interventions. Successful dev cases cannot score the original
thirty-six-system gate.

Finite behavioral compression is acceptable only when an independently
implemented checker reconstructs the complete grammar closure over every
registered condition/record vector. Each retained class must include its exact
truth vector, executable representative, and syntactic-alternative count.
Factorized inclusion and affine-value families must retain the entire surviving
Cartesian product, not one preferred pair. No quotient proves mechanism identity
or behavior beyond the registered domain.

## Custody and costs

The runtime interpreter has no oracle/source/filesystem/credential/effect
instructions. Its trusted broker uses only a registered route and separately
bound observation scope. Raw protected mechanism truth is not included in
candidate inputs, model, Work, logs or exports.

The builder still controls the development machine and fixture sources. Therefore
all results remain development evidence regardless of local signatures.
Protected admission requires independent original population/custody/authority.

Charge generation, calibration, unsuccessful experiments, observation, checking,
replication, recovery and decision use to original parent allocations. Preserve
unavailable money, human work, aggregate peak memory and provider billing as
UNKNOWN with reasons. Do not report a gain, amortization or financial result
without the complete matched denominator.
