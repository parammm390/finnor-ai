## Designs considered

1. Generated general source programmes in a subprocess, with a solver and active
   counterexample loop. This can express arbitrary behavior, but would require a
   new sandbox, execution platform and credential boundary. It is rejected.
2. Bounded relational/state-space expression programmes interpreted as data.
   Systematic grammar closure creates new predicates and numerical programmes,
   rather than selecting mechanism labels. Existing canonical execution owners
   deliver finite authorized inputs. This is chosen.

The chosen design has less mechanism coverage but a smaller falsifiable domain,
no arbitrary code execution, deterministic replay, exact integer computation,
explicit representation/search exhaustion and a direct canonical integration.
It does not force digital mechanisms into temporal-linear S3 or add six engines.

## Frozen development profile

`PE_FIRM_DIGITAL_RELATION_V1`, revision 1, investigates deterministic bounded
record inclusion and integer-valued transformations in consented PE-firm test
services. Operational questions retain their original descriptive, predictive,
intervention-contrast or mechanism request. An unsupported requested class is
reported rather than silently replaced.

The profile has at most 6 named integer features, at most 8 explicit admitted
values per feature, 16 records per experiment, 64 registered intervention
conditions and 64 physical experiments per original Work. Inputs, roles, version,
state, units and time semantics are explicit. Scientific numerical values are
safe bounded integers, not money converted to floating point. Admitted financial
values elsewhere remain unchanged strings with their native meaning.

Executable hypotheses are composed from TRUE/FALSE, equality and order
comparisons of fields/constants/fields, negated atoms and conjunction/disjunction
of two atoms. Numerical programmes are `a * value + b`, with integer parameters
generated from the registered coefficient domain [-3,3]. The generator is blind
to test mechanism labels. Its full bounded language is declared before scoring.
At most 16384 predicate programmes and 49 numerical programmes may be generated;
overflow refuses with a representation/search boundary, never a truncated proof.

The finite registered condition/record vectors define the prediction domain.
The implementation may store a behavioral quotient of the full grammar: retain
one executable representative, its complete finite truth vector, the grammar and
atom basis, and the number of syntactic alternatives in each equivalence class.
This is exact compression over the declared finite domain, not evidence of a
unique mechanism or equivalence outside it. Both the producer and an independent
checker must enumerate that closure; an omitted distinct truth vector is a
checker failure. Joint inclusion/numeric programmes may be represented as a
factorized product, with every surviving factor and counterexample retained.

The elimination rule requires exact categorical outcomes and exact integer
numerical outcomes (absolute tolerance 0). The deterministic profile assumes
fresh isolated records, no interference or unmeasured input dependence and
complete observations. Violations, noise, informative missingness, timing
ambiguity or changed exposure yield UNKNOWN/UNSUPPORTED, not a fitted claim.
This deliberately does not claim calibrated probabilistic uncertainty or
continuous-time, arbitrary hidden-state, stochastic or unrestricted service
identification. Scientific costs/missing observations are retained.

Selection maximizes worst-case elimination among current hypotheses, divided
by registered nonzero cost, then minimizes risk/exposure and stable identity.
This is a finite minimax partition criterion, not expected information gain,
a prior or a calibrated likelihood. All distinguishing predictions are durable
before dispatch. Equivalent programmes survive; narrative preference cannot
choose one. Exhausted search is not general non-identifiability.

The first scenario is the incomplete export. Ordinary observations support
timing, pagination and role explanations. Real permitted test operations vary
these controls independently. Candidate runtime cannot read mechanism source or
oracle state. It may inspect only fixed legitimate HTTP observation surfaces.
Known calibration cases are separate from unknown instances.

programme predictions, input IDs, application/version, principal/role, source,

allocation and deadline. Actual delivered exposure is independently read back.

to canonical owner records, including UNKNOWN values with reasons. It carries
programmes and parameter identities, observations, failed predictions, protocols,
checks, replication, withheld predictions, claim class, assumptions, equivalent
alternatives, selection rule, transport restrictions and canonical findings.
No duplicate authoritative truth, success, effect or cost ledger is introduced.

## Recovery and current use

Before possible mutation S6 commits the original canonical effect identity.
Possible delivery is sticky across worker death and response loss. Recovery only
reads that original operation; it never allocates a new effect to repeat it.
Cancelled/expired Works retain effects, observations, costs and liabilities.
Stale jobs require the ordinary durable delivery token/fence and cannot publish.
Resource acquisition, dispatch, observation, checking and decision commits recheck
current rights, Work cut, application version, source/runtime/checker identity,
deadline and original remaining budget. Negative decisions remain wait/stop.

Failed replication fences future model use and S8 CURRENT through actual native
proof. P7 invalidates model/use descendants without erasing original records.
A model cannot publish itself as qualified or renew its allocation.
