# Independent discovery inspection, before driver integration

The configured development S8 evaluation must not copy `check.accepted` or a
candidate's claimed transition as its expected truth. The primary owner is the
existing native authenticated portal E2E. The inspector runs only in the
separate driver/evaluator, never in delivered acquisition or execution.

The frozen `evaluate.mjs snapshot` command first validates the independent
provider's fsynced journal and frozen oracle bytes. For each actual native
discovery request, match its original wire request/effect/obligation/parent and
target to the independently retained dispatch event. Compare native before and
after observations with the provider's corresponding durable record revisions.
Use the original discovered field types, not candidate-supplied types, for
identity/quantity normalization. Preserve exact decimal/currency/entity/unit/
date/basis strings. Missing revisions or completion remain unpassed.

Evaluate candidate guards as predictions only. The original independent
dispatch outcome is truth. Count false-valid and false-invalid separately for
operations in the model's declared role domain. Role-denied exploration outside
that domain remains separately visible, not successful business throughput.
Check all accepted authority snapshots, delayed submission completion and exact
identity counts. Require positive evidence on both independently issued records
for every admitted operation. A refusing model, missing observation, duplicated
effect, false completion or unsupported original evidence cannot qualify.

Credible regressions: forged native observation, rounded quantity, wrong target/
role/grant, guard expansion, an approval-looking response without completion,
duplicate effect identity, or an inspector copying model/checker expectations.
Earlier native positive proof grades only the fresh business result, so it does
not independently grade every discovery transition used by S8.

Retain the inspector inputs/results privately with hashes and use only its
verified counts in the signed development evaluation. No fabricated owner
receipt, currentness stamp or protected qualification is supplied. The existing
S8 service must perform register/evaluate/admit/activate through its real journal
and separate authenticated principals. No production test hook is introduced.
