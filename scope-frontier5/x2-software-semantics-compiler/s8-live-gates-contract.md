unexpected/nonordinary/multiple blocked writers; wrong session/claim or
noncanonical lease/request; reconciled, expired or possibly consequential
subject; stale alternate stamp; incomplete/racing canonical READ; an owner
refusal at the wrong guard; any accepted negative or extra USE; changed
positive revision, source cut, accounting, liability or native request;
any independent application-journal change during the pause; deadline,
timeout, cancellation, lost lock ownership, cleanup failure or late suite.

and stops/awaits its owned worker before releasing a still-held gate. It
does not resume an unchecked positive. Preserve the partial private report,

delete or overwrite earlier proof. No unit tests are added after code.

independent portal journal before/after the suite, successful original

original reconciliations must still pass separately. A passing fixture
