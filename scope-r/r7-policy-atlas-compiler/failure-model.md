## Original-input mathematical failures

- Noncanonical/rounded rationals, lost inequality orientation, unchecked integer
  growth, inconsistent units, guessed interval or midpoint membership.
- Empty, unbounded, lower-dimensional, point, strict or degenerate domains
  incorrectly classified; unsupported nonlinear/fitted-law transformations.
- Per-period world minimization instead of one complete fixed world; omitted
  discounts, costs, liabilities, constraints, worlds, periods or policies.
- Feasibility established at a centroid or only in the utility-minimizing world;
  a competitor pruned although feasible elsewhere.
- Missing ranking, worst-world, feasibility, equality or intersection boundary;
  lost tied policies or distinct tied world identities.
- Incomplete vertex enumeration, false empty leaf, wrong hull/common slack,
  forged implication proof, omitted split child, contradictory overlap,
  sample/volume coverage or budget-truncated family called complete.
- A checker importing producer lowering/geometry/ranking, trusting producer
  vertices or accepting swapped original input/policy/checker identities.
- Original policy branches use hidden world identity, reordered duplicate
  information states, premature observations or conflicting event tokens.
- Budget exhaustion loses frontier leaves or becomes mathematical infeasibility.

## Owner and integration failures

- Caller coefficients or synthetic receipts masquerade as authenticated owner
  truth; S1 snapshot bytes differ from immutable SQL/source witnesses.
- A 32-member family is truncated to 16 or mixed across owner revision vectors.
- Projection changes the selected original action/history/tie/demand, rounds
  rational money, claims a new conditional optimum, or repeats full optimization
  on a covered query.
- Present S5 resources, scope, lower/upper envelopes, covenants or outstanding
  UNKNOWN responsibility are omitted; build-time feasibility reserves resources.
- A stale parameter/Work/source/right/family/model/world/tie/checker/method/episode
  passes admission, claim, publication, query, selection or continuation.
- Canonical S4/S5 ports are guessed from the lead rather than implemented on the
  sealed cumulative base; R1/R2/R4 or old jobs are overwritten to obtain R7.
- Replay changes semantic payload/status, loses consumed work, renews a grant,
  invents a fallback receipt or widens effect authority.
- Child acceptance followed by supervisor loss causes blind replay, unrecorded
  costs, false physical release or unfenced publication.
- New SQL/schema/queue/API proposals are advertised as applied integration;
  source execution becomes installed Linux/non-root image qualification.
- Mounted Work is replaced by CLI output, a headless smoke or DOM-click proof.
  Identity/Work changes resurrect private output; reload uses unbound slider state.

## Adoption, custody and qualification failures

- Entire lead/history, raw evidence, private state, credentials, dependencies,
  runtimes, caches or unrelated M/P/R source is imported.
- An adopted file lacks exact immutable blob/SHA256 and cumulative-base preimage.
  Scientific dependencies or licensing are inferred without inspection.
- Historical red evidence is erased or historical ON 278.228829247 s versus
  OFF 174.600458365 s is relabeled a gain.
- Missing money/human/aggregate-memory/storage/preparation meters become zero.
- Conditional exact geometry becomes family adequacy, global programme-grammar
  optimality, protected admission/activation, completed-X gain or mature H2 value.

## Test-value rules

Use the registered original-input E2E/reference corpus and actual owner/SQL/queue
boundaries. Every family names its observable invariant, credible regression,
stronger missing coverage and any test-only product seam. No post-code unit
tests, producer-generated expectations, fixture-supplied acceptance receipts,
source/self comparisons or early unrelated refusals count as behavioral proof.
Private artifacts retain exact inputs, commands, steps, physical observations,
results, source/runtime pins and all reds. Rerun only affected registered checks
after a concrete repair. Original gates and profile bounds are not reduced.

## Registered connected-review regression before repair

A completed checked build can be canceled through its real authenticated control
route. Parameter and current S5 digests may remain unchanged, while its old query
row remains immutable. Current context and presentation must not relabel that
historical recommendation as current once the build is terminal/unpublished or
its original deadline has passed. The new actual bearer/SQL regression is
registered in verification-plan.json before changing the affected owner or UI.
