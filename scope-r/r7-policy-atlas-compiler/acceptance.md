Registered before new code. The immutable source specification is lead blob
`8127b3fc7f2f515dac79de4cce1b7b03a9caafe9`; no historical result is inherited.

The supported model has 1–4 bounded continuous rational coordinates, at most
32 immutable original policy templates, 64 complete fixed worlds and 12 periods.
All quantities retain source/owner/unit/currency/time/discount conventions.
Only explicitly supplied affine hypotheses are supported, not fitted FLOAT64
coefficients, products, variable denominators, nonlinear discounting or guessed
interval queries. Optimality is over the named family, not the full M3 grammar.

The separate Python Fraction checker reconstructs original whole-world utility,
every hard/resource/risk predicate, lawful policy topology and all identities from
original source bytes. It imports no producer lowering, geometry, sign, partition
or ranking functions. Hashes and successful child exits are not acceptance.

For each bounded cell, independently row-reduce original equalities and enumerate
every full-rank active inequality basis. Check every solved point against all
closed facets and deduplicate exact vertices. Coordinate bounds establish
boundedness; complete active-basis enumeration supplies H/V equivalence even for
implicit equalities and point domains. Compare the complete independently
reconstructed vertex set, affine-hull dimension and nonempty witness.

For strict facets independently enumerate the common-slack polytope, adding
`0 <= delta <= 1` and `delta` to every strict row. Positive slack proves actual
nonemptiness. No positive slack, after complete bounded enumeration, proves
emptiness. Limit/timeout means UNKNOWN, never EMPTY. Whole-cell non-strict bounds
come from closure extrema; strict exclusion additionally proves the intersection
with the zero hyperplane empty. Equality faces are not discarded.

For every policy independently prove whole-cell feasibility, or one original
hard predicate violated throughout. Independently identify every tied minimizing
world, compare every feasible robust value, retain every maximizing original
policy, and apply the frozen owner tie order. No feasible member means only
named-family infeasibility.

Reconstruct every split from its original constraint, within-policy world
difference or cross-policy payoff difference. Traverse exactly negative/zero/
positive children; reject omitted/reused/unreachable/cyclic children. Every leaf
is accepted CELL, independently EMPTY or explicit GAP. This proves exact coverage
including equality boundaries. COMPLETE iff no GAP remains. Exceeding fixed
node/depth/facet/basis/byte/integer/deadline limits retains gaps, not a smaller
secretly substituted family/domain.

The distinct reference uses original trajectories and Fraction evaluation, and
Cramer's determinant arrangement enumeration for registered one/two-coordinate
tractable fixtures. Entire-cell checker evidence, not sampled queries, carries
the universal claim. The four-coordinate 32/64/12 fixture is tractable by
construction; it does not establish arbitrary maximal-arrangement capacity.
