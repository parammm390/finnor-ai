## Original model and acceptance predicate

The finite, time-indexed information graph declares every original state, roots/current period, legal observations/history, stopped state, full fixed-world support or an owner-qualified probability law, complete semantic labels and action-indexed transitions. Full semantic labels cover observation rights/availability/pending delay, timing/maturity/options/guards, accrued per-world value, resources/reservations/consumption/occupancy/coupling, exposure schedules/locks, human/compute use, employee constraints, and S6 known/unknown/partial/terminal obligations. Source/model/mandate/rights/assumption/dependency references are retained. Hidden world data is checking context and never a branch-choice input.

The independent validator checks original bytes: finite shape, unique state/action/world/member identities, supported roots, exact quantities and their contexts, complete actions and transition support, temporal closure, enabled action/guard correspondence, lawful observations and sufficient-state/history premises. Unknown obligations remain unknown and cannot become zero. Unreachable members are checked and retained. Bounds are at most 128 information states, eight actions per state and 12 periods; world/byte/integer/step bounds are additional explicit limits, not truncation permission.

For a proposed relation R, acceptance requires every state exactly once, nonempty blocks, identical period and complete controller/resource/obligation labels, preserved roots and action identities/guards, identical exact immediate payoff/resource/obligation changes, and complete successor correspondence. For fixed worlds, compare each required transition under the same complete world and observation outcome; do not minimize/average first or select a new world later. For probabilities, validate exact nonnegative complete mass and compare action/observation/reward/resource-labelled mass into each R block; a current owner law is needed for certified use. No manufactured qualification is accepted.

The proposed quotient and lift must be derived from the declared relation without dropped/fabricated members, actions, transitions, labels or aliases. Model, domain, assumptions, producer/checker versions and checking receipt identity are rechecked at consumption. A malformed proof cannot be relabeled infeasibility. Checker failure/crash/timeout leaves no accepted dependent certificate and executes the compatible original path within remaining resources.

For acyclic time-indexed models, the preservation argument is backward horizon induction: equal terminal labels preserve per-world values/feasibility; equal enabled actions, immediate changes and corresponding successor classes preserve lawful feasible continuation alternatives and their payoff vectors; the root mapping then preserves the original objective and chosen tie order. The structural premises are checked across the complete model. This is neither a Lean theorem nor evidence of causal adequacy.
