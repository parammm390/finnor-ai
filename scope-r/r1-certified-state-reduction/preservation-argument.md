# Finite preservation argument

This argument concerns the supplied `finite-information-rational-v1` model,
not its causal adequacy or a field outcome. Its premises are executable in the
independent original-byte Python Fraction checker. It is not a Lean theorem,
S8 admission, probabilistic-law qualification or a universal capacity claim.

Let two original information states be related exactly when the accepted
`stateToBlock` map assigns them the same block. The checker accounts for every
declared state once, including unreachable states, requires nonempty blocks,
checks original roots and reconstructs every original history/observation lift.
It independently parses the original UTF-8 bytes, rejects duplicate JSON members
and floating-point source values, and validates the complete finite table.

At terminal period T, related states have equal normalized terminal labels,
per-world accrued values, terminal liabilities, resource/constraint state,
staffing/compensation/benefit constraints, obligations and information rights.
Therefore they have the same supplied terminal value and the same feasibility
disposition. UNKNOWN/PARTIAL liabilities are retained and prevent a justified
optimization claim; equality does not turn them into known zero liabilities.

Assume continuation preservation holds at period t+1. Related states at t
have the same lawful observations, support, semantic state and enabled original
typed actions in the same catalogue order. The accepted relation checks every
enabled action and its immediate cash/resource/human/obligation labels. The
original validator independently checks timing, appended action history,
observation rights/delivery, pending information, stopped state, accrued value,
terminal debt, resource use and full-horizon occupancy propagation.

For fixed complete worlds, each action preserves that same world identity and
has one complete successor for each supported world. Successors lie in equal
blocks with equal observation outcomes. A fresh adversary is never selected at
the next period, and no averaging of the world payoff vector is used to decide
equivalence. Applying the induction hypothesis to those successors preserves
each complete-world continuation payoff and every supplied hard constraint.
Consequently the complete attainable lawful continuation family, its robust
root value, and every action/continuation participating in an optimal original
root policy are preserved. Distinct globally tied continuations are retained
even when one has a smaller local worst value.

The probability branch checks exact nonnegative complete root/transition mass
and equal action, observation, reward, resource, world-labelled mass into each
block. The corresponding finite expectation argument requires a current
qualified owner law. The implementation performs structural mass checking but
does not have such a law receipt; current S4 probability optimization is
explicitly UNSUPPORTED. Structural acceptance alone confers no probabilistic
decision claim.

Backward induction to the declared original root establishes the fixed-world
claim within the complete supplied finite family. The actual S4 evaluator uses
identical exact arithmetic, source bytes, catalogue order, constraints and
family/stopping limits with R1 off or on. Only its continuation memo key changes
to an independently accepted class. Original histories and observation keys are
reconstructed outside that reusable evaluation path. The original tie rule is
the first globally winning family in owner catalogue order. The independent
small-case reference enumerates complete original and quotient policy families
and compares exact values, world vectors, feasibility and all observable ties.

Bounds can prevent completion. Byte, rational-digit, operation, family, clock or
original grant refusal leaves the affected claim INCOMPLETE/UNKNOWN/UNSUPPORTED;
it cannot establish complete search or infeasibility. Acceptance of an entire
relation and completion of policy search remain separate. A failed checker
never enables quotient reuse: the compatible original S4 child uses the same
remaining original episode/grant/response deadline, or its precise unavailable
disposition is retained. No claim here expands that fallback's authority.

Executable premise owners: `checker.py:validate_original/check`,
`exact-continuation.ts:evaluateExactContingentControl`, the original policy
projection and exact S5 allocation adapter. Evidence owners are the registered
mathematical reference, authenticated owner and lifecycle proofs, compiled
runtime, and mounted Work proof. Their exact source digests/results are recorded
in the final evidence index; a proof from earlier bytes is not silently inherited
after a relevant change.
