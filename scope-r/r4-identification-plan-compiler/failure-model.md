# Pre-code failure model and independent verifier specification

This specification precedes isolated mathematical producer/checker code.
One primary scientific E2E owns the mathematical contract; HTTP/SQL/worker and
mounted Work scenarios separately own transport, responsibility and privacy.
Expected functionals are independently specified, not copied from y0 output.
No test-only production callbacks, fake receipts or post-code unit tests.

## Material failures

* Input omission, duplicate variables/bundles/graphs/cost identities, cyclic
  directed edges, self-confounding, unknown nodes, invalid disjoint query sets,
  unsupported variable/unit/intervention/population/time mappings.
* Calling plain ID/IDC on an experimental, selected, error-contaminated,
  counterfactual, cyclic or unqualified projected graph/data problem.
* Inventing a joint law from incompatible or merely overlapping marginals;
  dropping a confounder; losing row linkage, rights, measurement, source or
  time/population/support identity. Observed counts do not prove positivity.
* Trusting y0 expression/None/exception without checking each rule and binding
  to original bytes. Invalid ancestor/district/decomposition/mutilation
  witnesses, missing factors, variable capture or wrong IDC denominator.
* Claiming a structural negative from timeout, unsupported rule or missing data.
* Missing the empty mask, cheaper/tie-preferred mask or retained graph;
  sampling masks; false cost totals, free delays, double-counted shared items,
  hidden joint exposure/resource violation or unknown price treated as zero.
* Equating graph-relative identification with a common effect. Alpha capture,
  syntax hashes and sampled numerical agreement are not equivalence proofs.
* Prospective data promoted before source/support readback; formula rendered as
  effect size, economic credit, verified field truth or effect authority.
* Owner/schema/auth refusal mistaken for reached scientific integration.
  Caller-supplied owner receipts, quantities, grants or empty obligations trusted.
* Cross-principal/tenant reads, stale publication, missing dependency closure,
  stale source/checker/runtime after restart, delayed UI resurrection.
* Fresh grants/deadlines per mask, double compute reservation, child release
  before termination, crash/duplicate blindly retried, unknown costs/effects
  erased. Existing S6 responsibility cannot be reset by cancellation.
* Builder-only Python, mutable dependency pins, incompatible scientific locks,
  stale migration head, job compatibility refusal or source-custody bypass.

## Rule verifier acceptance

The verifier runs separately and imports neither y0 nor producer predicates,
trace acceptance, menu search or expression builders. It reads original input
and candidate traces. It independently checks graph DAG identity, directed
ancestors, bidirected connected components, induced ancestral transformations,
and separation by explicit latent-parent expansion and ancestral moralization.
Producer witnesses are compared with independently derived properties.

For ID(y,x,Q,G), with V the current nodes:

1. x empty: result is sum(V minus y) Q.
2. A = directed ancestors including y, A proper subset V: recurse on G[A],
   y, x intersect A and sum(V minus A) Q.
3. W = (V minus x) minus ancestors(y) in G[V minus x], W nonempty:
   recurse with x union W, unchanged Q/G.
4. G[V minus x] has multiple districts S: recurse ID(S,V minus S,Q,G)
   for every district, multiply and sum(V minus (x union y)).
5. Both current G and G[V minus x] are single districts: require an explicit
   nested, equally rooted bidirected-connected C-forest hedge, one intersecting
   x and the other disjoint x, roots ancestral to y in the mutilated graph.
   Check all forest edges/outdegrees/root/containment predicates. No exception
   alone is a failure proof.
6. Single district S of G[V minus x] is a district of G: use the current
   kernel's ordered chain factors, sum(S minus y). Each factor is derived as
   a ratio of consecutive marginals of Q, not an invented global P symbol.
7. S is strictly inside one district S' of G: derive Q[S'] from the same
   current kernel ordered factors, recurse on G[S'], x intersect S', y.

Trace root, each child query/kernel/graph, ordering, all witnesses, returned
expression/failure and backend identity must match. Unsupported/missing steps
leave an unaccepted claim. The pinned y0 release is producer machinery; any
release-specific mismatch must be diagnosed, not silently accepted.

IDC: every observation/action exchange requires independent separation in
G with incoming treatment and outgoing exchanged-observation edges removed,
including the applicable bidirected incoming intervention removal. If no
exchange applies, verify ID(y union z,x,Q,G), then divide by its marginal over
y. A nonzero denominator/support premise is required on the query domain.

## Data, equivalence and minimality

Data checking resolves one permitted compatible observational joint law with
all required coordinates, or its explicitly witnessed marginals. Source,
population, intervention, time, unit, selection/missingness, measurement and
positivity premises are checked separately. UNKNOWN stays unresolved;
CONTRADICTED rejects. Prospective premises remain conditional. No graph truth
is established by this predicate.

The declared sound equivalence path uses lexical bound-variable normalization,
associative/commutative products, justified cancellation under nonzero support,
probability chain-factor identities, marginalization and conditional-kernel
normalization. It compares normalized typed terms and records the applied
rules, original law identity and support premises. Unsupported algebra returns
NOT_PROVED, not unequal. Independent exact SCM/formula evaluations on tractable
synthetic references are additional diagnostics, not universal proofs.

The minimality verifier independently enumerates every menu subset and graph,
recomputes cost-item union, exact schedules/resource/exposure admission,
data/support disposition and rank. Every candidate result references an accepted
proof for its exact graph/query/law/method key. Missing pair/mask or potentially
cheaper unresolved competitor prevents complete minimality. Family and per-graph
claims remain distinct.

## Fault and retention contract

Acceptance requires current real S1/S2/S3/S4/S5/S7 and Work bindings at submit,
claim, checker acceptance, publication and consumption. Durable SQL fences,
attempts, process IDs, termination, costs and history are inspected under faults.
Ordinary development receipts confer no protected authority.
After two failures of the same predicate, inspect runtime/process/SQL/lease/
fixture/browser/currentness before another run. Preserve original failed runs,
deadlines and durations. Missing material meters prevent affected cost claims.
