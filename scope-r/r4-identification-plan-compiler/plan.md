256 subsets and 2048 pair dispositions. Additional explicit development limits
are 12 nodes/graph, 132 directed/bidirected edges/graph, 32 available laws,
64 cost items, 64 resources, 512 KiB original input, 64 MiB output, 65536 AST
nodes/proof, 4096 trace nodes/proof, recursion depth 96, and 256 decimal digits
per exact numerator/denominator. These are implementation bounds, not invented
PRD limits or measured throughput. Refusals do not truncate inputs.

The supported scientific profile is an owner-declared acyclic directed mixed
graph and observational joint law. A plan can use a single compatible joint
law or an explicitly derived marginal from that law. Unlinked/overlapping
marginals, experimental/transport/selection/measurement-error algorithms and
unqualified latent projection are unsupported. The compiler does not drop
unobserved nodes with an induced subgraph. It can still identify a graph-relative
functional and report that its required data are missing.

owner share, horizon/time and discount basis. Unique registered cost-item IDs
are charged once. Unknown/incomparable costs are unresolved, not free.
Collection scheduling uses the supplied dependency DAG and explicit earliest
start/duration; resource occupancy uses those same intervals. Ranking is exact
cost, critical-path completion, declared resource order, canonical bundle IDs.
No free parallelism is inferred. Family sufficiency requires every retained
graph. Prospective laws/support remain conditions, not observations.

No formula supplies an effect estimate, scientific graph truth, acquisition
authority, reservation, S8 admission, comparative gain or economic credit.
