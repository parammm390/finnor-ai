This records isolation risks before backend code and gates every new E2E case.
The user invoked https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/blob/main/.agents/skills/test-audit/SKILL.md.
Apply its observable-contract, credible-regression, coverage-owner and production-
seam questions. OpenClaw repository-specific runners/landing workflows are not
FINNOR tools. This is authoring mode, not a broad deletion campaign.

All statistical computations may fail for bad conditioning, rank deficiency,
misspecified lag/model form, heterogeneous/heteroskedastic errors, insufficient
independent information, nonstationarity, unstable recursion, unsupported
histories, degenerate bootstrap resamples or floating overflow. Preserve those
failures; do not replace missing samples/parameters/costs with zero, clip
unsupported outcomes into confident forecasts or silently fall back to a weaker
identified estimator. Source/mechanism ambiguity, prior choices (none in this
method), bootstrap approximation and measurement/transport/identification limits
stay separate from stochastic variation.

The numerical coverage campaign is a necessary isolated check of sampling
uncertainty across independent generated datasets: the single integrated fit
cannot establish repeated-fit parameter coverage. Its production boundary is the
same version-pinned stdio adapter used by the PE handler; no test-only export or
injection hook. Credible regressions are independent outcome bootstrap, wrong
block indices or falsely narrow dynamic-effect intervals. The independently
implemented generator/true recurrence protects those; results remain numerical
H1, without S1 authenticity, field identification or method admission. Register
40 independent generated datasets before this campaign, 128 block draws, original
0.82–0.98 observed 90% coverage window and <=0.10 fixed-range mean error. Cluster
uncertainty by dataset, never count horizons/firms as independent experiments.

Source claims, declared assumptions, fitted diagnostics, simulations and prepared
ledger events have no protected admission receipt. A matching digest detects
mutation relative to its supplied commitment; it does not authenticate a
producer or prove scientific truth. Current-use checks resolve the source
bindings again. Sealed evaluation manifests/release authorities are absent;
ordinary local reports are not independent release certificates.
